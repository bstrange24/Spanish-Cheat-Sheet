Body tips
  • Spanish uses the definite article, not "my," with body parts: Me duele la cabeza meh DWEH-leh lah kah-BEH-sah, not mi cabeza.
  • doler doh-LEHR works like gustar goos-TAHR: me duele meh DWEH-leh + singular; me duelen meh DWEH-lehn + plural.
  • la mano lah MAH-noh is feminine even though it ends in -o: las manos lahs MAH-nohs.
  • el pie ehl pyeh → plural los pies lohs pyehs (the -e stays, add -s).
  • Gender of "sick": enfermo ehn-FEHR-moh / enferma ehn-FEHR-mah. Same for alérgico ah-LEHR-hee-koh / alérgica ah-LEHR-hee-kah.
  • For "I broke my arm," Spanish often uses Me lastimé el brazo meh lahs-tee-MEH ehl BRAH-soh or Me rompí el brazo meh rohm-PEE ehl BRAH-soh.
