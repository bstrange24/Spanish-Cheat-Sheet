Food tips
  • Tengo hambre TEHN-goh AHM-breh / Tengo sed TEHN-goh sehd use tener teh-NEHR, not estar.
  • el agua ehl AH-gwah is feminine but takes el in the singular; still agua fría AH-gwah FREE-ah, las aguas lahs AH-gwahs.
  • Polite orders: Quisiera kee-SYEH-rah… or Me gustaría meh goos-tah-REE-ah… + por favor por fah-BOHR.
  • Ask for the bill with La cuenta, por favor lah KWEHN-tah por fah-BOHR. Tipping customs vary by country.
  • la comida lah koh-MEE-dah can mean food in general, or the midday meal in much of Spain and parts of LatAm.
  • Regional pairs: jugo HOO-goh / zumo SOO-moh; papa PAH-pah / patata pah-TAH-tah; mesero meh-SEH-roh / camarero kah-mah-REH-roh.
