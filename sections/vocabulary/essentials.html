Essential Spanish Sayings & Filler Words

Everyday expressions, connectors, and conversational glue

Common Fillers & Connectors

These words don't always translate directly, but they are essential for natural, fluent conversation.

Saying Pronunciation Meaning / Use
pues pwehs Well / so / then — filler, used to pause or transition
bueno BWEH-noh Well / okay / so — filler to start a sentence or show agreement
o sea oh SEH-ah I mean / in other words / that is — used to clarify or rephrase
entonces ehn-TOHN-sehs So / then / therefore — logical connector
es decir ehs deh-SEER That is / I mean — used to specify or explain
en realidad ehn reh-ah-lee-DAHD Actually / in reality — to correct or add nuance
la verdad lah behr-DAHD To be honest / the truth is — softens a statement
a ver ah behr Let's see / let me see — used to think or to ask for attention
claro KLAH-roh Of course / clearly — agreement or emphasis
vale BAH-leh Okay / got it (very common in Spain)
listo LEES-toh Ready / okay (used in Latin America for "okay")
dale DAH-leh Go ahead / okay / let's do it (common in Argentina, Uruguay, Caribbean)
ajá ah-HAH Uh-huh / yeah — acknowledgment while listening
Example sentences:
  • Pues, no sé. pwehs noh seh
    Well, I don't know.
  • Bueno, vamos. BWEH-noh BAH-mohs
    Okay, let's go.
  • O sea, no me gusta. oh SEH-ah noh meh GOOS-tah
    I mean, I don't like it.
  • Entonces, ¿qué hacemos? ehn-TOHN-sehs keh ah-SEH-mohs
    So, what do we do?
  • Es decir, no podemos. ehs deh-SEER noh poh-DEH-mohs
    That is, we can't.
  • En realidad, no es así. ehn reh-ah-lee-DAHD noh ehs ah-SEE
    Actually, it's not like that.
  • La verdad, no sé. lah behr-DAHD noh seh
    To be honest, I don't know.
  • A ver, explícame. ah behr ehks-PLEE-kah-meh
    Let's see, explain to me.
  • Claro que sí. KLAH-roh keh see
    Of course yes.
  • Vale, de acuerdo. BAH-leh deh ah-KWEHR-doh
    Okay, agreed.
  • Listo, ya entendí. LEES-toh yah ehn-tehn-DEE
    Okay, I got it now.
  • Dale, vamos. DAH-leh BAH-mohs
    Okay, let's go.
  • Ajá, sigo. ah-HAH SEE-goh
    Uh-huh, I'm following.

Opinion & Attitude Expressions

Phrases to express personal views, uncertainty, or to soften what you say.

Saying Pronunciation Meaning / Use
creo que KREH-oh keh I think that / I believe that
no creo que noh KREH-oh keh I don't think that (often triggers subjunctive)
me parece meh pah-REH-seh It seems to me / I think
pienso que PYEHN-soh keh I think that (more deliberate than creo que)
tal vez tahl behs Maybe / perhaps (often with subjunctive)
quizás kee-SAHS Perhaps / maybe (synonym of tal vez)
a lo mejor ah loh meh-HOR Maybe / perhaps (more colloquial, does not trigger subjunctive)
seguro que seh-GOO-roh keh Surely / for sure
es que ehs keh It's just that / the thing is — to justify or explain
lo que pasa es que loh keh PAH-sah ehs keh The thing is that / what happens is that
en mi opinión ehn mee oh-pee-NYOHN In my opinion
Example sentences:
  • Creo que sí. KREH-oh keh see
    I think so.
  • No creo que venga. noh KREH-oh keh BEHN-gah
    I don't think he's coming.
  • Me parece bien. meh pah-REH-seh BYEHN
    That seems good to me.
  • Pienso que es importante. PYEHN-soh keh ehs eem-por-TAHN-teh
    I think it's important.
  • Tal vez venga mañana. tahl behs BEHN-gah mah-NYAH-nah
    Maybe he'll come tomorrow.
  • Quizás tengas razón. kee-SAHS TEHN-gahs rah-SOHN
    Perhaps you're right.
  • A lo mejor viene. ah loh meh-HOR BYEH-neh
    Maybe he'll come.
  • Seguro que sí. seh-GOO-roh keh see
    For sure yes.
  • Es que no tengo tiempo. ehs keh noh TEHN-goh TYEHM-poh
    It's just that I don't have time.
  • Lo que pasa es que llegué tarde. loh keh PAH-sah ehs keh yeh-GEH TAR-deh
    The thing is I arrived late.
  • En mi opinión, es mejor. ehn mee oh-pee-NYOHN ehs meh-HOR
    In my opinion, it's better.
Agreeing, Disagreeing & Reacting

Quick ways to agree, disagree, or show surprise.

Saying Pronunciation Meaning / Use
de acuerdo deh ah-KWEHR-doh Agreed / okay
por supuesto por soo-PWEHS-toh Of course
ni hablar nee ah-BLAHR No way / out of the question
qué va keh bah No way / not at all (used to disagree gently)
claro que sí KLAH-roh keh SEE Of course yes
claro que no KLAH-roh keh NOH Of course not
no importa noh eem-POHR-tah It doesn't matter
no pasa nada noh PAH-sah NAH-dah No problem / it's nothing
qué bien keh BYEHN How nice! / Great!
qué mal keh MAHL How awful! / That's too bad
qué lástima keh LAHS-tee-mah What a pity / shame
qué suerte keh SWEHR-teh How lucky!
en serio ehn SEH-ryoh Really? / Seriously — surprise or disbelief
no me digas noh meh DEE-gahs You don't say! / No way! (surprise)
Example sentences:
  • Estoy de acuerdo. ehs-TOY deh ah-KWEHR-doh
    I agree.
  • Por supuesto que sí. por soo-PWEHS-toh keh see
    Of course yes.
  • ¿Ir yo? ¡Ni hablar! eer yoh nee ah-BLAHR
    Me go? No way!
  • ¿Estás enojado? — ¡Qué va! ehs-TAHS eh-noh-HAH-doh keh bah
    Are you angry? — Not at all!
  • Claro que sí, te ayudo. KLAH-roh keh see teh ah-YOO-doh
    Of course yes, I'll help you.
  • Claro que no me olvidé. KLAH-roh keh noh meh ohl-bee-DEH
    Of course I didn't forget.
  • No importa, lo hacemos después. noh eem-POHR-tah loh ah-SEH-mohs dehs-PWEHS
    It doesn't matter, we'll do it later.
  • No pasa nada, tranquilo. noh PAH-sah NAH-dah trahn-KEE-loh
    No problem, don't worry.
  • ¿Aprobaste? ¡Qué bien! ah-proh-BAHS-teh keh BYEHN
    You passed? Great!
  • Perdimos. — ¡Qué mal! pehr-DEE-mohs keh mahl
    We lost. — That's too bad.
  • No pudo venir. — Qué lástima. noh POO-doh beh-NEER keh LAHS-tee-mah
    He couldn't come. — What a pity.
  • Gané el sorteo. — ¡Qué suerte! gah-NEH ehl sohr-TEH-oh keh SWEHR-teh
    I won the raffle. — How lucky!
  • ¿En serio? No sabía. ehn SEH-ryoh noh sah-BEE-ah
    Really? I didn't know.
  • Se casó. — ¡No me digas! seh kah-SOH noh meh DEE-gahs
    He got married. — No way!
Time & Sequence Expressions

Words to organize your ideas and tell stories.

Saying Pronunciation Meaning / Use
primero pree-MEH-roh First / firstly
luego LWEH-goh Then / later
después dehs-PWEHS After / afterwards
mientras MYEHN-trahs While / meanwhile
entonces ehn-TOHN-sehs So / then (also used for sequence)
al final ahl fee-NAHL In the end / finally
por fin por FEEN Finally / at last (with relief)
a veces ah BEH-sehs Sometimes
de vez en cuando deh behs ehn KWAHN-doh Once in a while
más tarde mahs TAHR-deh Later / later on
Example sentences:
  • Primero, vamos al banco. pree-MEH-roh BAH-mohs ahl BAHN-koh
    First, let's go to the bank.
  • Luego, fuimos al cine. LWEH-goh FWEE-mohs ahl SEE-neh
    Then, we went to the movies.
  • Después de comer, salimos. dehs-PWEHS deh koh-MEHR sah-LEE-mohs
    After eating, we went out.
  • Mientras tú trabajas, yo descanso. MYEHN-trahs too trah-BAH-hahs yoh dehs-KAHN-soh
    While you work, I rest.
  • Entonces, ¿qué pasó? ehn-TOHN-sehs keh pah-SOH
    So, what happened?
  • Al final, decidimos quedarnos. ahl fee-NAHL deh-see-DEE-mohs keh-DAHR-nohs
    In the end, we decided to stay.
  • ¡Por fin llegaste! por feen yeh-GAHS-teh
    You finally arrived!
  • A veces voy al gimnasio. ah BEH-sehs boy ahl heem-NAH-syoh
    Sometimes I go to the gym.
  • De vez en cuando salimos a cenar. deh behs ehn KWAHN-doh sah-LEE-mohs ah seh-NAHR
    Once in a while we go out for dinner.
  • Te llamo más tarde. teh YAH-moh mahs TAR-deh
    I'll call you later.
Quick Comparison — Similar Sayings

Understanding the subtle differences between commonly confused expressions.

Saying Pronunciation Meaning When to use
creo que KREH-oh keh I think / I believe Everyday opinion, less certain
pienso que PYEHN-soh keh I think More deliberate, thought-out opinion
me parece que meh pah-REH-seh keh It seems to me Based on perception, less certain
tal vez tahl behs Maybe Formal, often triggers subjunctive
quizás kee-SAHS Maybe Similar to tal vez, also triggers subjunctive
a lo mejor ah loh meh-HOR Maybe More informal, does NOT trigger subjunctive
por supuesto por soo-PWEHS-toh Of course Emphatic agreement
claro que sí KLAH-roh keh SEE Of course yes Affirmative, obvious agreement
no importa noh eem-POHR-tah It doesn't matter Dismissive, it's not important
no pasa nada noh PAH-sah NAH-dah No problem Reassuring, "it's all good"
Example sentences:
  • Creo que sí. KREH-oh keh see

  • Pienso que deberíamos esperar. PYEHN-soh keh deh-beh-REE-ah-mohs ehs-peh-RAHR
    I think we should wait.
  • Me parece que va a llover. meh pah-REH-seh keh bah ah yoh-BEHR
    It seems to me it's going to rain.
  • Tal vez venga. tahl behs BEHN-gah
    Maybe he'll come.
  • Quizás tenga razón. kee-SAHS TEHN-gah rah-SOHN
    Maybe he's right.
  • A lo mejor viene. ah loh meh-HOR BYEH-neh

  • Claro que sí, te ayudo. KLAH-roh keh see teh ah-YOO-doh
