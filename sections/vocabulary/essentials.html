Essential Spanish Sayings & Filler Words

Everyday expressions, connectors, and conversational glue

Common Fillers & Connectors

These words don't always translate directly, but they are essential for natural, fluent conversation.

Saying Pronunciation Meaning / Use Example English
pues PWEHS pwehs Well / so / then — filler, used to pause or transition Pues, no sé. PWEHS noh seh 🔊 Well, I don't know.
bueno BWEH-noh BWEH-noh Well / okay / so — filler to start a sentence or show agreement Bueno, vamos. BWEH-noh BAH-mohs 🔊 Okay, let's go.
o sea oh SEH-ah oh SEH-ah I mean / in other words / that is — used to clarify or rephrase O sea, no me gusta. oh SEH-ah noh meh GOOS-tah 🔊 I mean, I don't like it.
entonces ehn-TOHN-sehs ehn-TOHN-sehs So / then / therefore — logical connector Entonces, ¿qué hacemos? ehn-TOHN-sehs keh ah-SEH-mohs 🔊 So, what do we do?
es decir ehs deh-SEER ehs deh-SEER That is / I mean — used to specify or explain Es decir, no podemos. ehs deh-SEER noh poh-DEH-mohs 🔊 That is, we can't.
en realidad ehn reh-ah-lee-DAHD ehn reh-ah-lee-DAHD Actually / in reality — to correct or add nuance En realidad, no es así. ehn reh-ah-lee-DAHD noh ehs ah-SEE 🔊 Actually, it's not like that.
la verdad lah behr-DAHD lah behr-DAHD To be honest / the truth is — softens a statement La verdad, no sé. lah behr-DAHD noh seh 🔊 To be honest, I don't know.
a ver ah behr ah behr Let's see / let me see — used to think or to ask for attention A ver, explícame. ah behr ehks-PLEE-kah-meh 🔊 Let's see, explain to me.
claro KLAH-roh KLAH-roh Of course / clearly — agreement or emphasis Claro que sí. KLAH-roh keh see 🔊 Of course yes.
vale BAH-leh BAH-leh Okay / got it (very common in Spain) Vale, de acuerdo. BAH-leh deh ah-KWEHR-doh 🔊 Okay, agreed.
listo LEES-toh LEES-toh Ready / okay (used in Latin America for "okay") Listo, ya entendí. LEES-toh yah ehn-tehn-DEE 🔊 Okay, I got it now.
dale DAH-leh DAH-leh Go ahead / okay / let's do it (common in Argentina, Uruguay, Caribbean) Dale, vamos. DAH-leh BAH-mohs 🔊 Okay, let's go.
ajá ah-HAH ah-HAH Uh-huh / yeah — acknowledgment while listening Ajá, sigo. ah-HAH SEE-goh 🔊 Uh-huh, I'm following.
Opinion & Attitude Expressions

Phrases to express personal views, uncertainty, or to soften what you say.

Saying Pronunciation Meaning / Use Example English
creo que KREH-oh keh KREH-oh keh I think that / I believe that Creo que sí. KREH-oh keh see 🔊 I think so.
no creo que noh KREH-oh keh noh KREH-oh keh I don't think that (often triggers subjunctive) No creo que venga. noh KREH-oh keh BEHN-gah 🔊 I don't think he's coming.
me parece meh pah-REH-seh meh pah-REH-seh It seems to me / I think Me parece bien. meh pah-REH-seh BYEHN 🔊 That seems good to me.
pienso que PYEHN-soh keh PYEHN-soh keh I think that (more deliberate than creo que) Pienso que es importante. PYEHN-soh keh ehs eem-por-TAHN-teh 🔊 I think it's important.
tal vez tahl behs tahl behs Maybe / perhaps (often with subjunctive) Tal vez venga mañana. tahl behs BEHN-gah mah-NYAH-nah 🔊 Maybe he'll come tomorrow.
quizás kee-SAHS kee-SAHS Perhaps / maybe (synonym of tal vez) Quizás tengas razón. kee-SAHS TEHN-gahs rah-SOHN 🔊 Perhaps you're right.
a lo mejor ah loh meh-HOR ah loh meh-HOR Maybe / perhaps (more colloquial, does NOT trigger subjunctive) A lo mejor viene. ah loh meh-HOR BYEH-neh 🔊 Maybe he'll come.
seguro que seh-GOO-roh keh seh-GOO-roh keh Surely / for sure Seguro que sí. seh-GOO-roh keh see 🔊 For sure yes.
es que ehs keh ehs keh It's just that / the thing is — to justify or explain Es que no tengo tiempo. ehs keh noh TEHN-goh TYEHM-poh 🔊 It's just that I don't have time.
lo que pasa es que loh keh PAH-sah ehs keh loh keh PAH-sah ehs keh The thing is that / what happens is that Lo que pasa es que llegué tarde. loh keh PAH-sah ehs keh yeh-HEH TAR-deh 🔊 The thing is I arrived late.
en mi opinión ehn mee oh-pee-NYOHN ehn mee oh-pee-NYOHN In my opinion En mi opinión, es mejor. ehn mee oh-pee-NYOHN ehs meh-HOR 🔊 In my opinion, it's better.
Agreeing, Disagreeing & Reacting

Quick ways to agree, disagree, or show surprise.

Saying Pronunciation Meaning / Use Example English

qué suerte keh SWEHR-teh keh SWEHR-teh How lucky! Gané el sorteo. — ¡Qué suerte! gah-NEH ehl sohr-TEH-oh keh SWEHR-teh 🔊 I won the raffle. — How lucky!
en serio ehn SEH-ryoh ehn SEH-ryoh Really? / Seriously — surprise or disbelief ¿En serio? No sabía. ehn SEH-ryoh noh sah-BEE-ah 🔊 Really? I didn't know.
no me digas noh meh DEE-gahs noh meh DEE-gahs You don't say! / No way! (surprise) Se casó. — ¡No me digas! seh kah-SOH noh meh DEE-gahs 🔊 He got married. — No way!
