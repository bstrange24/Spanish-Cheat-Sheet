Sentirse
Spanish Pronunciation English
Me siento bien. meh SYEHN-toh byehn I feel good.
Me siento mal. meh SYEHN-toh mahl I feel bad / sick.
¿Cómo te sientes? KOH-moh teh SYEHN-tehs How do you feel?
Example sentences:
  • Me siento bien. meh SYEHN-toh byehn
    I feel good.
  • Me siento mal. meh SYEHN-toh mahl
    I feel bad.
  • ¿Cómo te sientes? KOH-moh teh SYEHN-tehs
    How do you feel?
  • Me siento triste. meh SYEHN-toh TREES-teh
    I feel sad.
  • Me siento feliz. meh SYEHN-toh feh-LEES
    I feel happy.
  • ¿Cómo se siente usted? KOH-moh seh SYEHN-teh oos-TEHD
    How do you feel? (formal)
Emotion tips
  • Temporary feelings use estar, not ser: Está triste ehs-TAH TREES-teh (she's sad now).
  • Ser feliz sehr feh-LEES is more "to be a happy person" in general.
  • Match gender: cansado kahn-SAH-doh / cansada kahn-SAH-dah.
