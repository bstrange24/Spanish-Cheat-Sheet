Emotion tips
  • Temporary feelings use estar ehs-TAHR, not ser sehr: Está triste ehs-TAH TREES-teh (she’s sad now).
  • Ser feliz sehr feh-LEES is more “to be a happy person” in general.
  • Match gender: cansado kahn-SAH-doh / cansada kahn-SAH-dah.
