Work & school tips
  • Jobs usually drop the article after ser sehr: Soy médico soy MEH-dee-koh, not soy un médico (unless you mean “just some doctor”).
  • Many job titles change for gender: el jefe ehl HEH-feh / la jefa lah HEH-fah. Some stay the same: el / la periodista ehl lah peh-ryoh-DEES-tah.
  • estudiar ehs-too-DYAHR = to study a subject; aprender ah-prehn-DEHR = to learn (a skill or fact).
  • Ask someone’s job with ¿A qué te dedicas? ah keh teh deh-DEE-kahs or ¿En qué trabajas? ehn kay trah-BAH-hahs
  • el aula ehl OW-lah is feminine but takes el because it starts with stressed a-: still un aula grande oon OW-lah GRAHN-deh, las aulas lahs OW-lahs.
  • Computer: computadora kohm-poo-tah-DOH-rah (LatAm) vs. ordenador ohr-deh-nah-DOHR (Spain).
