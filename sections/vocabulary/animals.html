Animals tips
  • Many animals have masc/fem pairs: el perro / la perra, el gato / la gata. Mixed groups use the masculine plural.
  • el pez = a live fish; el pescado = fish as food. Plural of pez is los peces.
  • el águila is feminine but takes el in the singular: still un águila grande, las águilas.
  • el pollo often means chicken (meat or young bird); la gallina is the hen.
  • Common sounds: ladrar (bark), maullar (meow), relinchar (neigh), mugir (moo).
  • To say you have a pet: Tengo un perro or Tengo una mascota.
