Animals tips
  • Many animals have masc/fem pairs: el perro ehl PEH-rroh / la perra lah PEH-rrah, el gato ehl GAH-toh / la gata lah GAH-tah. Mixed groups use the masculine plural.
  • el pez ehl pehs = a live fish; el pescado ehl pehs-KAH-doh = fish as food. Plural of pez is los peces lohs PEH-sehs.
  • el águila ehl AH-ghee-lah is feminine but takes el in the singular: still un águila grande oon AH-hee-lah GRAHN-deh, las águilas lahs AH-hee-lahs.
  • el pollo ehl POH-yoh often means chicken (meat or young bird); la gallina lah gah-YEE-nah is the hen.
  • Common sounds: ladrar lah-DRAHR (bark), maullar mow-YAHR (meow), relinchar reh-leen-CHAHR (neigh), mugir moo-HEER (moo).
  • To say you have a pet: Tengo un perro TEHN-goh oon PEH-rroh or Tengo una mascota TEHN-goh OO-nah mahs-KOH-tah.
