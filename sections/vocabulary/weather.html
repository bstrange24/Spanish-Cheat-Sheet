Weather tips
  • Use hace for general conditions: cold, hot, sun, wind. Use está + -ando/-iendo for what's happening now: está lloviendo.
  • Person vs. weather: Hace frío = the weather is cold; Tengo frío = I am cold.
  • Intensify with mucho: hace mucho calor (not muy calor).
  • Simple present also works: llueve, nieva.
  • Temperatures are usually Celsius: Hace treinta grados.
  • Many tropical countries talk about rainy vs. dry season more than four seasons.
