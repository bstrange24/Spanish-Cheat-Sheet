What's the weather like?

el clima / el tiempo ehl KLEE-mah / ehl TYEHM-poh the weather / climate
¿Qué tiempo hace? kay TYEHM-poh AH-seh (LatAm) / AH-theh (Spain) What's the weather like?
¿Cómo está el clima? KOH-moh ehs-TAH ehl KLEE-mah How's the weather? (esp. LatAm)
el pronóstico ehl proh-NOHS-tee-koh the forecast
la temperatura lah tehm-peh-rah-TOO-rah the temperature
el grado ehl GRAH-doh the degree
Example sentences:
  • ¿Qué tiempo hace hoy? kay TYEHM-poh AH-seh oy
    What's the weather like today?
  • ¿Cómo está el clima? KOH-moh ehs-TAH ehl KLEE-mah
    How's the weather?
  • El pronóstico dice que va a llover. ehl proh-NOHS-tee-koh DEE-seh keh bah ah yoh-BEHR
    The forecast says it's going to rain.
  • La temperatura es de veinte grados. lah tehm-peh-rah-TOO-rah ehs deh BAYN-teh GRAH-dohs
    The temperature is twenty degrees.
  • Hace veinticinco grados. AH-seh bayn-tee-SEEN-koh GRAH-dohs
    It's 25 degrees.
  • El clima aquí es agradable. ehl KLEE-mah ah-KEE ehs ah-grah-DAH-bleh
    The climate here is pleasant.

Weather tips
  • Use hace AH-seh for general conditions: cold, hot, sun, wind. Use está ehs-TAH + -ando/-iendo for what's happening now: está lloviendo ehs-TAH yoh-BYEHN-doh.
  • Person vs. weather: Hace frío AH-seh FREE-oh = the weather is cold; Tengo frío TEHN-goh FREE-oh = I am cold.
  • Intensify with mucho MOO-choh: hace mucho calor AH-seh MOO-choh kah-LOHR (not muy calor).
  • Simple present also works: llueve YWEH-beh, nieva NYEH-bah.
  • Temperatures are usually Celsius: Hace treinta grados AH-seh TRAYN-tah GRAH-dohs.
  • Many tropical countries talk about rainy vs. dry season more than four seasons.
