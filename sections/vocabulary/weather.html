Weather tips
  • Use hace AH-seh for general conditions: cold, hot, sun, wind. Use está ehs-TAH + -ando/-iendo for what's happening now: está lloviendo ehs-TAH yoh-BYEHN-doh.
  • Person vs. weather: Hace frío AH-seh FREE-oh = the weather is cold; Tengo frío TEHN-goh FREE-oh = I am cold.
  • Intensify with mucho MOO-choh: hace mucho calor AH-seh MOO-choh kah-LOHR (not muy calor).
  • Simple present also works: llueve YWEH-beh, nieva NYEH-bah.
  • Temperatures are usually Celsius: Hace treinta grados AH-seh TRAYN-tah GRAH-dohs.
  • Many tropical countries talk about rainy vs. dry season more than four seasons.
