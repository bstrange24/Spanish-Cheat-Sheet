Family tips
  • Masculine plural often covers mixed groups: los padres lohs PAH-drehs = parents; los hijos lohs EE-hohs = children; los hermanos lohs ehr-MAH-nohs = siblings.
  • Possessives match the thing owned, not the owner: mi hermana mee ehr-MAH-nah, mis hermanos mees ehr-MAH-nohs.
  • mayor mah-YOHR / menor meh-NOHR = older / younger: mi hermano mayor mee ehr-MAH-noh mah-YOHR.
  • la mujer lah moo-HEHR can mean “the woman” or “the wife” — context (or esposa ehs-POH-sah) makes it clear.
  • To say someone is a relative: Es mi tío ehs mee TEE-oh. Use tener teh-NEHR for “I have…” family members.
  • Diminutives are common and affectionate: abuelita ah-bweh-LEE-tah, mamita mah-MEE-tah, hijito ee-HEE-toh.
