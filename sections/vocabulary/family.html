Family tips
  • Masculine plural often covers mixed groups: los padres = parents; los hijos = children; los hermanos = siblings.
  • Possessives match the thing owned, not the owner: mi hermana, mis hermanos.
  • mayor / menor = older / younger: mi hermano mayor.
  • la mujer can mean “the woman” or “the wife” — context (or esposa) makes it clear.
  • To say someone is a relative: Es mi tío. Use tener for “I have…” family members.
  • Diminutives are common and affectionate: abuelita, mamita, hijito.
