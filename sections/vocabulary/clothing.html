Clothing tips
  • la ropa lah ROH-pah is singular: la ropa está sucia lah ROH-pah ehs-TAH SOO-syah (the clothes are dirty).
  • Many garments are plural: los pantalones lohs pahn-tah-LOH-nehs, los zapatos lohs sah-PAH-tohs, las gafas lahs GAH-fahs.
  • Color adjectives agree: camisa blanca kah-MEE-sah BLAHN-kah, zapatos negros sah-PAH-tohs NEH-grohs. Colors ending in a consonant stay the same in gender: azul ah-SOOL, then add -es in plural: azules ah-SOO-lehs.
  • ponerse poh-NEHR-seh = put on (the action); llevar yeh-BAHR = be wearing. Me pongo el abrigo meh POHN-goh ehl ah-BREE-goh vs. Llevo un abrigo YEH-boh oon ah-BREE-goh.
  • Fit uses quedar keh-DAHR: Me queda bien meh KAY-dah byehn / Me quedan grandes meh KEH-dahn GRAHN-dehs (plural clothes).
  • Clothes size = talla TAH-yah; shoe size = número NOO-meh-roh.
