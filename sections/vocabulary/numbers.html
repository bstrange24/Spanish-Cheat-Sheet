Numbers tips
  • uno OO-noh becomes un oon before a masculine noun: un libro oon LEE-broh, veintiún años bayn-tee-OON AH-nyohs, treinta y un días TRAYN-tah ee oon DEE-ahs.
  • Use cien syehn before a noun (cien personas syehn pehr-SOH-nahs); use ciento SYEHN-toh in compounds (ciento veinte SYEHN-toh BAYN-teh).
  • From 31 up, join with y ee: cuarenta y cinco kwah-REHN-tah ee SEEN-koh. 16–29 are one word.
  • Hundreds agree in gender: doscientos libros dohs-SYEHN-tohs LEE-brohs, doscientas casas dohs-SYEHN-tahs KAH-sahs.
  • Age uses tener teh-NEHR, not ser: Tengo treinta años TEHN-goh TRAYN-tah AH-nyohs (I am 30).
  • primero pree-MEH-roh / tercero tehr-SEH-roh shorten before a masculine singular noun: el primer día ehl pree-MEHR DEE-ah, el tercer piso ehl tehr-SEHR PEE-soh.
  • Phone numbers are usually read digit by digit. doble DOH-bleh = double (e.g. 55 → doble cinco DOH-bleh SEEN-koh).
