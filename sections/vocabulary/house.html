House tips
  • la cocina lah koh-SEE-nah is both “kitchen” and, in some places, “stove.” Context (or estufa ehs-TOO-fah) tells you which.
  • el piso ehl PEE-soh can mean “floor” (level) or “apartment” (Spain). “First floor” = el primer piso ehl pree-MEHR PEE-soh.
  • Mexico often says la recámara lah reh-KAH-mah-rah for bedroom; Spain often el dormitorio ehl dohr-mee-TOH-ryoh or la habitación lah ah-bee-tah-SYOHN.
  • Lights: encender ehn-sehn-DEHR / prender prehn-DEHR = turn on; apagar ah-pah-GAHR = turn off.
  • Invite someone in with Pasa PAH-sah / Pase, por favor PAH-seh por fah-BOHR and Siéntate SYEHN-tah-teh.
  • To rent: alquilar ahl-kee-LAHR / rentar rehn-TAHR — Alquilo un departamento ahl-KEE-loh oon deh-pahr-tah-MEHN-toh.
