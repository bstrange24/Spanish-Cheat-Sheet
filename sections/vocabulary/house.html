Around-the-house phrases
Spanish LatAm Spain (if different) English
Vivo en una casa. 🔊 BEE-boh ehn OO-nah KAH-sah same I live in a house.
Vivo en un apartamento. 🔊 BEE-boh ehn oon ah-pahr-tah-MEHN-toh same I live in an apartment.
Estoy en casa. 🔊 ehs-TOY ehn KAH-sah same I'm at home.
¿Dónde está el baño? 🔊 DOHN-deh ehs-TAH ehl BAH-nyoh same Where is the bathroom?
La cocina está al lado de la sala. 🔊 lah koh-SEE-nah ehs-TAH ahl LAH-doh deh lah SAH-lah …koh-THEE-nah… The kitchen is next to the living room.
Enciende la luz. 🔊 ehn-SYEHN-deh lah loos ehn-THYEHN-deh lah looth Turn on the light.
Apaga la luz. 🔊 ah-PAH-gah lah loos …looth Turn off the light.
Abre la ventana. 🔊 AH-breh lah behn-TAH-nah same Open the window.
Cierra la puerta. 🔊 SYEH-rrah lah PWEHR-tah same Close the door.
Pasa, por favor. 🔊 PAH-sah por fah-BOHR same Come in, please.
Siéntate, por favor. 🔊 SYEHN-tah-teh por fah-BOHR same Sit down, please.
Mi casa es tu casa. 🔊 mee KAH-sah ehs too KAH-sah same Make yourself at home.
House tips
  • la cocina is both “kitchen” and, in some places, “stove.” Context (or estufa) tells you which.
  • el piso can mean “floor” (level) or “apartment” (Spain). “First floor” = el primer piso.
  • Mexico often says la recámara for bedroom; Spain often el dormitorio or la habitación.
  • Lights: encender / prender = turn on; apagar = turn off.
  • Invite someone in with Pasa / Pase, por favor and Siéntate.
  • To rent: alquilar / rentar — Alquilo un departamento.
