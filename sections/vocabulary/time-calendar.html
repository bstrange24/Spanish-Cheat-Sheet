Time & calendar tips
  • Use Es la una ehs lah OO-nah for 1:00; all other hours take Son las sohn lahs + number.
  • Add minutes with y ee after the hour; use menos MEH-nohs to count back from the next hour: las tres menos cuarto lahs trehs MEH-nohs KWAHR-toh = 2:45.
  • Clock times often add de la mañana deh lah mah-NYAH-nah / de la tarde deh lah TAHR-deh / de la noche deh lah NOH-cheh instead of a.m./p.m.
  • Days are not capitalized: lunes LOO-nehs, not Lunes. Same for months.
  • To say “on Monday,” use the article: el lunes ehl LOO-nehs. “On Mondays” (habit) is los lunes lohs LOO-nehs.
  • Dates: el cinco de mayo ehl SEEN-koh deh MAH-yoh (day + de + month). Years are read as a full number: dos mil veintiséis dohs meel bayn-tee-SAYS (2026).
  • mañana mah-NYAH-nah means both “morning” and “tomorrow” — context (or por la mañana por lah mah-NYAH-nah) makes it clear.
