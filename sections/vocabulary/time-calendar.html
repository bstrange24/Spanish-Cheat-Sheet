Time & calendar tips
  • Use Es la una for 1:00; all other hours take Son las + number.
  • Add minutes with y after the hour; use menos to count back from the next hour: las tres menos cuarto = 2:45.
  • Clock times often add de la mañana / de la tarde / de la noche instead of a.m./p.m.
  • Days are not capitalized: lunes, not Lunes. Same for months.
  • To say “on Monday,” use the article: el lunes. “On Mondays” (habit) is los lunes.
  • Dates: el cinco de mayo (day + de + month). Years are read as a full number: dos mil veintiséis (2026).
  • mañana means both “morning” and “tomorrow” — context (or por la mañana) makes it clear.
