Shopping tips
  • ¿Cuánto cuesta? for one item; ¿Cuánto cuestan? for several; ¿Cuánto es? for the total.
  • Bargaining is common in markets, not usually in supermarkets or malls. Stay polite: ¿Me puede hacer un descuento?
  • efectivo = cash; tarjeta = card. Small shops may be cash-only.
  • In Spain, shops often close for a midday break; LatAm hours vary. Ask ¿A qué hora cierran?
  • Currency names change by country: pesos, soles, colones, euros, etc.
  • Decimals: many countries write prices with a comma: 12,50. Point vs. comma varies — watch the local format.
