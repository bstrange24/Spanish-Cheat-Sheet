Shopping tips
  • ¿Cuánto cuesta? KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah for one item; ¿Cuánto cuestan? KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tahn for several; ¿Cuánto es? KWAHN-toh ehs for the total.
  • Bargaining is common in markets, not usually in supermarkets or malls. Stay polite: ¿Me puede hacer un descuento? meh PWEH-deh ah-SEHR oon dehs-KWEHN-toh
  • efectivo eh-fehk-TEE-boh = cash; tarjeta tar-HEH-tah = card. Small shops may be cash-only.
  • In Spain, shops often close for a midday break; LatAm hours vary. Ask ¿A qué hora cierran? ah keh OH-rah SYEH-rrhahn
  • Currency names change by country: pesos PEH-sohs, soles SOH-lehs, colones koh-LOH-nehs, euros EH-OO-rohs, etc.
  • Decimals: many countries write prices with a comma: 12,50. Point vs. comma varies — watch the local format.
