Greetings tips
  • tú too = informal “you”; usted oos-TEHD = formal. Use usted oos-TEHD with strangers, older people, and in shops until invited otherwise.
  • Buenos días BWEH-nohs DEE-ahs until around noon; then buenas tardes BWEH-nahs TAHR-dehs; after dark, buenas noches BWEH-nahs NOH-chehs (hello or goodbye).
  • perdón pehr-DOHN / disculpe dees-KOOL-peh to get attention or pass; lo siento loh SYEHN-toh to apologize for something real.
  • A short, friendly greeting before a question is expected: Hola, buenos días OH-lah BWEH-nohs DEE-ahs… then your request.
  • Answer ¿Cómo estás? KOH-moh ehs-TAHS with a status + gracias GRAH-see-ahs + ¿y tú? ee too.
  • If you get lost in a conversation: Más despacio, por favor mahs dehs-PAH-syoh por fah-BOHR and ¿Puede repetir? PWEH-deh reh-peh-TEER.
