Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Tener is a stem-changing verb (e → ie) in the present tense for all forms except nosotros and vosotros. The yo form is also irregular (tengo).

Singular Plural
tengo TEHN-goh
(yo — I have)		 tenemos teh-NEH-mohs
(nosotros/as — we have)
tienes TYEH-nehs
(tú — you have)		 tenéis teh-NAYS
(vosotros/as — you all have)
tiene TYEH-neh
(él / ella / usted — he/she has / you have)		 tienen TYEH-nehn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have / you all have)
Example sentences:
  • Yo tengo un libro. 🔊 yoh TEHN-goh oon LEE-broh — I have a book.
  • Tú tienes una idea. 🔊 too TYEH-nehs OO-nah ee-DEH-ah — You have an idea.
  • Ella tiene 25 años. 🔊 EH-yah TYEH-neh VYEHN-teh seen-koh AH-nyohs — She is 25 years old.
  • Nosotros tenemos que estudiar. 🔊 noh-SOH-trohs teh-NEH-mohs keh ehs-too-DYAHR — We have to study.
  • Ellos tienen hambre. 🔊 EH-yohs TYEH-nehn AHM-breh — They are hungry.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Tener is irregular in the preterite.

Singular Plural
tuve TOO-beh
(yo — I had)		 tuvimos too-BEE-mohs
(nosotros/as — we had)
tuviste too-BEES-teh
(tú — you had)		 tuvisteis too-BEES-tays
(vosotros/as — you all had)
tuvo TOO-boh
(él / ella / usted — he/she had / you had)		 tuvieron too-BYEH-rohn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they had / you all had)
Example sentences:
  • Yo tuve un perro cuando era niño. 🔊 yoh TOO-beh oon PEH-rroh KWAHN-doh EH-rah NEE-nyoh — I had a dog when I was a child.
  • Tú tuviste una buena idea ayer. 🔊 too too-BEES-teh OO-nah BWEH-nah ee-DEH-ah ah-YEHR — You had a good idea yesterday.
  • Ella tuvo mucho éxito en su carrera. 🔊 EH-yah TOO-boh MOO-choh EH-ksee-toh ehn soo kah-RRHEH-rah — She had a lot of success in her career.
  • Nosotros tuvimos que trabajar mucho. 🔊 noh-SOH-trohs too-BEE-mohs keh trah-bah-HAHR MOO-choh — We had to work a lot.
  • Ellos tuvieron miedo durante la tormenta. 🔊 EH-yohs too-BYEH-rohn MYEH-doh doo-RAHN-teh lah tohr-MEHN-tah — They were afraid during the storm.
Imperfect Tense (Past — Ongoing / Habitual)

Used for past actions that were ongoing, habitual, or without a specific end. Tener is regular in the imperfect.

Singular Plural
tenía teh-NEE-ah
(yo — I used to have / I was having)		 teníamos teh-NEE-ah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we used to have)
tenías teh-NEE-ahs
(tú — you used to have)		 teníais teh-NEE-eyes
(vosotros/as — you all used to have)
tenía teh-NEE-ah
(él / ella / usted — he/she used to have / you used to have)		 tenían teh-NEE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they used to have / you all used to have)
Example sentences:
  • Yo tenía un coche rojo. 🔊 yoh teh-NEE-ah oon KOH-cheh ROH-hoh — I used to have a red car.
  • Tú tenías mucho dinero cuando eras joven. 🔊 too teh-NEE-ahs MOO-choh dee-NEH-roh KWAHN-doh EH-rahs HOH-behn — You used to have a lot of money when you were young.
  • Ella tenía miedo de los perros. 🔊 EH-yah teh-NEE-ah MYEH-doh deh lohs PEH-rrohs — She used to be afraid of dogs.
  • Nosotros teníamos una casa grande. 🔊 noh-SOH-trohs teh-NEE-ah-mohs OO-nah KAH-sah GRAHN-deh — We used to have a big house.
  • Ellos tenían que trabajar todos los días. 🔊 EH-yohs teh-NEE-ahn keh trah-bah-HAHR TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs — They used to have to work every day.
Conditional Tense (Would — Hypothetical)

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Tener is irregular in the conditional (stem change).

Singular Plural
tendría tehn-DREE-ah
(yo — I would have)		 tendríamos tehn-DREE-ah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we would have)
tendrías tehn-DREE-ahs
(tú — you would have)		 tendríais tehn-DREE-eyes
(vosotros/as — you all would have)
tendría tehn-DREE-ah
(él / ella / usted — he/she would have / you would have)		 tendrían tehn-DREE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they would have / you all would have)
Example sentences:
  • Yo tendría más tiempo si no trabajara tanto. 🔊 yoh tehn-DREE-ah mahs TYEHM-poh see noh trah-bah-HAH-rah TAHN-toh — I would have more time if I didn't work so much.
  • Tú tendrías un coche nuevo si ahorraras. 🔊 too tehn-DREE-ahs oon KOH-cheh NWEH-boh see ah-oh-RRHAH-rahs — You would have a new car if you saved.
  • Ella tendría más energía si durmiera mejor. 🔊 EH-yah tehn-DREE-ah mahs eh-nehr-HEE-ah see door-MYEH-rah meh-HOR — She would have more energy if she slept better.
  • Nosotros tendríamos una casa más grande si pudiéramos. 🔊 noh-SOH-trohs tehn-DREE-ah-mohs OO-nah KAH-sah mahs GRAHN-deh see poo-DYEH-rah-mohs — We would have a bigger house if we could.
  • Ellos tendrían éxito si lo intentaran. 🔊 EH-yohs tehn-DREE-ahn EH-ksee-toh see loh een-tehn-TAH-rahn — They would have success if they tried.

(él / ella / usted — he/she will have / you will have)		 tendrán tehn-DRAHN
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they will have / you all will have)
Example sentences:
  • Yo tendré un coche nuevo el próximo año. 🔊 yoh tehn-DREH oon KOH-cheh NWEH-boh ehl PROH-ksee-moh AH-nyoh — I will have a new car next year.
  • Tú tendrás más tiempo cuando te jubiles. 🔊 too tehn-DRAHS mahs TYEHM-poh KWAHN-doh teh hoo-BEE-lehs — You will have more time when you retire.
  • Ella tendrá 30 años el mes que viene. 🔊 EH-yah tehn-DRAH treh-NTAH AH-nyohs ehl mehs keh BYEH-neh — She will be 30 years old next month.

  • Ellos tendrán una casa grande algún día. 🔊 EH-yohs tehn-DRAHN OO-nah KAH-sah GRAHN-deh ahl-GOON DEE-ah — They will have a big house someday.
Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + tenido (past participle of tener).

Singular Plural
he tenido eh teh-NEE-doh
(yo — I have had)		 hemos tenido EH-mohs teh-NEE-doh
(nosotros/as — we have had)
has tenido ahs teh-NEE-doh
(tú — you have had)		 habéis tenido ah-BEH-ees teh-NEE-doh
(vosotros/as — you all have had)
ha tenido ah teh-NEE-doh
(él / ella / usted — he/she has had / you have had)		 han tenido ahn teh-NEE-doh
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have had / you all have had)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he tenido un buen día hoy. 🔊 yoh eh teh-NEE-doh oon BWEHN DEE-ah oy — I have had a good day today.
  • ¿Has tenido alguna vez un perro? 🔊 ahs teh-NEE-doh ahl-GOO-nah behs oon PEH-rroh — Have you ever had a dog?
  • Ella ha tenido muchas oportunidades. 🔊 EH-yah ah teh-NEE-doh MOO-chahs oh-pohr-too-nee-DAH-dehs — She has had many opportunities.
  • Nosotros hemos tenido que trabajar mucho esta semana. 🔊 noh-SOH-trohs EH-mohs teh-NEE-doh keh trah-bah-HAHR MOO-choh EHS-tah seh-MAH-nah — We have had to work a lot this week.
  • Ellos han tenido éxito en sus proyectos. 🔊 EH-yohs ahn teh-NEE-doh EH-ksee-toh ehn soos proh-YEHS-tohs — They have had success in their projects.
