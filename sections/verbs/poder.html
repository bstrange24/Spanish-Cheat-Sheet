Poder

To be able to / can — permission, ability, and possibility

Note: Vos (used in parts of Latin America) is not included in this chart. Usted and ustedes use the same verb forms as él/ella and ellos/ellas.


Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Poder is a stem-changing verb (o → ue) in the present tense for all forms except nosotros and vosotros.

Singular Plural
puedo PWEH-doh
(yo — I can / I am able)		 podemos poh-DEH-mohs
(nosotros/as — we can / we are able)
puedes PWEH-dehs
(tú — you can / you are able)		 podéis poh-DEH-ees
(vosotros/as — you all can / you all are able)
puede PWEH-deh
(él / ella / usted — he/she can / you can)		 pueden PWEH-dehn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they can / you all can)
Example sentences:
  • Yo puedo nadar. yoh PWEH-doh nah-DAHR — I can swim.
  • Tú puedes venir mañana. too PWEH-dehs beh-NEER mah-NYAH-nah — You can come tomorrow.
  • Ella puede hablar tres idiomas. EH-yah PWEH-deh ah-BLAHR trehs ee-DYOH-mahs — She can speak three languages.
  • Nosotros podemos ayudarte. noh-SOH-trohs poh-DEH-mohs ah-yoo-DAHR-teh — We can help you.
  • Ellos pueden llegar tarde. EH-yohs PWEH-dehn yeh-GAHR TAHR-deh — They can arrive late.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Poder is irregular in the preterite (stem pud-). In the preterite, poder often means "managed to" or "succeeded in".

Singular Plural
pude POO-deh
(yo — I managed to / I could)		 pudimos poo-DEE-mohs
(nosotros/as — we managed to / we could)
pudiste poo-DEES-teh
(tú — you managed to / you could)		 pudisteis poo-DEES-tehs
(vosotros/as — you all managed to / you all could)
pudo POO-doh
(él / ella / usted — he/she managed to / you managed to)		 pudieron poo-DYEH-rohn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they managed to / you all managed to)
Example sentences:
  • Yo pude terminar el proyecto a tiempo. yoh POO-deh tehr-mee-NAHR ehl proh-YEHK-toh ah TYEHM-poh — I managed to finish the project on time.
  • Tú pudiste resolver el problema. too poo-DEES-teh reh-sohl-BEHR ehl proh-BLEH-mah — You managed to solve the problem.
  • Ella pudo llegar antes de que cerraran. EH-yah POO-doh yeh-GAHR AHN-tehs deh keh seh-RRAH-rahn — She managed to arrive before they closed.
  • Nosotros pudimos encontrar el restaurante. noh-SOH-trohs poo-DEE-mohs ehn-kohn-TRAHR ehl rehs-tow-RAHN-teh — We managed to find the restaurant.
  • Ellos pudieron terminar el trabajo. EH-yohs poo-DYEH-rohn tehr-mee-NAHR ehl trah-BAH-hoh — They managed to finish the work.
Imperfect Tense (Past — Ongoing / Habitual)

Used for past actions that were ongoing, habitual, or without a specific end. Poder is regular in the imperfect. In the imperfect, poder means "used to be able" or "was able to" in a general sense.

Singular Plural
podía poh-DEE-ah
(yo — I used to be able / I was able)		 podíamos poh-DEE-ah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we used to be able)
podías poh-DEE-ahs
(tú — you used to be able)		 podíais poh-DEE-ah-ees
(vosotros/as — you all used to be able)
podía poh-DEE-ah
(él / ella / usted — he/she used to be able / you used to be able)		 podían poh-DEE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they used to be able / you all used to be able)
Example sentences:
  • Yo podía nadar cuando era niño. yoh poh-DEE-ah nah-DAHR KWAHN-doh EH-rah NEE-nyoh — I used to be able to swim when I was a child.
  • Tú podías correr muy rápido. too poh-DEE-ahs koh-RREHR mwee RAH-pee-doh — You used to be able to run very fast.
  • Ella podía hablar francés cuando vivía en París. EH-yah poh-DEE-ah ah-BLAHR frahn-SEHS KWAHN-doh bee-BEE-ah ehn pah-REES — She used to be able to speak French when she lived in Paris.
  • Nosotros podíamos ver las estrellas desde aquí. noh-SOH-trohs poh-DEE-ah-mohs behr lahs ehs-TREH-yahs DEHS-deh ah-KEE — We used to be able to see the stars from here.
  • Ellos podían jugar en la calle antes. EH-yohs poh-DEE-ahn hoo-GAHR ehn lah KAH-yeh AHN-tehs — They used to be able to play in the street before.
Conditional Tense (Would — Hypothetical)

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Poder is irregular in the conditional (stem podr-). It is used for polite requests (¿Podrías...?).

Singular Plural
podría poh-DREE-ah
(yo — I could / I would be able)		 podríamos poh-DREE-ah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we could / we would be able)
podrías poh-DREE-ahs
(tú — you could / you would be able)		 podríais poh-DREE-ah-ees
(vosotros/as — you all could / you all would be able)
podría poh-DREE-ah
(él / ella / usted — he/she could / you could)		 podrían poh-DREE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they could / you all could)
Example sentences:
  • ¿Podrías ayudarme con esto? poh-DREE-ahs ah-yoo-DAHR-meh kohn EHS-toh — Could you help me with this? (polite request)
  • Yo podría ir mañana si quisiera. yoh poh-DREE-ah eer mah-NYAH-nah see kee-SYEH-rah — I could go tomorrow if I wanted.
  • Ella podría terminar el proyecto sola. EH-yah poh-DREE-ah tehr-mee-NAHR ehl proh-YEHK-toh SOH-lah — She could finish the project alone.
  • Nosotros podríamos viajar si tuviéramos dinero. noh-SOH-trohs poh-DREE-ah-mohs byah-HAHR see too-BYEHR-ah-mohs dee-NEH-roh — We could travel if we had money.
  • Ellos podrían llegar a tiempo si salieran ahora. EH-yohs poh-DREE-ahn yeh-GAHR ah TYEHM-poh see sah-LYEH-rahn ah-OH-rah — They could arrive on time if they left now.

Used to express what will happen. Poder is irregular in the future (stem podr-).

(yo — I will be able)		 podremos poh-DREH-mohs
(nosotros/as — we will be able)
podrás poh-DRAHS
(tú — you will be able)		 podréis poh-DREH-ees
(vosotros/as — you all will be able)
podrá poh-DRAH
(él / ella / usted — he/she will be able / you will be able)		 podrán poh-DRAHN
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they will be able / you all will be able)
Example sentences:
  • Yo podré terminar el trabajo mañana. yoh poh-DREH tehr-mee-NAHR ehl trah-BAH-hoh mah-NYAH-nah — I will be able to finish the work tomorrow.
  • Tú podrás venir a la fiesta. too poh-DRAHS beh-NEER ah lah FYEHS-tah — You will be able to come to the party.
  • Ella podrá hablar con el director. EH-yah poh-DRAH ah-BLAHR kohn ehl dee-rehk-TOHR — She will be able to speak with the director.
  • Nosotros podremos resolver este problema. noh-SOH-trohs poh-DREH-mohs reh-sohl-BEHR EHS-teh proh-BLEH-mah — We will be able to solve this problem.
  • Ellos podrán llegar antes de la cena. EH-yohs poh-DRAHN yeh-GAHR AHN-tehs deh lah SEH-nah — They will be able to arrive before dinner.
Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + podido (past participle of poder).

Singular Plural
he podido eh poh-DEE-doh
(yo — I have been able)		 hemos podido EH-mohs poh-DEE-doh
(nosotros/as — we have been able)
has podido ahs poh-DEE-doh
(tú — you have been able)		 habéis podido ah-BEH-ees poh-DEE-doh
(vosotros/as — you all have been able)
ha podido ah poh-DEE-doh
(él / ella / usted — he/she has been able / you have been able)		 han podido ahn poh-DEE-doh
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have been able / you all have been able)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he podido terminar el proyecto. yoh eh poh-DEE-doh tehr-mee-NAHR ehl proh-YEHK-toh — I have been able to finish the project.
  • ¿Has podido hablar con ella? ahs poh-DEE-doh ah-BLAHR kohn EH-yah — Have you been able to speak with her?
  • Ella ha podido resolver el problema. EH-yah ah poh-DEE-doh reh-sohl-BEHR ehl proh-BLEH-mah — She has been able to solve the problem.
  • Nosotros hemos podido encontrar el lugar. noh-SOH-trohs EH-mohs poh-DEE-doh ehn-kohn-TRAHR ehl loo-GAHR — We have been able to find the place.
  • Ellos han podido llegar a tiempo. EH-yohs ahn poh-DEE-doh yeh-GAHR ah TYEHM-poh — They have been able to arrive on time.
Poder — Uses (to be able to / can)

Poder is used for:

Use Example Pronunciation English
Ability Puedo nadar. PWEH-doh nah-DAHR I can swim.
Permission ¿Puedo entrar? PWEH-doh ehn-TRAHR May I come in?
Possibility Puede llover. PWEH-deh yoh-BEHR It might rain.
Polite request (conditional) ¿Podrías ayudarme? poh-DREE-ahs ah-yoo-DAHR-meh Could you help me?
Managed to (preterite) Pude terminar. POO-deh tehr-mee-NAHR I managed to finish.
More examples with poder
  • ¿Puedes ayudarme? PWEH-dehs ah-yoo-DAHR-meh — Can you help me?
  • No puedo ir hoy. noh PWEH-doh eer oy — I can't go today.
  • ¿Podría usar su teléfono? poh-DREE-ah oo-SAHR soo teh-LEH-foh-noh — Could I use your phone? (formal)
  • No pude encontrar las llaves. noh POO-deh ehn-kohn-TRAHR lahs YAH-behs — I couldn't find the keys. (I didn't manage to)
  • Mañana podré descansar. mah-NYAH-nah poh-DREH dehs-kahn-SAHR — Tomorrow I will be able to rest.
Poder tips
  • Follow with an infinitive: puedo ir PWEH-doh eer, not *puedo voy.
  • Preterite pude POO-deh often means "I succeeded / I managed." Imperfect podía poh-DEE-ah is general ability in the past.
  • No puedo noh PWEH-doh = I can't. No pude noh POO-deh = I wasn't able to (that time).
  • In the present, ¿Puedes? PWEH-dehs means "Can you?" In the conditional, ¿Podrías? poh-DREE-ahs is more polite — "Could you?"
