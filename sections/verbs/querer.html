Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Querer is a stem-changing verb (e → ie) in the present tense for all forms except nosotros and vosotros.

Singular Plural
quiero
(yo — I want / I love)		 queremos
(nosotros/as — we want / we love)
quieres
(tú — you want / you love)		 queréis
(vosotros/as — you all want / you all love)
quiere
(él / ella / usted — he/she wants / you want)		 quieren
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they want / you all want)
Example sentences:
  • Yo quiero un café. 🔊 — I want a coffee.
  • Tú quieres ir al cine. 🔊 — You want to go to the movies.
  • Ella quiere viajar a España. 🔊 — She wants to travel to Spain.
  • Nosotros queremos ayudarte. 🔊 — We want to help you.
  • Ellos quieren una casa grande. 🔊 — They want a big house.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Querer is irregular in the preterite (stem quis-). In the preterite, querer often means "tried to" or "attempted to" (especially in negative: no quise = I refused).

Singular Plural
quise
(yo — I tried / I wanted to)		 quisimos
(nosotros/as — we tried / we wanted to)
quisiste
(tú — you tried / you wanted to)		 quisisteis
(vosotros/as — you all tried / you all wanted to)
quiso
(él / ella / usted — he/she tried / you tried)		 quisieron
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they tried / you all tried)
Example sentences:
  • Yo quise llamarte pero no pude. 🔊 — I tried to call you but I couldn't.
  • Tú quisiste comprar ese coche. 🔊 — You wanted to buy that car (and you tried).
  • Ella quiso llegar temprano. 🔊 — She tried to arrive early.
  • Nosotros quisimos ayudarlos. 🔊 — We tried to help them.
  • Ellos quisieron terminar el proyecto. 🔊 — They tried to finish the project.

Example sentences:

  • Tú querías viajar por todo el mundo. 🔊 — You wanted to travel around the world.
  • Ella quería mucho a su abuela. 🔊 — She loved her grandmother very much.
  • Nosotros queríamos comprar esa casa. 🔊 — We wanted to buy that house.
  • Ellos querían aprender español. 🔊 — They wanted to learn Spanish.

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Querer is irregular in the conditional (stem querr-). It is often used for polite wishes.

(él / ella / usted — he/she would want / you would want)		 querrían
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they would want / you all would want)
Example sentences:
  • Yo querría un café, por favor. 🔊 — I would like a coffee, please. (polite)
  • Tú querrías viajar a Italia si pudieras. 🔊 — You would want to travel to Italy if you could.
  • Ella querría ayudarte pero no puede. 🔊 — She would want to help you but she can't.
  • Nosotros querríamos más información. 🔊 — We would like more information.
  • Ellos querrían quedarse más tiempo. 🔊 — They would like to stay longer.

Querer — Uses (to want / to love)

Querer is used for:

Use Example Pronunciation English
Want a thing Quiero un café. 🔊 KYEH-roh oon kah-FEH I want a coffee.
Want to do (infinitive) Quiero salir. 🔊 KYEH-roh sah-LEER I want to go out.
Want someone else to do → subjunctive Quiero que vengas. 🔊 KYEH-roh kay BEHN-gahs I want you to come.
Love (people) Te quiero. 🔊 teh KYEH-roh I love you. (warm / not as heavy as te amo)
Polite "I'd like" (imperfect subjunctive) Quisiera el menú. 🔊 kee-SYEH-rah ehl meh-NOO I would like the menu.
More examples with querer
  • ¿Qué quieres hacer hoy? 🔊 — What do you want to do today?
  • Quiero que me escuches. 🔊 — I want you to listen to me.
  • Te quiero mucho. 🔊 — I love you very much.
  • Quisiera dos entradas. 🔊 — I would like two tickets.
  • No quiero irme. 🔊 — I don't want to leave.
Querer tips
  • Querer + infinitive when you do it: quiero comer.
  • Querer que + subjunctive when someone else does it: quiero que comas.
  • Preterite no quise often = I refused. Imperfect quería = I wanted (as a state).
  • Te quiero is the most common way to say "I love you" to family, friends, and partners. Te amo is stronger and more romantic.
  • In restaurants, quisiera is a very polite way to order: Quisiera una paella.
