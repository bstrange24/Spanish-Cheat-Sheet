Querer

To want / to love — wishes, requests, and querer que + subjunctive

Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Querer is a stem-changing verb (e → ie) in the present tense for all forms except nosotros and vosotros.

Singular Plural
quiero KYEH-roh
(yo — I want / I love)		 queremos keh-REH-mohs
(nosotros/as — we want / we love)
quieres KYEH-rehs
(tú — you want / you love)		 queréis keh-RAYS
(vosotros/as — you all want / you all love)
quiere KYEH-reh
(él / ella / usted — he/she wants / you want)		 quieren KYEH-rehn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they want / you all want)
Example sentences:
  • Yo quiero un café. yoh KYEH-roh oon kah-FEH — I want a coffee.
  • Tú quieres ir al cine. too KYEH-rehs eer ahl SEE-neh — You want to go to the movies.
  • Ella quiere viajar a España. EH-yah KYEH-reh byah-HAHR ah ehs-PAH-nyah — She wants to travel to Spain.
  • Nosotros queremos ayudarte. noh-SOH-trohs keh-REH-mohs ah-yoo-DAHR-teh — We want to help you.
  • Ellos quieren una casa grande. EH-yohs KYEH-rehn OO-nah KAH-sah GRAHN-deh — They want a big house.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Querer is irregular in the preterite (stem quis-). In the preterite, querer often means "tried to" or "attempted to" (especially in negative: no quise = I refused).

Singular Plural
quise KEE-seh
(yo — I tried / I wanted to)		 quisimos kee-SEE-mohs
(nosotros/as — we tried / we wanted to)
quisiste kee-SEES-teh
(tú — you tried / you wanted to)		 quisisteis kee-SEES-tays
(vosotros/as — you all tried / you all wanted to)
quiso KEE-soh
(él / ella / usted — he/she tried / you tried)		 quisieron kee-SYEH-rohn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they tried / you all tried)
Example sentences:
  • Yo quise llamarte pero no pude. yoh KEE-seh yah-MAHR-teh PEH-roh noh POO-deh — I tried to call you but I couldn't.
  • Tú quisiste comprar ese coche. too kee-SEES-teh kohm-PRAHR EH-seh KOH-cheh — You wanted to buy that car (and you tried).
  • Ella quiso llegar temprano. EH-yah KEE-soh yeh-GAHR tehm-PRAH-noh — She tried to arrive early.
  • Nosotros quisimos ayudarlos. noh-SOH-trohs kee-SEE-mohs ah-yoo-DAHR-lohs — We tried to help them.
  • Ellos quisieron terminar el proyecto. EH-yohs kee-SYEH-rohn tehr-mee-NAHR ehl proh-YEHS-toh — They tried to finish the project.
Imperfect Tense (Past — Ongoing / Habitual)

Used for past actions that were ongoing, habitual, or without a specific end. Querer is regular in the imperfect. In the imperfect, querer means "wanted" as a state of mind (not an action).

Singular Plural
quería keh-REE-ah
(yo — I wanted / I used to want)		 queríamos keh-REE-ah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we wanted / we used to want)
querías keh-REE-ahs
(tú — you wanted / you used to want)		 queríais keh-REE-eyes
(vosotros/as — you all wanted / you all used to want)
quería keh-REE-ah
(él / ella / usted — he/she wanted / you wanted)		 querían keh-REE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they wanted / you all wanted)
Example sentences:
  • Yo quería ser médico. yoh keh-REE-ah sehr MEH-dee-koh — I wanted to be a doctor (I used to want).
  • Tú querías viajar por todo el mundo. too keh-REE-ahs byah-HAHR por TOH-doh ehl MOON-doh — You wanted to travel around the world.
  • Ella quería mucho a su abuela. EH-yah keh-REE-ah MOO-choh ah soo ah-BWEH-lah — She loved her grandmother very much.
  • Nosotros queríamos comprar esa casa. noh-SOH-trohs keh-REE-ah-mohs kohm-PRAHR EH-sah KAH-sah — We wanted to buy that house.
  • Ellos querían aprender español. EH-yohs keh-REE-ahn ah-prehn-DEHR ehs-pah-NYOHL — They wanted to learn Spanish.
Conditional Tense (Would — Hypothetical)

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Querer is irregular in the conditional (stem querr-). It is often used for polite wishes.

Singular Plural
querría keh-RRHEE-ah
(yo — I would want / I would love)		 querríamos keh-RRHEE-ah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we would want / we would love)
querrías keh-RRHEE-ahs
(tú — you would want / you would love)		 querríais keh-RRHEE-eyes
(vosotros/as — you all would want / you all would love)
querría keh-RRHEE-ah
(él / ella / usted — he/she would want / you would want)		 querrían keh-RRHEE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they would want / you all would want)
Example sentences:
  • Yo querría un café, por favor. yoh keh-RRHEE-ah oon kah-FEH por fah-BOHR — I would like a coffee, please. (polite)
  • Tú querrías viajar a Italia si pudieras. too keh-RRHEE-ahs byah-HAHR ah ee-TAH-lyah see poo-DYEH-rahs — You would want to travel to Italy if you could.
  • Ella querría ayudarte pero no puede. EH-yah keh-RRHEE-ah ah-yoo-DAHR-teh PEH-roh noh PWEH-deh — She would want to help you but she can't.
  • Nosotros querríamos más información. noh-SOH-trohs keh-RRHEE-ah-mohs mahs een-fohr-mah-SYOHN — We would like more information.
  • Ellos querrían quedarse más tiempo. EH-yohs keh-RRHEE-ahn keh-DAHR-seh mahs TYEHM-poh — They would like to stay longer.

Used to express what will happen. Querer is irregular in the future (stem querr-).

(nosotros/as — we will want / we will love)
querrás keh-RRHAHS
(tú — you will want / you will love)		 querréis keh-RRHAYS
(vosotros/as — you all will want / you all will love)
querrá keh-RRHAH
(él / ella / usted — he/she will want / you will want)		 querrán keh-RRHAHN
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they will want / you all will want)
Example sentences:
  • Yo querré ir a la fiesta mañana. yoh keh-RRHEH eer ah lah FYEHS-tah mah-NYAH-nah — I will want to go to the party tomorrow.
  • Tú querrás saber la verdad algún día. too keh-RRHAHS sah-BEHR lah behr-DAHD ahl-GOON DEE-ah — You will want to know the truth someday.
  • Ella querrá verte cuando llegues. EH-yah keh-RRHAH BEHR-teh KWAHN-doh YEH-hehs — She will want to see you when you arrive.
  • Nosotros querremos descansar después del viaje. noh-SOH-trohs keh-RRHEH-mohs dehs-kahn-SAHR dehs-PWEHS dehl BYAH-heh — We will want to rest after the trip.
  • Ellos querrán celebrar su victoria. EH-yohs keh-RRHAHN seh-leh-BRAHR soo bees-TOH-ryah — They will want to celebrate their victory.
Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + querido (past participle of querer).

Singular Plural
he querido eh keh-REE-doh
(yo — I have wanted / I have loved)		 hemos querido EH-mohs keh-REE-doh
(nosotros/as — we have wanted / we have loved)
has querido ahs keh-REE-doh
(tú — you have wanted / you have loved)		 habéis querido ah-BEH-ees keh-REE-doh
(vosotros/as — you all have wanted / you all have loved)
ha querido ah keh-REE-doh
(él / ella / usted — he/she has wanted / you have wanted)		 han querido ahn keh-REE-doh
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have wanted / you all have wanted)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he querido viajar a Japón desde niño. yoh eh keh-REE-doh byah-HAHR ah hah-POHN DEHS-deh NEE-nyoh — I have wanted to travel to Japan since I was a child.
  • ¿Has querido llamarla alguna vez? ahs keh-REE-doh yah-MAHR-lah ahl-GOO-nah behs — Have you ever wanted to call her?
  • Ella ha querido mucho a sus amigos. EH-yah ah keh-REE-doh MOO-choh ah soos ah-MEE-gohs — She has loved her friends very much.
  • Nosotros hemos querido comprar una casa nueva. noh-SOH-trohs EH-mohs keh-REE-doh kohm-PRAHR OO-nah KAH-sah NWEH-bah — We have wanted to buy a new house.
  • Ellos han querido cambiar de trabajo. EH-yohs ahn keh-REE-doh chahm-BYAHR deh trah-BAH-hoh — They have wanted to change jobs.
Querer — Uses (to want / to love)

Querer is used for:

Use Example Pronunciation English
Want a thing Quiero un café. KYEH-roh oon kah-FEH I want a coffee.
Want to do (infinitive) Quiero salir. KYEH-roh sah-LEER I want to go out.
Want someone else to do → subjunctive Quiero que vengas. KYEH-roh kay BEHN-gahs I want you to come.
Love (people) Te quiero. teh KYEH-roh I love you. (warm / not as heavy as te amo)
Polite "I'd like" (imperfect subjunctive) Quisiera el menú. kee-SYEH-rah ehl meh-NOO I would like the menu.
More examples with querer
  • ¿Qué quieres hacer hoy? keh KYEH-rehs ah-SEHR oy — What do you want to do today?
  • Quiero que me escuches. KYEH-roh keh meh ehs-KOO-chehs — I want you to listen to me.
  • Te quiero mucho. teh KYEH-roh MOO-choh — I love you very much.
  • Quisiera dos entradas. kee-SYEH-rah dohs ehn-TRAH-dahs — I would like two tickets.
  • No quiero irme. noh KYEH-roh EER-meh — I don't want to leave.
Querer tips
  • Querer keh-REHR + infinitive when you do it: quiero comer KYEH-roh koh-MEHR.
  • Querer que keh-REHR keh + subjunctive when someone else does it: quiero que comas KYEH-roh keh KOH-mahs.
  • Preterite no quise noh KEE-seh often = I refused. Imperfect quería keh-REE-ah = I wanted (as a state).
  • Te quiero teh KYEH-roh is the most common way to say "I love you" to family, friends, and partners. Te amo teh AH-moh is stronger and more romantic.
  • In restaurants, quisiera kee-SYEH-rah is a very polite way to order: Quisiera una paella kee-SYEH-rah OO-nah pah-EH-yah.
