Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Jugar is a stem-changing verb (u → ue) in the present tense for all forms except nosotros and vosotros.

Singular Plural
juego HWEH-goh
(yo — I play)		 jugamos hoo-GAH-mohs
(nosotros/as — we play)
juegas HWEH-gahs
(tú — you play)		 jugáis hoo-GEYES
(vosotros/as — you all play)
juega HWEH-gah
(él / ella / usted — he/she plays / you play)		 juegan HWEH-gahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they play / you all play)
Example sentences:
  • Yo juego al fútbol. yoh HWEH-goh ahl FOOT-bohl
    I play soccer.
  • Tú juegas al tenis. too HWEH-gahs ahl TEH-nees
    You play tennis.
  • Ella juega al ajedrez. EH-yah HWEH-gah ahl ah-heh-DREHS
    She plays chess.
  • Nosotros jugamos a las cartas. noh-SOH-trohs hoo-GAH-mohs ah lahs KAHR-tahs
    We play cards.
  • Ellos juegan en el parque. EH-yohs HWEH-gahn ehn ehl PAHR-keh
    They play in the park.

Jugar — Uses (to play)

Jugar is used for:

Use Example Pronunciation English
Sports & games (with a) Juego al tenis. HWEH-goh ahl TEH-nees I play tennis.
Sports & games (with a) Juegan al fútbol. HWEH-gahn ahl FOOT-bohl They play soccer.
Board games (with a) Jugamos al ajedrez. hoo-GAH-mohs ahl ah-heh-DREHS We play chess.
Card games (with a) Juegas a las cartas. HWEH-gahs ah lahs KAHR-tahs You play cards.
Video games (with a) Juego a videojuegos. HWEH-goh ah bee-deh-oh-HWEH-gohs I play video games.
Playing an instrument (use tocar instead) Toco la guitarra. TOH-koh lah gee-TAH-rrah I play the guitar. (not jugar)
Playing with a toy / object (with con) Juego con mi perro. HWEH-goh kohn mee PEH-rroh I play with my dog.
Playing a role / pretend Juega a ser doctor. HWEH-gah ah sehr DOHK-tor He plays at being a doctor.
To play around / not be serious No juegues conmigo. noh HWEH-ghehs kohn-MEE-goh Don't play with me.
Example sentences:
  • ¿Juegas al fútbol? HWEH-gahs ahl FOOT-bohl
    Do you play soccer?
  • Juego al baloncesto los sábados. HWEH-goh ahl bah-lohn-SEHS-toh lohs SAH-bah-dohs
    I play basketball on Saturdays.
  • Los niños juegan en el jardín. lohs NEE-nyohs HWEH-gahn ehn ehl hahr-DEEN
    The children play in the garden.
  • ¿Quieres jugar conmigo? KYEH-rehs hoo-GAHR kohn-MEE-goh
    Do you want to play with me?
  • Jugamos a las escondidas. hoo-GAH-mohs ah lahs ehs-kohn-DEE-dahs
    We play hide and seek.
Jugar vs. Tocar (Quick Comparison)
Situation Verb Example Pronunciation English
Sports & games jugar + a Juego al fútbol. HWEH-goh ahl FOOT-bohl I play soccer.
Musical instruments tocar Toco la guitarra. TOH-koh lah gee-TAH-rrah I play the guitar.
Playing with someone/something jugar + con Juego con mi hermano. HWEH-goh kohn mee ehr-MAH-noh I play with my brother.
Playing a game (general) jugar + a Jugamos a un juego. hoo-GAH-mohs ah oon HWEH-goh We play a game.
Playing a role / pretend jugar + a ser Juega a ser bombero. HWEH-gah ah sehr bohm-BEH-roh He plays at being a firefighter.
To touch / to play (instrument) tocar Toca el piano. TOH-kah ehl PYAH-noh He plays the piano.
