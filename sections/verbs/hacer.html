Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Hacer is irregular in the present tense (yo form only).

Singular Plural
hago ah-go
(yo — I do / I make)		 hacemos ah-say-mos
(nosotros/as — we do / we make)
haces ah-says
(tú — you do / you make)		 hacéis ah-say-es
(vosotros/as — you all do / you all make)
hace ah-say
(él / ella / usted)
he does/she does - he do/she do - you do 		hacen ah-sane
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they do / you all do)
Example sentences:
  • Yo hago la tarea. yoh AH-goh lah tah-REH-ah — I do the homework.
  • Tú haces un pastel. too AH-sehs oon pahs-TEHL — You make a cake.
  • Ella hace ejercicio. EH-yah AH-seh eh-hehr-SEE-syoh — She exercises.
  • Nosotros hacemos la cama. noh-SOH-trohs ah-SEH-mohs lah KAH-mah — We make the bed.
  • Ellos hacen la comida. EH-yohs AH-sehn lah koh-MEE-dah — They make the food.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Hacer is irregular in the preterite.

(nosotros/as — we did / we made)
hiciste ee-SEES-teh
(tú — you did / you made)		 hicisteis ee-SEES-tays
(vosotros/as — you all did / you all made)
hizo EE-soh
(él / ella / usted — he/she did / you did)		 hicieron ee-SYEH-rohn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they did / you all did)
Example sentences:
  • Yo hice la tarea ayer. yoh EE-seh lah tah-REH-ah ah-YEHR — I did the homework yesterday.
  • Tú hiciste un pastel el sábado. too ee-SEES-teh oon pahs-TEHL ehl SAH-bah-doh — You made a cake on Saturday.
  • Ella hizo ejercicio esta mañana. EH-yah EE-soh eh-hehr-SEE-syoh EHS-tah mah-NYAH-nah — She exercised this morning.
  • Nosotros hicimos un viaje el verano pasado. noh-SOH-trohs ee-SEE-mohs oon BYAH-heh ehl beh-RAH-noh pah-SAH-doh — We took a trip last summer.
  • Ellos hicieron la comida para la fiesta. EH-yohs ee-SYEH-rohn lah koh-MEE-dah PAH-rah lah FYEHS-tah — They made the food for the party.
Imperfect Tense (Past — Ongoing / Habitual)

Used for past actions that were ongoing, habitual, or without a specific end. Hacer is regular in the imperfect.

hacía ah-SEE-ah
(él / ella / usted — he/she used to do / you used to do)		 hacían ah-SEE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they used to do / you all used to do)
Example sentences:
  • Yo hacía la tarea todos los días. yoh ah-SEE-ah lah tah-REH-ah TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs — I used to do the homework every day.
  • Tú hacías pasteles cada semana. too ah-SEE-ahs pahs-TEH-lehs KAH-dah seh-MAH-nah — You used to make cakes every week.
  • Ella hacía ejercicio cuando era joven. EH-yah ah-SEE-ah eh-hehr-SEE-syoh KWAHN-doh EH-rah HOH-behn — She used to exercise when she was young.
  • Nosotros hacíamos un viaje cada verano. noh-SOH-trohs ah-SEE-ah-mohs oon BYAH-heh KAH-dah beh-RAH-noh — We used to take a trip every summer.
  • Ellos hacían la comida para la familia. EH-yohs ah-SEE-ahn lah koh-MEE-dah PAH-rah lah fah-MEE-lyah — They used to make food for the family.
Conditional Tense (Would — Hypothetical)

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Hacer is irregular in the conditional (stem change).

(él / ella / usted — he/she would do / you would do)		 harían ah-REE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they would do / you all would do)
Example sentences:
  • Yo haría la tarea si tuviera tiempo. yoh ah-REE-ah lah tah-REH-ah see too-BYEH-rah TYEHM-poh — I would do the homework if I had time.
  • Tú harías un pastel para la fiesta. too ah-REE-ahs oon pahs-TEHL PAH-rah lah FYEHS-tah — You would make a cake for the party.
  • Ella haría ejercicio pero está cansada. EH-yah ah-REE-ah eh-hehr-SEE-syoh PEH-roh ehs-TAH kahn-SAH-dah — She would exercise but she's tired.
  • Nosotros haríamos un viaje si pudiéramos. noh-SOH-trohs ah-REE-ah-mohs oon BYAH-heh see poo-DYEH-rah-mohs — We would take a trip if we could.
  • Ellos harían la comida si tuvieran ingredientes. EH-yohs ah-REE-ahn lah koh-MEE-dah see too-BYEH-rahn een-greh-DYEHN-tehs — They would make the food if they had ingredients.

Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + hecho (irregular past participle of hacer).

Singular Plural
he hecho eh EH-choh
(yo — I have done / I have made)		 hemos hecho EH-mohs EH-choh
(nosotros/as — we have done / we have made)
has hecho ahs EH-choh
(tú — you have done / you have made)		 habéis hecho ah-BEH-ees EH-choh
(vosotros/as — you all have done / you all have made)
ha hecho ah EH-choh
(él / ella / usted — he/she has done / you have done)		 han hecho ahn EH-choh
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have done / you all have done)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he hecho la tarea hoy. yoh eh EH-choh lah tah-REH-ah oy — I have done the homework today.
  • ¿Has hecho un pastel alguna vez? ahs EH-choh oon pahs-TEHL ahl-GOO-nah behs — Have you ever made a cake?
  • Ella ha hecho ejercicio esta semana. EH-yah ah EH-choh eh-hehr-SEE-syoh EHS-tah seh-MAH-nah — She has exercised this week.
  • Nosotros hemos hecho un viaje a México. noh-SOH-trohs EH-mohs EH-choh oon BYAH-heh ah MEH-hee-koh — We have taken a trip to Mexico.
  • Ellos han hecho la comida hoy. EH-yohs ahn EH-choh lah koh-MEE-dah oy — They have made the food today.

Hacer vs. Ser vs. Estar (Quick Comparison)
Situation Verb Example Pronunciation English
Identity / Origin / Profession ser Soy de México. soy deh MEH-hee-koh I am from Mexico.
Location / Condition / Emotion estar Estoy en casa. ehs-TOY ehn KAH-sah I am at home.
Weather (temperature) hacer Hace frío. AH-seh FREE-oh It's cold.
Weather (description / condition) estar Está nublado. ehs-TAH noo-BLAH-doh It's cloudy.
Weather (action) estar + gerund Está lloviendo. ehs-TAH yoh-BYEHN-doh It's raining.
Time (on the clock) ser Son las dos. sohn lahs dohs It's two o'clock.
Time (duration) hacer Hace dos horas que espero. AH-seh dohs OH-rahs keh ehs-PEH-roh I have been waiting for two hours.
Day / Date ser Hoy es lunes. oy ehs LOO-nehs Today is Monday.
Quick decision guide
Ask yourself… Use Example Pronunciation English
Is it a Description, Occupation, Characteristic, Time (day/date), Origin, or Relationship? (DOCTOR) ser Es un médico. ehs oon MEH-dee-koh He is a doctor.
Is it Position, Location, Action, Condition, or Emotion? (PLACE) estar Está en casa. ehs-TAH ehn KAH-sah He is at home.
Is it weather (temperature) or time (duration)? hacer Hace frío. / Hace dos años que… AH-seh FREE-oh / AH-seh dohs AH-nyohs keh It's cold. / It's been two years since…
Is it weather (condition / state)? estar Está nublado. ehs-TAH noo-BLAH-doh It's cloudy.
Is it weather (action)? estar + gerund Está lloviendo. ehs-TAH yoh-BYEHN-doh It's raining.
Is it time (on the clock)? ser Son las tres. sohn lahs trehs It's three o'clock.
