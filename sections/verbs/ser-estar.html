Ser - Estar

Location, identity, feelings, and more

Ser vs. Estar — Full Conjugation Comparison (to be)

Ser = permanent characteristics (identity, origin, time, profession)
Estar = temporary states, locations, conditions, emotions, progressive actions

Ser — DOCTOR (Uses)

D.O.C.T.O.R. — Use ser for:

Letter Use Example Pronunciation English
D Description (physical/personality)
Essential qualities that define a person or thing and probably won’t change anytime soon. These descriptions can be names, physical descriptions, nationalities, and even religions. 		Él es alto y rubio. 🔊 ehl ehs AHL-toh ee ROO-byoh He is tall and blond.
O Origin (where from / nationality)
The place a person or thing is from or the material something is made of can be considered an origin. 		Somos de Colombia. 🔊 SOH-mohs deh koh-LOHM-byah We are from Colombia.
C Characteristic (traits/qualities)
Are personality descriptions of a person. This category is included to hammer home the point that ser is used to talk about descriptions. 		Ella es muy inteligente. 🔊 EH-yah ehs mwee een-teh-lee-HEHN-teh She is very intelligent.
T Time (date/day/time)
Can refer to days, dates, years, and the time on the clock. 		Son las tres. 🔊 sohn lahs trehs It's three o'clock.
O Occupation (job/profession)
What someone does for a living or as a hobby. Basically, if you're talking about how someone makes money or fills their time, you'll use ser. 		Soy profesor. 🔊 soy proh-feh-SOHR I am a teacher.
R Relationship (family/friends)
Personal relationships, such as family ties, friendship, and romantic relationships, are also talked about using ser. 		Ella es mi hermana. 🔊 EH-yah ehs mee ehr-MAH-nah She is my sister.

Estar — PLACE (Uses)

P.L.A.C.E. — Use estar for:

Letter Use Example Pronunciation English
P Position
Position refers to the physical position or posture a person or thing is in. 		El libro está en la mesa. 🔊 ehl LEE-broh ehs-TAH ehn lah MEH-sah The book is on the table.
L Location (where something is)
The location of someone or something describes where it is permanently, temporarily, or conceptually. 		Estamos en el parque. 🔊 ehs-TAH-mohs ehn ehl PAHR-kay We are in the park.
A Action (progressive -ing)
Estar is used to describe ongoing actions and is often followed by a present participle (such as lavando or a past participle (such as muerto. 		Estamos comiendo. 🔊 ehs-TAH-mohs koh-MYEHN-doh We are eating.
C Condition (physical/emotional state)
Physical and mental conditions are described using estar. Things that are likely to vary over several hours, days, or even years can fall into this category. 		Estoy feliz. 🔊 ehs-TOY feh-LEES I am happy.
E Emotion (feelings/moods)
How a person is feeling at a certain moment is described using estar. 		Está triste. 🔊 ehs-TAH TREES-teh He/she is sad.

Quick decision guide
Ask yourself… Use Example
Is it a Description, Occupation, Characteristic, Time, Origin, or Relationship? (DOCTOR) ser Es un médico. 🔊
Is it Position, Location, Action, Condition, or Emotion? (PLACE) estar Está en casa. 🔊
Where is a person/thing? estar Está en casa. 🔊
Where is an event? ser La fiesta es aquí. 🔊
What time/day is it? ser Es martes. 🔊 / Son las dos. 🔊
Where are you from? ser Soy de … 🔊
Are you doing something right now? estar + -ando/-iendo Estoy comiendo. 🔊
Is it a temporary condition? estar often Está abierto. 🔊 / Está cerrado. 🔊 / Está listo. 🔊
Is it a lasting trait or category? ser often Es alto. 🔊 / Es mexicano. 🔊 / Es mi hermano. 🔊
Is it a physical condition? estar Estoy cansado. 🔊 / Estoy enfermo. 🔊
Is it the date or time? ser Hoy es lunes. 🔊 / Son las ocho. 🔊

If stuck:

  • ser ≈ "equals / is (by nature)" — D.O.C.T.O.R.
  • estar ≈ "is located / is currently in a state" — P.L.A.C.E.

Ser vs estar tips
  • Ser = essence / identity / time / origin / traits / event location. — D.O.C.T.O.R.
  • Estar = location of people & things / feelings / conditions / progressive (-ing). — P.L.A.C.E.
  • ¿Cómo es? = What is he/she/it like? — ser (Description — DOCTOR)
  • ¿Cómo está? = How is he/she (feeling)? — estar (Emotion — PLACE)
  • ¿De dónde es? = Where is he/she from? — ser (Origin — DOCTOR)
  • ¿Dónde está? = Where is he/she/it (now)? — estar (Location — PLACE)
  • Adjective pairs can flip meaning: es listo (smart) vs está listo (ready).
  • Event place → ser; person/thing place → estar.
  • Conjugations (present): soy/eres/es/somos/sois/son vs estoy/estás/está/estamos/estáis/están.
  • For weather temperature, Spanish often uses hacer (hace frío), not ser/estar.
  • Progressive tense: estar + comiendo (eating), hablando (speaking). — A (PLACE)
  • Death: estar (state), not ser. — C (PLACE)
  • Marital status: estar (state), not ser. — C (PLACE)
