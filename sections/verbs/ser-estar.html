Ser — DOCTOR (Uses)

D.O.C.T.O.R. — Use ser sehr for:

Letter Use Example Pronunciation English
D Description (physical/personality)
Essential qualities that define a person or thing and probably won’t change anytime soon. These descriptions can be names, physical descriptions, nationalities, and even religions. 		Él es alto y rubio. ehl ehs AHL-toh ee ROO-byoh ehl ehs AHL-toh ee ROO-byoh He is tall and blond.
O Origin (where from / nationality)
The place a person or thing is from or the material something is made of can be considered an origin. 		Somos de Colombia. SOH-mohs deh koh-LOHM-byah SOH-mohs deh koh-LOHM-byah We are from Colombia.
C Characteristic (traits/qualities)
Are personality descriptions of a person. This category is included to hammer home the point that ser is used to talk about descriptions. 		Ella es muy inteligente. EH-yah ehs mwee een-teh-lee-HEHN-teh EH-yah ehs mwee een-teh-lee-HEHN-teh She is very intelligent.
T Time (date/day/time)
Can refer to days, dates, years, and the time on the clock. 		Son las tres. sohn lahs trehs sohn lahs trehs It's three o'clock.
O Occupation (job/profession)
What someone does for a living or as a hobby. Basically, if you're talking about how someone makes money or fills their time, you'll use ser. 		Soy profesor. soy proh-feh-SOHR soy proh-feh-SOHR I am a teacher.
R Relationship (family/friends)
Personal relationships, such as family ties, friendship, and romantic relationships, are also talked about using ser. 		Ella es mi hermana. EH-yah ehs mee ehr-MAH-nah EH-yah ehs mee ehr-MAH-nah She is my sister.

Estar — PLACE (Uses)

P.L.A.C.E. — Use estar ehs-TAHR for:

Letter Use Example Pronunciation English
P Position
Position refers to the physical position or posture a person or thing is in. 		El libro está en la mesa. ehl LEE-broh ehs-TAH ehn lah MEH-sah ehl LEE-broh ehs-TAH ehn lah MEH-sah The book is on the table.
L Location (where something is)
The location of someone or something describes where it is permanently, temporarily, or conceptually. 		Estamos en el parque. ehs-TAH-mohs ehn ehl PAHR-kay ehs-TAH-mohs ehn ehl PAHR-kay We are in the park.
A Action (progressive -ing)
Estar is used to describe ongoing actions and is often followed by a present participle (such as lavando or a past participle (such as muerto. 		Estamos comiendo. ehs-TAH-mohs koh-MYEHN-doh ehs-TAH-mohs koh-MYEHN-doh We are eating.
C Condition (physical/emotional state)
Physical and mental conditions are described using estar. Things that are likely to vary over several hours, days, or even years can fall into this category. 		Estoy feliz. ehs-TOY feh-LEES ehs-TOY feh-LEES I am happy.
E Emotion (feelings/moods)
How a person is feeling at a certain moment is described using estar. 		Está triste. ehs-TAH TREES-teh ehs-TAH TREES-teh He/she is sad.

Ser vs estar tips
  • Ser sehr = essence / identity / time / origin / traits / event location. — D.O.C.T.O.R.
  • Estar ehs-TAHR = location of people & things / feelings / conditions / progressive (-ing). — P.L.A.C.E.
  • ¿Cómo es? = What is he/she/it like? — ser sehr (Description — DOCTOR)
  • ¿Cómo está? = How is he/she (feeling)? — estar ehs-TAHR (Emotion — PLACE)
  • ¿De dónde es? = Where is he/she from? — ser sehr (Origin — DOCTOR)
  • ¿Dónde está? = Where is he/she/it (now)? — estar ehs-TAHR (Location — PLACE)
  • Adjective pairs can flip meaning: es listo ehs LEES-toh (smart) vs está listo ehs-TAH LEES-toh (ready).
  • Event place → ser sehr; person/thing place → estar ehs-TAHR.
  • Conjugations (present): soy/eres/es/somos/sois/son vs estoy/estás/está/estamos/estáis/están.
  • For weather temperature, Spanish often uses hacer ah-SEHR (hace frío AH-seh FREE-oh), not ser sehr/estar ehs-TAHR.
  • Progressive tense: estar ehs-TAHR + comiendo koh-MYEHN-doh (eating), hablando ah-BLAHN-doh (speaking). — A (PLACE)
  • Death: estar ehs-TAHR (state), not ser sehr. — C (PLACE)
  • Marital status: estar ehs-TAHR (state), not ser sehr. — C (PLACE)
