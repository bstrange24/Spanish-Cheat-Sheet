Gustar

To like (literally "to be pleasing") — plus encantar, doler, faltar, importar, and other reverse-construction verbs

The pattern (not "I like" word-for-word)

English I like coffee is Spanish Me gusta el café meh GOOS-tah ehl kah-FEH — literally "Coffee is pleasing to me."
Put an indirect object pronoun first, then gusta (one thing / infinitive) or gustan (plural things).

To whom Pronoun Pronunciation With gusta Pronunciation English
to me me meh Me gusta meh GOOS-tah I like
to you (informal) te teh Te gusta teh GOOS-tah you like
to him / her / you (formal) le leh Le gusta leh GOOS-tah he/she likes / you like
to us nos nohs Nos gusta nohs GOOS-tah we like
to you all (Spain informal) os ohs Os gusta ohs GOOS-tah you all like
to them / you all les lehs Les gusta lehs GOOS-tah they like / you all like
gusta vs gustan
When Form Example Pronunciation English
One noun gusta Me gusta el libro. meh GOOS-tah ehl LEE-broh I like the book.
Infinitive (an activity) gusta Me gusta bailar. meh GOOS-tah beye-LAHR I like to dance.
Plural nouns gustan Me gustan los libros. meh GOOS-tahn lohs LEE-brohs I like books.
Don't like no + gusta/n No me gusta el café. noh meh GOOS-tah ehl kah-FEH I don't like coffee.
Clarifying who likes it (a mí, a ti…)

Add a + prepositional pronoun for emphasis or to clear up le / les.

Clarifier Full phrase Pronunciation English
a mí A mí me gusta. ah MEE meh GOOS-tah I like it (as for me).
a ti A ti te gusta. ah TEE teh GOOS-tah You like it.
a él / a ella / a usted A ella le gusta. ah EH-yah leh GOOS-tah She likes it.
a nosotros A nosotros nos gusta. ah noh-SOH-trohs nohs GOOS-tah We like it.
a ellos / a ustedes A ustedes les gusta. ah oos-TEH-dehs lehs GOOS-tah You all like it.

Gustar tips
  • The verb agrees with the thing liked, not with "I/you."
  • Two activities still take gusta GOOS-tah: Me gusta cantar y bailar meh GOOS-tah kahn-TAHR ee bah-ee-LAHR.
  • Questions: ¿Te gusta? teh GOOS-tah — Do you like it?
  • Past: Me gustó meh goos-TOH (that one thing pleased me) / Me gustaban meh goos-TAH-bahn (I used to like them).
