Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Ir is irregular in the present tense.

Singular Plural
voy boy
(yo — I go)		 vamos BAH-mohs
(nosotros/as — we go / let's go)
vas bahs
(tú — you go)		 vais vice
(vosotros/as — you all go)
va bah
(él / ella / usted — he/she goes / you go)		 van bahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they go / you all go)
Example sentences:
  • Yo voy a la tienda. yoh boy ah lah TYEHN-dah — I am going to the store.
  • Tú vas al cine. too bahs ahl SEE-neh — You go to the movies.
  • Ella va a la escuela. EH-yah bah ah lah ehs-KWEH-lah — She goes to school.
  • Nosotros vamos a estudiar. noh-SOH-trohs BAH-mohs ah ehs-too-DYAHR — We are going to study / Let's study.
  • Ellos van a México. EH-yohs bahn ah MEH-hee-koh — They are going to Mexico.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Ir is irregular in the preterite (and identical to ser).

Singular Plural
fui fwee
(yo — I went)		 fuimos FWEE-mohs
(nosotros/as — we went)
fuiste FWEES-teh
(tú — you went)		 fuisteis FWEES-tays
(vosotros/as — you all went)
fue fweh
(él / ella / usted — he/she went / you went)		 fueron FWEH-rohn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they went / you all went)
Example sentences:
  • Yo fui a la tienda ayer. yoh fwee ah lah TYEHN-dah ah-YEHR — I went to the store yesterday.
  • Tú fuiste al cine el sábado. too FWEES-teh ahl SEE-neh ehl SAH-bah-doh — You went to the movies on Saturday.
  • Ella fue a la escuela a las ocho. EH-yah fweh ah lah ehs-KWEH-lah ah lahs OH-choh — She went to school at eight.
  • Nosotros fuimos a México el año pasado. noh-SOH-trohs FWEE-mohs ah MEH-hee-koh ehl AH-nyoh pah-SAH-doh — We went to Mexico last year.
  • Ellos fueron al parque. EH-yohs FWEH-rohn ahl PAHR-keh — They went to the park.
Imperfect Tense (Past — Ongoing / Habitual)

Used for past actions that were ongoing, habitual, or without a specific end. Ir is irregular in the imperfect.

Singular Plural
iba EE-bah
(yo — I used to go / I was going)		 íbamos EE-bah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we used to go)
ibas EE-bahs
(tú — you used to go)		 ibais EE-beyes
(vosotros/as — you all used to go)
iba EE-bah
(él / ella / usted — he/she used to go / you used to go)		 iban EE-bahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they used to go / you all used to go)
Example sentences:
  • Yo iba a la tienda todos los días. yoh EE-bah ah lah TYEHN-dah TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs — I used to go to the store every day.
  • Tú ibas al cine cada semana. too EE-bahs ahl SEE-neh KAH-dah seh-MAH-nah — You used to go to the movies every week.
  • Ella iba a la escuela cuando era joven. EH-yah EE-bah ah lah ehs-KWEH-lah KWAHN-doh EH-rah HOH-behn — She used to go to school when she was young.
  • Nosotros íbamos a México cada verano. noh-SOH-trohs EE-bah-mohs ah MEH-hee-koh KAH-dah beh-RAH-noh — We used to go to Mexico every summer.
  • Ellos iban al parque a jugar. EH-yohs EE-bahn ahl PAHR-keh ah hoo-GAHR — They used to go to the park to play.
Conditional Tense (Would — Hypothetical)

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Ir is regular in the conditional (infinitive + ending).

(tú — you would go)		 iríais ee-REE-eyes

(él / ella / usted — he/she would go / you would go)		 irían ee-REE-ahn

Example sentences:
  • Yo iría a la tienda si tuviera tiempo. yoh ee-REE-ah ah lah TYEHN-dah see too-BYEH-rah TYEHM-poh — I would go to the store if I had time.
  • Tú irías al cine conmigo. too ee-REE-ahs ahl SEE-neh kohn-MEE-goh — You would go to the movies with me.
  • Ella iría a la escuela pero está enferma. EH-yah ee-REE-ah ah lah ehs-KWEH-lah PEH-roh ehs-TAH ehn-FEHR-mah — She would go to school but she's sick.
  • Nosotros iríamos a México si pudiéramos. noh-SOH-trohs ee-REE-ah-mohs ah MEH-hee-koh see poo-DYEH-rah-mohs — We would go to Mexico if we could.
  • Ellos irían al parque si hiciera sol. EH-yohs ee-REE-ahn ahl PAHR-keh see ee-SYEH-rah sohl — They would go to the park if it were sunny.

Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + ido (past participle of ir).

Singular Plural
he ido eh EE-doh
(yo — I have gone)		 hemos ido EH-mohs EE-doh
(nosotros/as — we have gone)
has ido ahs EE-doh
(tú — you have gone)		 habéis ido ah-BEH-ees EE-doh
(vosotros/as — you all have gone)
ha ido ah EE-doh
(él / ella / usted — he/she has gone / you have gone)		 han ido ahn EE-doh
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have gone / you all have gone)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he ido a la tienda hoy. yoh eh EE-doh ah lah TYEHN-dah oy — I have gone to the store today.
  • ¿Has ido al cine alguna vez? ahs EE-doh ahl SEE-neh ahl-GOO-nah behs — Have you ever gone to the movies?
  • Ella ha ido a la escuela esta semana. EH-yah ah EE-doh ah lah ehs-KWEH-lah EHS-tah seh-MAH-nah — She has gone to school this week.
  • Nosotros hemos ido a México varias veces. noh-SOH-trohs EH-mohs EE-doh ah MEH-hee-koh BAH-ryahs BEH-sehs — We have gone to Mexico several times.
  • Ellos han ido al parque hoy. EH-yohs ahn EE-doh ahl PAHR-keh oy — They have gone to the park today.
Ir — Uses (to go)
Use Example Pronunciation English
Destination (ir + a) Voy a casa. boy ah KAH-sah I'm going home.
a + el = al Vamos al parque. BAH-mohs ahl PAHR-kay We're going to the park.
Informal future (ir a + infinitive) Voy a comer. boy ah koh-MEHR I'm going to eat.
Let's… (vamos + a) Vamos a estudiar. BAH-mohs ah ehs-too-DYAHR Let's study. / We're going to study.
How to get there (ir + en) Voy en autobús. boy ehn ow-toh-BOOS I go by bus.
How you are doing (¿cómo te va?) ¿Cómo te va? KOH-moh teh bah How's it going?
Ir tips
  • Preterite of ir eer and ser sehr is the same: fui fwee, fue fweh, fueron FWEH-rohn. Context tells you “went” vs “was.”
  • Use a ah for the place: voy a España boy ah ehs-PAH-nyah. Use en ehn for the vehicle: voy en tren boy ehn trehn.
  • Me voy. meh boy = I'm leaving.
  • See also Informal Future in Tenses for more ir a eer ah + infinitive.
Ir — Conjugation (to go)

Ir = to go
It is irregular in almost every tense. Use a ah + destination, and ir a eer ah + infinitive for “going to…” (informal future).
