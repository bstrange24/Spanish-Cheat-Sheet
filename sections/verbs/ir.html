Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Ir is irregular in the present tense.

Singular Plural
voy
(yo — I go)		 vamos
(nosotros/as — we go / let's go)
vas
(tú — you go)		 vais vice
(vosotros/as — you all go)
va
(él / ella / usted — he/she goes / you go)		 van
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they go / you all go)
Example sentences:
  • Yo voy a la tienda. 🔊 — I am going to the store.
  • Tú vas al cine. 🔊 — You go to the movies.
  • Ella va a la escuela. 🔊 — She goes to school.
  • Nosotros vamos a estudiar. 🔊 — We are going to study / Let's study.
  • Ellos van a México. 🔊 — They are going to Mexico.

  • Yo fui a la tienda ayer. 🔊 — I went to the store yesterday.

  • Ellos fueron al parque. 🔊 — They went to the park.

Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + ido (past participle of ir).

Singular Plural
he ido
(yo — I have gone)		 hemos ido
(nosotros/as — we have gone)
has ido
(tú — you have gone)		 habéis ido
(vosotros/as — you all have gone)
ha ido
(él / ella / usted — he/she has gone / you have gone)		 han ido
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have gone / you all have gone)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he ido a la tienda hoy. 🔊 — I have gone to the store today.
  • ¿Has ido al cine alguna vez? 🔊 — Have you ever gone to the movies?
  • Ella ha ido a la escuela esta semana. 🔊 — She has gone to school this week.
  • Nosotros hemos ido a México varias veces. 🔊 — We have gone to Mexico several times.
  • Ellos han ido al parque hoy. 🔊 — They have gone to the park today.
Ir — Uses (to go)
Use Example Pronunciation English
Destination (ir + a) Voy a casa. 🔊 boy ah KAH-sah I'm going home.
a + el = al Vamos al parque. 🔊 BAH-mohs ahl PAHR-kay We're going to the park.
Informal future (ir a + infinitive) Voy a comer. 🔊 boy ah koh-MEHR I'm going to eat.
Let's… (vamos + a) Vamos a estudiar. 🔊 BAH-mohs ah ehs-too-DYAHR Let's study. / We're going to study.
How to get there (ir + en) Voy en autobús. 🔊 boy ehn ow-toh-BOOS I go by bus.
How you are doing (¿cómo te va?) ¿Cómo te va? 🔊 KOH-moh teh bah How's it going?
Ir tips
  • Preterite of ir and ser is the same: fui, fue, fueron. Context tells you “went” vs “was.”
  • Use a for the place: voy a España. Use en for the vehicle: voy en tren.
  • Me voy. = I'm leaving.
  • See also Informal Future in Tenses for more ir a + infinitive.
Ir — Conjugation (to go)

Ir = to go
It is irregular in almost every tense. Use a + destination, and ir a + infinitive for “going to…” (informal future).
