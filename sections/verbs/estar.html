Estar

To be — temporary location, conditions, emotions, and actions in progress

Estar — Uses (to be — temporary - PLACE)

Estar is used for temporary states and conditions. Remember the acronym PLACE:

  • Position
  • Location
  • Action (progressive tenses)
  • Condition
  • Emotion
Use Example Pronunciation English
Position El libro está en la mesa. ehl LEE-broh ehs-TAH ehn lah MEH-sah The book is on the table.
Location Estoy en casa. ehs-TOY ehn KAH-sah I am at home.
Action (progressive) Estoy comiendo. ehs-TOY koh-MYEHN-doh I am eating.
Condition Está roto. ehs-TAH ROH-toh It's broken.
Emotion Estoy feliz. ehs-TOY feh-LEES I am happy.
More examples with estar
  • El perro está debajo de la cama. ehl PEH-rroh ehs-TAH deh-BAH-hoh deh lah KAH-mah — The dog is under the bed.
  • ¿Cómo estás? KOH-moh ehs-TAHS — How are you?
  • Estoy cansado. ehs-TOY kahn-SAH-doh — I am tired.
  • La puerta está abierta. lah PWEHR-tah ehs-TAH ah-BYEHR-tah — The door is open.

Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Estar is irregular in the present tense (yo form: estoy; accent on está, están).

Singular Plural
estoy ehs-TOY
(yo — I am)		 estamos ehs-TAH-mohs
(nosotros/as — we are)
estás ehs-TAHS
(tú — you are)		 estáis ehs-TEYES
(vosotros/as — you all are)
está ehs-TAH
(él / ella / usted — he/she is / you are)		 están ehs-TAHN
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they are / you all are)
Example sentences:
  • Yo estoy en casa. yoh ehs-TOY ehn KAH-sah — I am at home.
  • Tú estás muy feliz. too ehs-TAHS mwee feh-LEES — You are very happy.
  • Ella está enferma. EH-yah ehs-TAH ehn-FEHR-mah — She is sick.
  • Nosotros estamos en el parque. noh-SOH-trohs ehs-TAH-mohs ehn ehl PAHR-keh — We are in the park.
  • Ellos están estudiando. EH-yohs ehs-TAHN ehs-too-DYAHN-doh — They are studying.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Estar is irregular in the preterite (stem estuv-).

Singular Plural
estuve ehs-TOO-beh
(yo — I was)		 estuvimos ehs-too-BEE-mohs
(nosotros/as — we were)
estuviste ehs-too-BEES-teh
(tú — you were)		 estuvisteis ehs-too-BEES-tays
(vosotros/as — you all were)
estuvo ehs-TOO-boh
(él / ella / usted — he/she was / you were)		 estuvieron ehs-too-BYEH-rohn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they were / you all were)
Example sentences:
  • Yo estuve en casa ayer. yoh ehs-TOO-beh ehn KAH-sah ah-YEHR — I was at home yesterday.
  • Tú estuviste muy feliz en la fiesta. too ehs-too-BEES-teh mwee feh-LEES ehn lah FYEHS-tah — You were very happy at the party.
  • Ella estuvo enferma la semana pasada. EH-yah ehs-TOO-boh ehn-FEHR-mah lah seh-MAH-nah pah-SAH-dah — She was sick last week.
  • Nosotros estuvimos en el parque todo el día. noh-SOH-trohs ehs-too-BEE-mohs ehn ehl PAHR-keh TOH-doh ehl DEE-ah — We were in the park all day.
  • Ellos estuvieron estudiando hasta tarde. EH-yohs ehs-too-BYEH-rohn ehs-too-DYAHN-doh AHS-tah TAR-deh — They were studying until late.
Imperfect Tense (Past — Ongoing / Habitual)

Used for past actions that were ongoing, habitual, or without a specific end. Estar is regular in the imperfect.

Singular Plural
estaba ehs-TAH-bah
(yo — I used to be / I was being)		 estábamos ehs-TAH-bah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we used to be)
estabas ehs-TAH-bahs
(tú — you used to be)		 estabais ehs-TAH-beyes
(vosotros/as — you all used to be)
estaba ehs-TAH-bah
(él / ella / usted — he/she used to be / you used to be)		 estaban ehs-TAH-bahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they used to be / you all used to be)
Example sentences:
  • Yo estaba en casa cuando llegaste. yoh ehs-TAH-bah ehn KAH-sah KWAHN-doh yeh-GAHS-teh — I was at home when you arrived.
  • Tú estabas muy feliz en ese tiempo. too ehs-TAH-bahs mwee feh-LEES ehn EH-seh TYEHM-poh — You used to be very happy at that time.
  • Ella estaba enferma a menudo. EH-yah ehs-TAH-bah ehn-FEHR-mah ah meh-NOO-doh — She used to be sick often.
  • Nosotros estábamos en el parque cada domingo. noh-SOH-trohs ehs-TAH-bah-mohs ehn ehl PAHR-keh KAH-dah doh-MEEN-goh — We used to be in the park every Sunday.
  • Ellos estaban estudiando cuando los llamé. EH-yohs ehs-TAH-bahn ehs-too-DYAHN-doh KWAHN-doh lohs yah-MEH — They were studying when I called them.
Conditional Tense (Would — Hypothetical)

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Estar is regular in the conditional (infinitive + ending).

Singular Plural
estaría ehs-tah-REE-ah
(yo — I would be)		 estaríamos ehs-tah-REE-ah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we would be)
estarías ehs-tah-REE-ahs
(tú — you would be)		 estaríais ehs-tah-REE-eyes
(vosotros/as — you all would be)
estaría ehs-tah-REE-ah
(él / ella / usted — he/she would be / you would be)		 estarían ehs-tah-REE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they would be / you all would be)
Example sentences:
  • Yo estaría en casa ahora si no trabajara. yoh ehs-tah-REE-ah ehn KAH-sah ah-OH-rah see noh trah-bah-HAH-rah — I would be at home now if I didn't work.
  • Tú estarías más contento si vinieras. too ehs-tah-REE-ahs mahs kohn-TEHN-toh see bee-NYEH-rahs — You would be happier if you came.
  • Ella estaría en la fiesta si pudiera. EH-yah ehs-tah-REE-ah ehn lah FYEHS-tah see poo-DYEH-rah — She would be at the party if she could.
  • Nosotros estaríamos en la playa si hiciera sol. noh-SOH-trohs ehs-tah-REE-ah-mohs ehn lah PLAH-yah see ee-SYEH-rah sohl — We would be at the beach if it were sunny.
  • Ellos estarían más tranquilos si lo supieran. EH-yohs ehs-tah-REE-ahn mahs trahn-KEE-lohs see loh soo-PYEH-rahn — They would be calmer if they knew.
Future Tense (Will — What will happen)

Used to express what will happen. Estar is regular in the future (infinitive + ending).

Singular Plural
estaré ehs-tah-REH
(yo — I will be)		 estaremos ehs-tah-REH-mohs
(nosotros/as — we will be)
estarás ehs-tah-RAHS
(tú — you will be)		 estaréis ehs-tah-RAYS
(vosotros/as — you all will be)
estará ehs-tah-RAH
(él / ella / usted — he/she will be / you will be)		 estarán ehs-tah-RAHN
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they will be / you all will be)
Example sentences:
  • Yo estaré en casa mañana. yoh ehs-tah-REH ehn KAH-sah mah-NYAH-nah — I will be at home tomorrow.
  • Tú estarás muy feliz cuando lo veas. too ehs-tah-RAHS mwee feh-LEES KWAHN-doh loh BEH-ahs — You will be very happy when you see it.
  • Ella estará en la oficina a las nueve. EH-yah ehs-tah-RAH ehn lah oh-fee-SEE-nah ah lahs NWEH-beh — She will be in the office at nine.
  • Nosotros estaremos en el parque mañana. noh-SOH-trohs ehs-tah-REH-mohs ehn ehl PAHR-keh mah-NYAH-nah — We will be in the park tomorrow.
  • Ellos estarán estudiando toda la noche. EH-yohs ehs-tah-RAHN ehs-too-DYAHN-doh TOH-dah lah NOH-cheh — They will be studying all night.
Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + estado (past participle of estar).

Singular Plural
he estado eh ehs-TAH-doh
(yo — I have been)		 hemos estado EH-mohs ehs-TAH-doh
(nosotros/as — we have been)
has estado ahs ehs-TAH-doh
(tú — you have been)		 habéis estado ah-BEH-ees ehs-TAH-doh
(vosotros/as — you all have been)
ha estado ah ehs-TAH-doh
(él / ella / usted — he/she has been / you have been)		 han estado ahn ehs-TAH-doh
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have been / you all have been)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he estado en casa todo el día. yoh eh ehs-TAH-doh ehn KAH-sah TOH-doh ehl DEE-ah — I have been at home all day.
  • ¿Has estado alguna vez en París? ahs ehs-TAH-doh ahl-GOO-nah behs ehn pah-REES — Have you ever been to Paris?
  • Ella ha estado enferma esta semana. EH-yah ah ehs-TAH-doh ehn-FEHR-mah EHS-tah seh-MAH-nah — She has been sick this week.
  • Nosotros hemos estado muy ocupados. noh-SOH-trohs EH-mohs ehs-TAH-doh mwee oh-koo-PAH-dohs — We have been very busy.
  • Ellos han estado estudiando mucho. EH-yohs ahn ehs-TAH-doh ehs-too-DYAHN-doh MOO-choh — They have been studying a lot.
Estar tips
  • Use estar for temporary states and conditions.
  • Use for location (where something/someone is).
  • Use for feelings and emotions.
  • Use with present participle (-ando, -iendo) to form progressive tenses: estoy bailando ehs-TOY beye-LAHN-doh.
  • Use for physical appearance that is temporary (e.g., estás guapo hoy ehs-TAHS GWAH-poh oy — you look handsome today).
