Estar

To be — temporary location, conditions, emotions, and actions in progress

Estar — Uses (to be — temporary - PLACE)

Estar is used for temporary states and conditions. Remember the acronym PLACE:

  • Position
  • Location
  • Action (progressive tenses)
  • Condition
  • Emotion
Use Example Pronunciation English
Position El libro está en la mesa. 🔊 ehl LEE-broh ehs-TAH ehn lah MEH-sah The book is on the table.
Location Estoy en casa. 🔊 ehs-TOY ehn KAH-sah I am at home.
Action (progressive) Estoy comiendo. 🔊 ehs-TOY koh-MYEHN-doh I am eating.
Condition Está roto. 🔊 ehs-TAH ROH-toh It's broken.
Emotion Estoy feliz. 🔊 ehs-TOY feh-LEES I am happy.
More examples with estar
  • El perro está debajo de la cama. 🔊 — The dog is under the bed.
  • ¿Cómo estás? 🔊 — How are you?
  • Estoy cansado. 🔊 — I am tired.
  • La puerta está abierta. 🔊 — The door is open.

Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Estar is irregular in the present tense (yo form: estoy; accent on está, están).

Singular Plural
estoy
(yo — I am)		 estamos
(nosotros/as — we are)
estás
(tú — you are)		 estáis
(vosotros/as — you all are)
está
(él / ella / usted — he/she is / you are)		 están
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they are / you all are)
Example sentences:
  • Yo estoy en casa. 🔊 — I am at home.
  • Tú estás muy feliz. 🔊 — You are very happy.
  • Ella está enferma. 🔊 — She is sick.
  • Nosotros estamos en el parque. 🔊 — We are in the park.
  • Ellos están estudiando. 🔊 — They are studying.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Estar is irregular in the preterite (stem estuv-).

Singular Plural

(yo — I was)		 estuvimos
(nosotros/as — we were)
estuviste
(tú — you were)		 estuvisteis
(vosotros/as — you all were)
estuvo
(él / ella / usted — he/she was / you were)		 estuvieron
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they were / you all were)
Example sentences:
  • Yo estuve en casa ayer. 🔊 — I was at home yesterday.
  • Tú estuviste muy feliz en la fiesta. 🔊 — You were very happy at the party.
  • Ella estuvo enferma la semana pasada. 🔊 — She was sick last week.
  • Nosotros estuvimos en el parque todo el día. 🔊 — We were in the park all day.
  • Ellos estuvieron estudiando hasta tarde. 🔊 — They were studying until late.
Imperfect Tense (Past — Ongoing / Habitual)

Used for past actions that were ongoing, habitual, or without a specific end. Estar is regular in the imperfect.

Singular Plural
estaba
(yo — I used to be / I was being)		 estábamos
(nosotros/as — we used to be)
estabas
(tú — you used to be)		 estabais
(vosotros/as — you all used to be)
estaba
(él / ella / usted — he/she used to be / you used to be)		 estaban
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they used to be / you all used to be)
Example sentences:
  • Yo estaba en casa cuando llegaste. 🔊 — I was at home when you arrived.
  • Tú estabas muy feliz en ese tiempo. 🔊 — You used to be very happy at that time.
  • Ella estaba enferma a menudo. 🔊 — She used to be sick often.
  • Nosotros estábamos en el parque cada domingo. 🔊 — We used to be in the park every Sunday.
  • Ellos estaban estudiando cuando los llamé. 🔊 — They were studying when I called them.
Conditional Tense (Would — Hypothetical)

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Estar is regular in the conditional (infinitive + ending).

Singular Plural

(nosotros/as — we would be)

(tú — you would be)		 estaríais
(vosotros/as — you all would be)
estaría
(él / ella / usted — he/she would be / you would be)		 estarían
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they would be / you all would be)
Example sentences:
  • Yo estaría en casa ahora si no trabajara. 🔊 — I would be at home now if I didn't work.
  • Tú estarías más contento si vinieras. 🔊 — You would be happier if you came.
  • Ella estaría en la fiesta si pudiera. 🔊 — She would be at the party if she could.
  • Nosotros estaríamos en la playa si hiciera sol. 🔊 — We would be at the beach if it were sunny.
  • Ellos estarían más tranquilos si lo supieran. 🔊 — They would be calmer if they knew.

Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + estado (past participle of estar).

Singular Plural
he estado
(yo — I have been)		 hemos estado
(nosotros/as — we have been)
has estado
(tú — you have been)		 habéis estado
(vosotros/as — you all have been)
ha estado
(él / ella / usted — he/she has been / you have been)		 han estado
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have been / you all have been)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he estado en casa todo el día. 🔊 — I have been at home all day.
  • ¿Has estado alguna vez en París? 🔊 — Have you ever been to Paris?
  • Ella ha estado enferma esta semana. 🔊 — She has been sick this week.
  • Nosotros hemos estado muy ocupados. 🔊 — We have been very busy.
  • Ellos han estado estudiando mucho. 🔊 — They have been studying a lot.
Estar tips
  • Use estar for temporary states and conditions.
  • Use for location (where something/someone is).
  • Use for feelings and emotions.
  • Use with present participle (-ando, -iendo) to form progressive tenses: estoy bailando.
  • Use for physical appearance that is temporary (e.g., estás guapo hoy — you look handsome today).
