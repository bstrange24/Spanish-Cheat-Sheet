To be — permanent identity, origin, profession, characteristics, and time

Ser — Uses (to be — permanent / DOCTOR)

Ser is used for permanent or inherent characteristics. Remember the acronym DOCTOR:

  • Description
  • Occupation
  • Characteristic
  • Time (day, date, clock)
  • Origin
  • Relationship
Use Example Pronunciation English
Description La casa es grande. lah KAH-sah ehs GRAHN-deh 🔊 lah KAH-sah ehs GRAHN-deh The house is big.
Occupation Soy profesor. soy proh-feh-SOR 🔊 soy proh-feh-SOR I am a teacher.
Characteristic Eres inteligente. EH-rehs een-teh-lee-HEHN-teh 🔊 EH-rehs een-teh-lee-HEHN-teh You are intelligent.
Time (day, date, clock) Son las tres. sohn lahs trehs 🔊 sohn lahs trehs It's three o'clock.
Origin Soy de Argentina. soy deh ahr-hehn-TEE-nah 🔊 soy deh ahr-hehn-TEE-nah I am from Argentina.
Relationship Ella es mi hermana. EH-yah ehs mee ehr-MAH-nah 🔊 EH-yah ehs mee ehr-MAH-nah She is my sister.
More examples with ser
  • Hoy es lunes. oy ehs LOO-nehs 🔊 — Today is Monday.
  • Son las dos de la tarde. sohn lahs dohs deh lah TAR-deh 🔊 — It's two in the afternoon.
  • Somos de Colombia. SOH-mohs deh koh-LOHM-byah 🔊 — We are from Colombia.
  • ¿De quién es esto? deh KYEHN ehs EHS-toh 🔊 — Whose is this?

Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Ser is highly irregular in the present tense.

Singular Plural
soy soy
(yo — I am)		 somos SOH-mohs
(nosotros/as — we are)
eres EH-rehs
(tú — you are)		 sois so-ees
(vosotros/as — you all are)
es ehs
(él / ella / usted — he/she is / you are)		 son sohn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they are / you all are)
Example sentences:
  • Yo soy de México. yoh soy deh MEH-hee-koh 🔊 — I am from Mexico.
  • Tú eres médico. too EH-rehs MEH-dee-koh 🔊 — You are a doctor.
  • Ella es inteligente. EH-yah ehs een-teh-lee-HEHN-teh 🔊 — She is intelligent.
  • Nosotros somos estudiantes. noh-SOH-trohs SOH-mohs ehs-too-DYAHN-tehs 🔊 — We are students.
  • Ellos son mis amigos. EH-yohs sohn mees ah-MEE-gohs 🔊 — They are my friends.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Ser is irregular in the preterite (identical to ir — context tells you the meaning).

Singular Plural
fui fwee
(yo — I was)		 fuimos FWEE-mohs
(nosotros/as — we were)
fuiste FWEES-teh
(tú — you were)		 fuisteis FWEES-tays
(vosotros/as — you all were)
fue fweh
(él / ella / usted — he/she was / you were)		 fueron FWEH-rohn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they were / you all were)
Example sentences:
  • Yo fui el mejor estudiante. yoh fwee ehl meh-HOR ehs-too-DYAHN-teh 🔊 — I was the best student.
  • Tú fuiste muy amable. too FWEES-teh mwee ah-MAH-bleh 🔊 — You were very kind.
  • Ella fue la primera en llegar. EH-yah fweh lah pree-MEH-rah ehn yeh-GAHR 🔊 — She was the first to arrive.
  • Nosotros fuimos muy felices allí. noh-SOH-trohs FWEE-mohs mwee feh-LEE-sehs ah-YEE 🔊 — We were very happy there.
  • Ellos fueron los ganadores. EH-yohs FWEH-rohn lohs gah-nah-DOH-rehs 🔊 — They were the winners.
Imperfect Tense (Past — Ongoing / Habitual)

Used for past actions that were ongoing, habitual, or without a specific end. Ser is irregular in the imperfect.

Singular Plural

(yo — I used to be / I was)		 éramos EH-rah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we used to be)
eras EH-rahs
(tú — you used to be)		 erais EH-reyes
(vosotros/as — you all used to be)
era EH-rah
(él / ella / usted — he/she used to be / you used to be)		 eran EH-rahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they used to be / you all used to be)
Example sentences:
  • Yo era muy tímido cuando era niño. yoh EH-rah mwee TEE-mee-doh KWAHN-doh EH-rah NEE-nyoh 🔊 — I used to be very shy when I was a child.
  • Tú eras mi mejor amigo. too EH-rahs mee meh-HOR ah-MEE-goh 🔊 — You used to be my best friend.
  • Ella era muy simpática. EH-yah EH-rah mwee seem-PAH-tee-kah 🔊 — She used to be very nice.
  • Nosotros éramos vecinos. noh-SOH-trohs EH-rah-mohs beh-SEE-nohs 🔊 — We used to be neighbors.
  • Ellos eran muy buenos estudiantes. EH-yohs EH-rahn mwee BWEH-nohs ehs-too-DYAHN-tehs 🔊 — They used to be very good students.
Conditional Tense (Would — Hypothetical)

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Ser is regular in the conditional (infinitive + ending).

Singular Plural
sería seh-REE-ah
(yo — I would be)		 seríamos seh-REE-ah-mohs
(nosotros/as — we would be)
serías seh-REE-ahs
(tú — you would be)		 seríais seh-REE-eyes
(vosotros/as — you all would be)
sería seh-REE-ah
(él / ella / usted — he/she would be / you would be)		 serían seh-REE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they would be / you all would be)
Example sentences:
  • Yo sería más feliz si tuviera más tiempo. yoh seh-REE-ah mahs feh-LEES see too-BYEH-rah mahs TYEHM-poh 🔊 — I would be happier if I had more time.
  • Tú serías un buen profesor. too seh-REE-ahs oon BWEHN proh-feh-SOHR 🔊 — You would be a good teacher.
  • Ella sería la primera en saberlo. EH-yah seh-REE-ah lah pree-MEH-rah ehn sah-BEHR-loh 🔊 — She would be the first to know.
  • Nosotros seríamos más productivos. noh-SOH-trohs seh-REE-ah-mohs mahs proh-doos-TEE-bohs 🔊 — We would be more productive.
  • Ellos serían excelentes candidatos. EH-yohs seh-REE-ahn ehks-seh-LEHN-tehs kahn-dee-DAH-tohs 🔊 — They would be excellent candidates.
Future Tense (Will — What will happen)

Used to express what will happen. Ser is regular in the future (infinitive + ending).

Singular Plural

(yo — I will be)		 seremos seh-REH-mohs
(nosotros/as — we will be)
serás seh-RAHS
(tú — you will be)		 seréis seh-RAYS
(vosotros/as — you all will be)
será seh-RAH
(él / ella / usted — he/she will be / you will be)		 serán seh-RAHN
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they will be / you all will be)
Example sentences:
  • Yo seré médico en el futuro. yoh seh-REH MEH-dee-koh ehn ehl foo-TOO-roh 🔊 — I will be a doctor in the future.
  • Tú serás un gran artista. too seh-RAHS oon GRAHN ahr-TEES-tah 🔊 — You will be a great artist.
  • Ella será la directora de la empresa. EH-yah seh-RAH lah dee-rehs-TOH-rah deh lah ehm-PREH-sah 🔊 — She will be the director of the company.
  • Nosotros seremos los anfitriones. noh-SOH-trohs seh-REH-mohs lohs ahn-fee-TRYOH-nehs 🔊 — We will be the hosts.
  • Ellos serán los encargados del proyecto. EH-yohs seh-RAHN lohs ehn-kahr-GAH-dohs dehl proh-YEHS-toh 🔊 — They will be the ones in charge of the project.
Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + sido (past participle of ser).

Singular Plural
he sido eh SEE-doh
(yo — I have been)		 hemos sido EH-mohs SEE-doh
(nosotros/as — we have been)
has sido ahs SEE-doh
(tú — you have been)		 habéis sido ah-BEH-ees SEE-doh
(vosotros/as — you all have been)
ha sido ah SEE-doh
(él / ella / usted — he/she has been / you have been)		 han sido ahn SEE-doh
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have been / you all have been)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he sido estudiante toda mi vida. yoh eh SEE-doh ehs-too-DYAHN-teh TOH-dah mee BEE-dah 🔊 — I have been a student my whole life.
  • ¿Has sido alguna vez a España? ahs SEE-doh ahl-GOO-nah behs ah ehs-PAH-nyah 🔊 — Have you ever been to Spain? (note: uses estar for location)
  • Ella ha sido muy amable conmigo. EH-yah ah SEE-doh mwee ah-MAH-bleh kohn-MEE-goh 🔊 — She has been very kind to me.
  • Nosotros hemos sido vecinos por años. noh-SOH-trohs EH-mohs SEE-doh beh-SEE-nohs por AH-nyohs 🔊 — We have been neighbors for years.
  • Ellos han sido los mejores empleados. EH-yohs ahn SEE-doh lohs meh-HOH-rehs ehm-pleh-AH-dohs 🔊 — They have been the best employees.
Ser tips
  • Use ser for inherent or permanent qualities.
  • Use for time, date, and day of the week.
  • Use for origin, nationality, and profession.
  • Use for relationship (family, friends).
  • Preterite of ser sehr is identical to ir eer: fui fwee, fuiste FWEES-teh, fue fweh, etc. Context tells you which one.
