Ser

To be — permanent identity, origin, profession, characteristics, and time

Ser — Uses (to be — permanent / DOCTOR)

Ser is used for permanent or inherent characteristics. Remember the acronym DOCTOR:

  • Description
  • Occupation
  • Characteristic
  • Time (day, date, clock)
  • Origin
  • Relationship
Use Example Pronunciation English
Description La casa es grande. 🔊 lah KAH-sah ehs GRAHN-deh The house is big.
Occupation Soy profesor. 🔊 soy proh-feh-SOR I am a teacher.
Characteristic Eres inteligente. 🔊 EH-rehs een-teh-lee-HEHN-teh You are intelligent.
Time (day, date, clock) Son las tres. 🔊 sohn lahs trehs It's three o'clock.
Origin Soy de Argentina. 🔊 soy deh ahr-hehn-TEE-nah I am from Argentina.
Relationship Ella es mi hermana. 🔊 EH-yah ehs mee ehr-MAH-nah She is my sister.
More examples with ser
  • Hoy es lunes. 🔊 — Today is Monday.
  • Son las dos de la tarde. 🔊 — It's two in the afternoon.
  • Somos de Colombia. 🔊 — We are from Colombia.
  • ¿De quién es esto? 🔊 — Whose is this?

Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Ser is highly irregular in the present tense.

Singular Plural
soy
(yo — I am)		 somos
(nosotros/as — we are)
eres
(tú — you are)		 sois
(vosotros/as — you all are)
es
(él / ella / usted — he/she is / you are)		 son
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they are / you all are)
Example sentences:
  • Yo soy de México. 🔊 — I am from Mexico.
  • Tú eres médico. 🔊 — You are a doctor.
  • Ella es inteligente. 🔊 — She is intelligent.
  • Nosotros somos estudiantes. 🔊 — We are students.
  • Ellos son mis amigos. 🔊 — They are my friends.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Ser is irregular in the preterite (identical to ir — context tells you the meaning).

Singular Plural

(yo — I was)		 fuimos
(nosotros/as — we were)

(tú — you were)		 fuisteis
(vosotros/as — you all were)
fue
(él / ella / usted — he/she was / you were)		 fueron
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they were / you all were)
Example sentences:
  • Yo fui el mejor estudiante. 🔊 — I was the best student.
  • Tú fuiste muy amable. 🔊 — You were very kind.
  • Ella fue la primera en llegar. 🔊 — She was the first to arrive.
  • Nosotros fuimos muy felices allí. 🔊 — We were very happy there.
  • Ellos fueron los ganadores. 🔊 — They were the winners.

Used for past actions that were ongoing, habitual, or without a specific end. Ser is irregular in the imperfect.

(él / ella / usted — he/she used to be / you used to be)		 eran

Example sentences:
  • Yo era muy tímido cuando era niño. 🔊 — I used to be very shy when I was a child.
  • Tú eras mi mejor amigo. 🔊 — You used to be my best friend.
  • Ella era muy simpática. 🔊 — She used to be very nice.
  • Nosotros éramos vecinos. 🔊 — We used to be neighbors.
  • Ellos eran muy buenos estudiantes. 🔊 — They used to be very good students.

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Ser is regular in the conditional (infinitive + ending).

Singular Plural

(nosotros/as — we would be)

(vosotros/as — you all would be)
sería
(él / ella / usted — he/she would be / you would be)		 serían
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they would be / you all would be)
Example sentences:
  • Yo sería más feliz si tuviera más tiempo. 🔊 — I would be happier if I had more time.
  • Tú serías un buen profesor. 🔊 — You would be a good teacher.
  • Ella sería la primera en saberlo. 🔊 — She would be the first to know.
  • Nosotros seríamos más productivos. 🔊 — We would be more productive.
  • Ellos serían excelentes candidatos. 🔊 — They would be excellent candidates.

Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + sido (past participle of ser).

Singular Plural
he sido
(yo — I have been)		 hemos sido
(nosotros/as — we have been)
has sido
(tú — you have been)		 habéis sido
(vosotros/as — you all have been)
ha sido
(él / ella / usted — he/she has been / you have been)		 han sido
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have been / you all have been)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he sido estudiante toda mi vida. 🔊 — I have been a student my whole life.
  • ¿Has sido alguna vez a España? 🔊 — Have you ever been to Spain? (note: uses estar for location)
  • Ella ha sido muy amable conmigo. 🔊 — She has been very kind to me.
  • Nosotros hemos sido vecinos por años. 🔊 — We have been neighbors for years.
  • Ellos han sido los mejores empleados. 🔊 — They have been the best employees.
Ser tips
  • Use ser for inherent or permanent qualities.
  • Use for time, date, and day of the week.
  • Use for origin, nationality, and profession.
  • Use for relationship (family, friends).
  • Preterite of ser is identical to ir: fui, fuiste, fue, etc. Context tells you which one.
