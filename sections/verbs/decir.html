Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Decir is irregular in the present tense (yo form: digo; stem change e → i in all forms except nosotros and vosotros).

Singular Plural
digo DEE-goh
(yo — I say / I tell)		 decimos deh-SEE-mohs
(nosotros/as — we say / we tell)
dices DEE-sehs
(tú — you say / you tell)		 decís deh-SEES
(vosotros/as — you all say / you all tell)
dice DEE-seh
(él / ella / usted — he/she says / you say)		 dicen DEE-sehn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they say / you all say)
Example sentences:
  • Yo digo la verdad. yoh DEE-goh lah behr-DAHD 🔊 — I tell the truth.
  • Tú dices que quieres ir. too DEE-sehs keh KYEH-rehs eer 🔊 — You say that you want to go.
  • Ella dice su nombre. EH-yah DEE-seh soo NOHM-breh 🔊 — She says her name.
  • Nosotros decimos siempre la verdad. noh-SOH-trohs deh-SEE-mohs SYEHM-preh lah behr-DAHD 🔊 — We always tell the truth.
  • Ellos dicen que van a llegar tarde. EH-yohs DEE-sehn keh bahn ah yeh-GAHR TAR-deh 🔊 — They say that they are going to arrive late.
Preterite Tense (Past — Completed Actions)

Used for actions completed in the past at a specific time. Decir is irregular in the preterite (stem dij-). Note that dij- forms have no accent mark on the yo/usted/ellos forms.

Singular Plural
dije DEE-heh
(yo — I said / I told)		 dijimos dee-HEE-mohs
(nosotros/as — we said / we told)
dijiste dee-HEES-teh
(tú — you said / you told)		 dijisteis dee-HEES-tays
(vosotros/as — you all said / you all told)
dijo DEE-hoh
(él / ella / usted — he/she said / you said)		 dijeron dee-HEH-rohn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they said / you all said)
Example sentences:
  • Yo dije la verdad ayer. yoh DEE-heh lah behr-DAHD ah-YEHR 🔊 — I told the truth yesterday.
  • Tú dijiste que vendrías. too dee-HEES-teh keh behn-DREE-ahs 🔊 — You said that you would come.
  • Ella dijo su nombre en la entrevista. EH-yah DEE-hoh soo NOHM-breh ehn lah ehn-treh-BEES-tah 🔊 — She said her name in the interview.
  • Nosotros dijimos la verdad a la policía. noh-SOH-trohs dee-HEE-mohs lah behr-DAHD ah lah poh-lee-SEE-ah 🔊 — We told the truth to the police.
  • Ellos dijeron que no querían venir. EH-yohs dee-HEH-rohn keh noh keh-REE-ahn beh-NEER 🔊 — They said that they didn't want to come.
Imperfect Tense (Past — Ongoing / Habitual)

Used for past actions that were ongoing, habitual, or without a specific end. Decir is regular in the imperfect.

(él / ella / usted — he/she used to say / you used to say)		 decían deh-SEE-ahn
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they used to say / you all used to say)
Example sentences:
  • Yo decía la verdad siempre. yoh deh-SEE-ah lah behr-DAHD SYEHM-preh 🔊 — I used to always tell the truth.
  • Tú decías que querías viajar. too deh-SEE-ahs keh keh-REE-ahs byah-HAHR 🔊 — You used to say that you wanted to travel.
  • Ella decía su nombre cuando la llamaban. EH-yah deh-SEE-ah soo NOHM-breh KWAHN-doh lah yah-MAH-bahn 🔊 — She used to say her name when they called her.
  • Nosotros decíamos siempre lo mismo. noh-SOH-trohs deh-SEE-ah-mohs SYEHM-preh loh MEES-moh 🔊 — We used to always say the same thing.
  • Ellos decían que no les importaba. EH-yohs deh-SEE-ahn keh noh lehs eem-pohr-TAH-bah 🔊 — They used to say that they didn't care.

Used to express what would happen or hypothetical situations. Decir is irregular in the conditional (stem dir-).

(él / ella / usted — he/she would say / you would say)		 dirían dee-REE-ahn

Example sentences:
  • Yo diría que es una buena idea. yoh dee-REE-ah keh ehs OO-nah BWEH-nah ee-DEH-ah 🔊 — I would say that it's a good idea.
  • Tú dirías la verdad si te preguntaran. too dee-REE-ahs lah behr-DAHD see teh preh-goon-TAH-rahn 🔊 — You would tell the truth if they asked you.
  • Ella diría que sí si la invitaras. EH-yah dee-REE-ah keh see see lah een-bee-TAH-rahs 🔊 — She would say yes if you invited her.
  • Nosotros diríamos lo mismo en tu lugar. noh-SOH-trohs dee-REE-ah-mohs loh MEES-moh ehn too loo-GAHR 🔊 — We would say the same in your place.
  • Ellos dirían que no tienen dinero. EH-yohs dee-REE-ahn keh noh TYEH-nehn dee-NEH-roh 🔊 — They would say that they don't have money.

  • Tú dirás lo que piensas. too dee-RAHS loh keh PYEHN-sahs 🔊 — You will say what you think.

Present Perfect Tense (Past — Recent / Relevant)

Used for past actions that have relevance to the present or have happened recently. Formed with haber + dicho (irregular past participle of decir).

Singular Plural

(yo — I have said / I have told)		 hemos dicho EH-mohs DEE-choh
(nosotros/as — we have said / we have told)
has dicho ahs DEE-choh
(tú — you have said / you have told)		 habéis dicho ah-BEH-ees DEE-choh
(vosotros/as — you all have said / you all have told)

(él / ella / usted — he/she has said / you have said)		 han dicho ahn DEE-choh
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they have said / you all have said)
Example sentences:
  • Yo he dicho la verdad siempre. yoh eh DEE-choh lah behr-DAHD SYEHM-preh 🔊 — I have always told the truth.
  • ¿Has dicho algo? ahs DEE-choh AHL-goh 🔊 — Have you said something?
  • Ella ha dicho que no puede venir. EH-yah ah DEE-choh keh noh PWEH-deh beh-NEER 🔊 — She has said that she can't come.
  • Nosotros hemos dicho todo lo que sabemos. noh-SOH-trohs EH-mohs DEE-choh TOH-doh loh keh sah-BEH-mohs 🔊 — We have said everything we know.
  • Ellos han dicho que lo harán. EH-yohs ahn DEE-choh keh loh ah-RAHN 🔊 — They have said that they will do it.
Decir — Uses (to say / to tell)

Decir is used for:

Use Example Pronunciation English
Say something ¿Qué dices? kay DEE-sehs 🔊 kay DEE-sehs What are you saying?
Tell someone (indirect object) Dile la verdad. DEE-leh lah behr-DAHD 🔊 DEE-leh lah behr-DAHD Tell him/her the truth.
Say that… Dice que viene. DEE-seh kay BYEH-neh 🔊 DEE-seh kay BYEH-neh He/she says he/she is coming.
How do you say…? ¿Cómo se dice? KOH-moh seh DEE-seh 🔊 KOH-moh seh DEE-seh How do you say it?
I mean / that is Es decir… ehs deh-SEER 🔊 ehs deh-SEER That is to say…
More examples with decir
  • ¿Qué dijiste? keh dee-HEES-teh 🔊 — What did you say?
  • Me dijo que llegaría tarde. meh DEE-hoh keh yeh-gah-REE-ah TAR-deh 🔊 — He told me that he would arrive late.
  • ¡Dime la verdad! DEE-meh lah behr-DAHD 🔊 — Tell me the truth!
  • Se dice que el restaurante es bueno. seh DEE-seh keh ehl rehs-tow-RAHN-teh ehs BWEH-noh 🔊 — They say the restaurant is good.
  • Es decir, no tenemos tiempo. ehs deh-SEER noh teh-NEH-mohs TYEHM-poh 🔊 — That is, we don't have time.
Decir tips
  • Tell someone: me meh / te teh / le leh + decir: Te dije teh DEE-heh.
  • Command: di dee (tú), diga DEE-gah (usted), digan DEE-gahn (ustedes).
  • Pronouns attach to the infinitive: quiero decírtelo KYEH-roh deh-SEER-teh-loh — I want to tell it to you.
  • Past participle dicho DEE-choh is irregular — don't say *decido.
  • In the preterite, the stem dij- deeh has no accent mark: dijo DEE-hoh, dijeron dee-HEH-rohn.
