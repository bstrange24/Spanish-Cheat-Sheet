Present Tense (Now — Current actions)

Used for actions happening right now. Decir is irregular in the present tense (yo form: digo; stem change e → i in all forms except nosotros and vosotros).

Singular Plural
digo
(yo — I say / I tell)		 decimos
(nosotros/as — we say / we tell)
dices
(tú — you say / you tell)		 decís
(vosotros/as — you all say / you all tell)
dice
(él / ella / usted — he/she says / you say)		 dicen
(ellos / ellas / ustedes — they say / you all say)
Example sentences:
  • Yo digo la verdad. 🔊 — I tell the truth.
  • Tú dices que quieres ir. 🔊 — You say that you want to go.
  • Ella dice su nombre. 🔊 — She says her name.
  • Nosotros decimos siempre la verdad. 🔊 — We always tell the truth.
  • Ellos dicen que van a llegar tarde. 🔊 — They say that they are going to arrive late.

Decir — Uses (to say / to tell)

Decir is used for:

Use Example Pronunciation English
Say something ¿Qué dices? 🔊 kay DEE-sehs What are you saying?
Tell someone (indirect object) Dile la verdad. 🔊 DEE-leh lah behr-DAHD Tell him/her the truth.
Say that… Dice que viene. 🔊 DEE-seh kay BYEH-neh He/she says he/she is coming.
How do you say…? ¿Cómo se dice? 🔊 KOH-moh seh DEE-seh How do you say it?
I mean / that is Es decir… 🔊 ehs deh-SEER That is to say…
More examples with decir
  • ¿Qué dijiste? 🔊 — What did you say?
  • Me dijo que llegaría tarde. 🔊 — He told me that he would arrive late.
  • ¡Dime la verdad! 🔊 — Tell me the truth!
  • Se dice que el restaurante es bueno. 🔊 — They say the restaurant is good.
  • Es decir, no tenemos tiempo. 🔊 — That is, we don't have time.
Decir tips
  • Tell someone: me / te / le + decir: Te dije.
  • Command: di (tú), diga (usted), digan (ustedes).
  • Pronouns attach to the infinitive: quiero decírtelo — I want to tell it to you.
  • Past participle dicho is irregular — don't say *decido.
  • In the preterite, the stem dij- has no accent mark: dijo, dijeron.
