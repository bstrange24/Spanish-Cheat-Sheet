Diminutives

Smaller, cuter, or softer meanings with endings like -ito / -ita

The usual ending: -ito / -ita

Add -ito (masculine) or -ita (feminine). It can mean small, affectionate, or "just a little."

Base Diminutive Pronunciation English
casa casita kah-SEE-tah little house
perro perrito peh-RREE-toh little dog / puppy (affection)
momento momentito moh-mehn-TEE-toh just a moment
ahora ahorita ah-oh-REE-tah right now / in a bit (very LatAm)
poco poquito poh-KEE-toh a little bit
gato gatito gah-TEE-toh kitten / kitty
Example sentences:
  • Vivo en una casita. BEE-boh ehn OO-nah kah-SEE-tah
    I live in a little house.
  • Mira el perrito. MEE-rah ehl peh-RREE-toh
    Look at the little dog.
  • Un momentito, por favor. oon moh-mehn-TEE-toh pohr fah-BOHR
    Just a moment, please.
  • Ahorita te llamo. ah-oh-REE-tah teh YAH-moh
    I'll call you in a bit. (LatAm)
  • Solo un poquito. SOH-loh oon poh-KEE-toh
    Just a little bit.
  • El gatito es muy lindo. ehl gah-TEE-toh ehs mwee LEEN-doh
    The kitten is very cute.
Spelling tweaks
If the word… Use Example Pronunciation
ends in a vowel drop the vowel, add -ito/-ita hermano → hermanito ehr-mah-NEE-toh
ends in -n, -r, -e often -cito / -cita rincón → rinconcito, café → cafecito reen-kohn-SEE-toh, kah-feh-SEE-toh
ends in -z -c + ito pez → pecito peh-SEE-toh (LatAm) / peh-THEE-toh (Spain)
ends in -co / -go keep a hard sound: -quito / -guito poco → poquito, amigo → amiguito poh-KEE-toh, ah-mee-GEE-toh
Example sentences:
  • Mi hermanito es pequeño. mee ehr-mah-NEE-toh ehs peh-KAY-nyoh
    My little brother is small.
  • Siéntate en el rinconcito. SYEHN-tah-teh ehn ehl reen-kohn-SEE-toh
    Sit in the little corner.
  • ¿Quieres un cafecito? KYEH-rehs oon kah-feh-SEE-toh
    Do you want a little coffee?
  • El pecito nada. ehl peh-SEE-toh NAH-dah (LatAm) / ...peh-THEE-toh (Spain)
    The little fish swims.
  • Solo un poquito de azúcar. SOH-loh oon poh-KEE-toh deh ah-SOO-kahr
    Just a little bit of sugar.
  • Mi amiguito viene hoy. mee ah-mee-GEE-toh BYEH-neh oy
    My little friend comes today.
Other flavors
Ending Feel Example English
-illo / -illa small, sometimes dismissive chiquillo, mesilla kid, little table / nightstand
-ón / -ona (augmentative) big, or intense un caserón, un soltón a huge house, a big single guy
-ote / -ota big / clumsy (varies by country) amigote pal (informal)
Example sentences:
  • Ese chiquillo corre mucho. EH-seh chee-KEE-yoh KOH-rreh MOO-choh
    That kid runs a lot.
  • Puse las llaves en la mesilla. POO-seh lahs YAH-behs ehn lah meh-SEE-yah
    I put the keys on the nightstand.
  • Viven en un caserón. BEE-behn ehn oon kah-seh-ROHN
    They live in a huge house.
  • Es un soltón. ehs oon sohl-TOHN
    He's a big single guy.
  • Voy con mi amigote. boy kohn mee ah-mee-GOH-teh
    I'm going with my pal.
Diminutive tips
  • Not always "small": un momentito oon moh-mehn-TEE-toh is politeness, not a shorter minute.
  • ahorita ah-oh-REE-tah can mean "right now" or "in a little while," depending on the country.
  • Match gender/number: casita kah-SEE-tah, perritos peh-RRHEE-tohs.
