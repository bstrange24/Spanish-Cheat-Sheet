Diminutives

Smaller, cuter, or softer meanings with endings like -ito / -ita

The usual ending: -ito / -ita

Add -ito (masculine) or -ita (feminine). It can mean small, affectionate, or "just a little."

Base Diminutive Pronunciation English idea
casa casita kah-SEE-tah little house
perro perrito peh-RREE-toh little dog / puppy (affection)
momento momentito moh-mehn-TEE-toh just a moment
ahora ahorita ah-oh-REE-tah right now / in a bit (very LatAm)
poco poquito poh-KEE-toh a little bit
gato gatito gah-TEE-toh kitten / kitty
Spelling tweaks
If the word… Use Example Pronunciation
ends in a vowel drop the vowel, add -ito/-ita hermano → hermanito ehr-mah-NEE-toh
ends in -n, -r, -e often -cito / -cita rincón → rinconcito, café → cafecito reen-kohn-SEE-toh, kah-feh-SEE-toh
ends in -z -c + ito pez → pecito peh-SEE-toh (LatAm) / peh-THEE-toh (Spain)
ends in -co / -go keep a hard sound: -quito / -guito poco → poquito, amigo → amiguito poh-KEE-toh, ah-mee-GEE-toh
Other flavors
Ending Feel Example English
-illo / -illa small, sometimes dismissive chiquillo, mesilla kid, little table / nightstand
-ón / -ona (augmentative) big, or intense un caserón, un soltón a huge house, a big single guy
-ote / -ota big / clumsy (varies by country) amigote pal (informal)
Diminutive tips
  • Not always "small": un momentito oon moh-mehn-TEE-toh is politeness, not a shorter minute.
  • ahorita ah-oh-REE-tah can mean "right now" or "in a little while," depending on the country.
  • Match gender/number: casita kah-SEE-tah, perritos peh-RRHEE-tohs.
