Negation

Saying no, never, nothing, nobody — and double negatives

no before the verb

Put no noh immediately before the conjugated verb.

Spanish Pronunciation English
No hablo francés. noh AH-bloh frahn-SEHS 🔊 noh AH-bloh frahn-SEHS (LatAm) / frahn-THEHS (Spain) I don't speak French.
No estoy cansado. noh ehs-TOY kahn-SAH-doh 🔊 noh ehs-TOY kahn-SAH-doh I'm not tired.
No he comido. noh eh koh-MEE-doh 🔊 noh eh koh-MEE-doh I haven't eaten.
No quiero ir. noh KYEH-roh eer 🔊 noh KYEH-roh eer I don't want to go.
No, gracias. noh GRAH-syahs 🔊 noh GRAH-syahs (LatAm) / GRAH-thyahs (Spain) No, thank you.

Object pronouns stay attached to the verb group: No lo veo. noh loh BEH-oh 🔊 — I don't see it.
No me gusta. noh meh GOOS-tah 🔊 — I don't like it.

Double negation

Spanish requires a second negative after the verb. No veo nada noh BEH-oh NAH-dah is correct — not “I don't see nothing” as an error.

Spanish Pronunciation English
No veo nada. noh BEH-oh NAH-dah 🔊 noh BEH-oh NAH-dah I don't see anything.
No conozco a nadie. noh koh-NOHS-koh ah NAH-dyeh 🔊 noh koh-NOHS-koh ah NAH-dyeh (LatAm) / koh-NOHTH-koh (Spain) I don't know anybody.
No voy nunca. noh boy NOON-kah 🔊 noh boy NOON-kah I never go.
No tengo ningún problema. noh TEHN-goh neen-GOON proh-BLEH-mah 🔊 noh TEHN-goh neen-GOON proh-BLEH-mah I don't have any problem.
No me gusta tampoco. noh meh GOOS-tah tahm-POH-koh 🔊 noh meh GOOS-tah tahm-POH-koh I don't like it either.

You can stack them: No le digo nunca nada a nadie. noh leh DEE-goh NOON-kah NAH-dah ah NAH-dyeh 🔊 — I never tell anybody anything.

Placement

If the negative word is before the verb, drop extra no noh.
If it is after the verb, keep no noh.

Negative first (no extra no noh) Verb first (need no noh) English
Nunca voy. NOON-kah boy 🔊 No voy nunca. noh boy NOON-kah 🔊 I never go.
Nadie vino. NAH-dyeh BEE-noh 🔊 No vino nadie. noh BEE-noh NAH-dyeh 🔊 Nobody came.
Nada pasó. NAH-dah pah-SOH 🔊 No pasó nada. noh pah-SOH NAH-dah 🔊 Nothing happened.
Ninguno funciona. neen-GOO-noh foon-SYOH-nah 🔊 No funciona ninguno. noh foon-SYOH-nah neen-GOO-noh 🔊 None of them work.
Tampoco quiero café. tahm-POH-koh KYEH-roh kah-FEH 🔊 No quiero café tampoco. noh KYEH-roh kah-FEH tahm-POH-koh 🔊 I don't want coffee either.

Ya no yah noh and todavía no toh-dah-BEE-ah noh keep no noh next to the verb: Ya no trabajo. yah noh trah-BAH-hoh 🔊 / Todavía no termino. toh-dah-BEE-ah noh tehr-MEE-noh 🔊

ningún / ninguno / ninguna
Form When Example English
ningún neen-GOON before a masculine singular noun ningún libro neen-GOON LEE-broh no book / not any book
ninguno neen-GOO-noh pronoun (stands alone, m.) No quiero ninguno. noh KYEH-roh neen-GOO-noh 🔊 I don't want any (of them).
ninguna neen-GOO-nah feminine singular (noun or pronoun) ninguna casa neen-GOO-nah KAH-sah no house
ningunos neen-GOO-nohs / ningunas neen-GOO-nahs rare; usually stay singular No tengo ninguno. noh TEHN-goh neen-GOO-noh 🔊 I don't have any.

Positive “some / someone / something”: alguien AHL-hyehn, algo AHL-goh, algún ahl-GOON / alguno ahl-GOO-noh. Don't mix them with no noh in the same way: not no veo algo → No veo nada. noh BEH-oh NAH-dah 🔊

Negation tips
  • Default: no noh right before the verb: No sé. noh seh 🔊
  • After the verb, Spanish loves a second negative: No veo nada. noh BEH-oh NAH-dah 🔊
  • Negative word first → no extra no noh: Nunca voy. NOON-kah boy 🔊
  • Nadie NAH-dyeh as a direct object still needs personal a ah: No veo a nadie. noh BEH-oh ah NAH-dyeh 🔊
  • ningún neen-GOON before masc. noun; ninguno neen-GOO-noh alone.
  • ya no yah noh = no longer; todavía no toh-dah-BEE-ah noh = not yet; tampoco tahm-POH-koh = neither.
  • English “I don't see anything” = Spanish “I don't see nothing.” That's the rule, not a mistake.
