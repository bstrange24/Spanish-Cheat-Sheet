Passive / Se

How Spanish says "it is done" or "people do X" with se

Se + 3rd person (the most common "passive")

Use se + a 3rd-person verb. The verb agrees with the thing, not with a person doing it.

Spanish Pronunciation English
Se habla español. seh AH-blah ehs-pah-NYOHL Spanish is spoken. / They speak Spanish.
Se vende una casa. seh BEHN-deh OO-nah KAH-sah A house is for sale. / House for sale.
Se venden casas. seh BEHN-dehn KAH-sahs Houses are for sale.
Se come bien aquí. seh KOH-meh byehn ah-KEE You eat well here. / The food is good here.
¿Cómo se dice…? KOH-moh seh DEE-seh How do you say…?
Example sentences:
  • Se habla español. seh AH-blah ehs-pah-NYOHL
    Spanish is spoken.
  • Se vende una casa. seh BEHN-deh OO-nah KAH-sah
    A house is for sale.
  • Se venden casas. seh BEHN-dehn KAH-sahs
    Houses are for sale.
  • Se come bien aquí. seh KOH-meh byehn ah-KEE
    You eat well here.
  • ¿Cómo se dice…? KOH-moh seh DEE-seh
    How do you say…?
  • ¿Cómo se escribe? KOH-moh seh ehs-KREE-beh
    How is it spelled?
  • Se necesita ayuda. seh neh-seh-SEE-tah ah-YOO-dah
    Help is needed.
  • Se prohíbe fumar. seh proh-EE-beh foo-MAHR
    Smoking is prohibited.
Impersonal se (people in general)

When no specific thing is the subject, se + singular verb often means "one / people / you (in general)."

Spanish Pronunciation English
Se vive bien en este pueblo. seh BEE-beh byehn ehn EHS-teh PWEH-bloh People live well in this town.
No se puede fumar. noh seh PWEH-deh foo-MAHR You can't smoke. / No smoking.
Example sentences:
  • Se vive bien en este pueblo. seh BEE-beh byehn ehn EHS-teh PWEH-bloh
    People live well in this town.
  • No se puede fumar. noh seh PWEH-deh foo-MAHR
    You can't smoke.
  • Se dice que va a llover. seh DEE-seh keh bah ah yoh-BEHR
    They say it's going to rain.
  • No se sabe. noh seh SAH-beh
    Nobody knows. / It's not known.
  • Se trabaja mucho aquí. seh trah-BAH-hah MOO-choh ah-KEE
    People work a lot here.
Accidental se (it happened to me)

Se me olvidó seh meh ohl-bee-DOH = "It slipped my mind" (not "I forgot on purpose"). The thing forgotten is the subject.

Spanish Pronunciation English
Se me olvidó la llave. seh meh ohl-bee-DOH lah YAH-beh I forgot the key. (it slipped my mind)
Se me olvidaron las llaves. seh meh ohl-bee-DAH-rohn lahs YAH-behs I forgot the keys.
Se le cayó el vaso. seh leh kah-YOH ehl BAH-soh He/she dropped the glass. (it fell on them)
Se nos acabó el pan. seh nohs ah-kah-BOH ehl pahn We ran out of bread.
Example sentences:
  • Se me olvidó la llave. seh meh ohl-bee-DOH lah YAH-beh
    I forgot the key.
  • Se me olvidaron las llaves. seh meh ohl-bee-DAH-rohn lahs YAH-behs
    I forgot the keys.
  • Se le cayó el vaso. seh leh kah-YOH ehl BAH-soh
    He/she dropped the glass.
  • Se nos acabó el pan. seh nohs ah-kah-BOH ehl pahn
    We ran out of bread.
  • Se me rompió el teléfono. seh meh rohm-PYOH ehl teh-LEH-foh-noh
    I broke my phone. (it broke on me)
  • Se te perdió el dinero. seh teh pehr-DYOH ehl dee-NEH-roh
    You lost the money. (it got lost on you)
Ser + participle (more formal passive)

Like English "was written." Less common in everyday speech than se.

Spanish Pronunciation English
El libro fue escrito en 1990. ehl LEE-broh fweh ehs-KREE-toh ehn The book was written in 1990.
La casa fue construida por mi abuelo. lah KAH-sah fweh kohns-tree-EE-dah por mee ah-BWEH-loh The house was built by my grandfather.
Example sentences:
  • El libro fue escrito en 1990. ehl LEE-broh fweh ehs-KREE-toh ehn
    The book was written in 1990.
  • La casa fue construida por mi abuelo. lah KAH-sah fweh kohns-tree-EE-dah por mee ah-BWEH-loh
    The house was built by my grandfather.
  • La carta fue enviada ayer. lah KAHR-tah fweh ehn-BYAH-dah ah-YEHR
    The letter was sent yesterday.
  • Los libros fueron comprados. lohs LEE-brohs FWEH-rohn kohm-PRAH-dohs
    The books were bought.
  • La comida fue preparada por ella. lah koh-MEE-dah fweh preh-pah-RAH-dah por EH-yah
    The food was prepared by her.
Passive / se tips
  • Signs and labels love this: Se alquila seh ahl-KEE-lah (for rent), Se busca seh BOOS-kah (wanted / hiring).
  • Plural thing → plural verb: se venden seh BEHN-dehn, not *se vende casas.
  • This se is not the same as a reflexive "wash yourself," even though the word looks identical. Context decides.
