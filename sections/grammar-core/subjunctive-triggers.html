Subjunctive Triggers

When to use the subjunctive — you already have the tense pages; this is the when

The basic shape

Two clauses, different subjects, and a trigger in the first clause:
Quiero que vengas. 🔊 — I want you to come.

Indicative (fact) Subjunctive (wish / doubt / emotion)
Sé que vienes. 🔊 — I know you’re coming. Dudo que vengas. 🔊 — I doubt you’re coming.
Es verdad que trabaja. 🔊 — It’s true that he works. Es posible que trabaje. 🔊 — It’s possible that he works.

Same subject → infinitive, not subjunctive: Quiero ir. (I want to go.) vs Quiero que vayas. (I want you to go.)

WEIRDO (memory aid)
Letter Idea Trigger Example English
W Wishes querer que, ojalá Ojalá que llueva. 🔊 I hope it rains.
E Emotion alegrarse de que, temer que Me alegro de que estés aquí. 🔊 I’m glad you’re here.
I Impersonal expressions es importante que, es mejor que Es importante que estudies. 🔊 It’s important that you study.
R Requests / recommendations pedir que, recomendar que Te pido que esperes. 🔊 I’m asking you to wait.
D Doubt / denial dudar que, no creer que No creo que sea fácil. 🔊 I don’t think it’s easy.
O Ojalá / maybe quizá, tal vez Quizá venga mañana. 🔊 Maybe he’ll come tomorrow.
Conjunctions that often take subjunctive
Conjunction Pronunciation Example English
para que PAH-rah kay Te lo explico para que entiendas. 🔊 I’m explaining it so that you understand.
antes de que AHN-tehs deh kay Sal antes de que llueva. 🔊 Leave before it rains.
sin que seen kay Salió sin que yo lo viera. 🔊 He left without my seeing him.
cuando (future / not yet) KWAHN-doh Llámame cuando llegues. 🔊 Call me when you arrive. (not yet)
aunque (unknown / contrary) OWN-kay Aunque llueva, voy. 🔊 Even if it rains, I’m going.
Subjunctive trigger tips
  • Full forms: Present Subjunctive and Imperfect Subjunctive in the Tenses menu.
  • Creo que + indicative. No creo que + subjunctive.
  • After a past trigger, Spanish often uses imperfect subjunctive: Quería que vinieras.
