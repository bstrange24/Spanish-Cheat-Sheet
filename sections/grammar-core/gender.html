Gender

Every noun is masculine or feminine

Gender (el ehl / la lah)

In Spanish, every noun is masculine or feminine.
Articles (el ehl / la lah), adjectives, and many pronouns must match that gender (and singular/plural).
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm; Spain only if different.

This sits here on purpose: possessive forms like el mío ehl MEE-oh / la mía lah MEE-ah only make sense once you know gender.

Why el agua ehl AH-gwah?
Feminine words that start with a stressed a- / ha- sound take el ehl in the singular (easier to say), but stay feminine:

  • el agua fría ehl AH-gwah FREE-ah → ehl AH-gwah FREE-ah → the cold water (fría FREE-ah is feminine)
  • las aguas lahs AH-gwahs → lahs AH-gwahs → the waters (plural back to las lahs)

Gender tips
  • Learn nouns with the article: el libro ehl LEE-broh, la casa lah KAH-sah — not just libro LEE-broh / casa KAH-sah.
  • -o often masculine, -a often feminine — but watch exceptions (el día ehl DEE-ah, la mano lah MAH-noh, el problema ehl proh-BLEH-mah).
  • Plural mixed groups usually use masculine: los niños lohs NEE-nyohs, los amigos lohs ah-MEE-gohs.
  • Adjectives match the noun: casa blanca KAH-sah BLAHN-kah, libro rojo LEE-broh ROH-hoh.
  • Speaker gender shows up in soy alto/alta soy AHL-toh, estoy cansado/cansada ehs-TOY kahn-SAH-doh, encantado/encantada.
  • Possessives like mío MEE-oh/mía MEE-ah match the owned thing, not the owner.
  • el agua ehl AH-gwah, el águila ehl AH-hee-lah: singular el ehl, but adjective stays feminine (agua fría AH-gwah FREE-ah).

Pronoun tips
  • tú too (you) has an accent; tu too (your) does not.
  • él ehl (he) has an accent; el ehl (the) does not.
  • mí mee (me, after preposition) has an accent; mi mee (my) does not.
  • Subject pronouns are optional: Hablo español AH-bloh ehs-pah-NYOHL already means "I speak Spanish."
  • LatAm: everyday plural "you" = ustedes oos-TEH-dehs (not vosotros boh-SOH-trohs).
  • Spain: informal plural "you" = vosotros boh-SOH-trohs; formal plural = ustedes oos-TEH-dehs.
  • su soo / suyo SOO-yoh is ambiguous (his/her/your/their) — Spanish often clarifies with de él deh ehl, de ella deh EH-yah, de usted deh oos-TEHD, etc.
