Obligation

Must, have to, should — tener que teh-NEHR keh, hay que eye keh, deber deh-BEHR

Three common patterns
Pattern Who is obliged Example Pronunciation English
tener que teh-NEHR keh + infinitive a specific person (the subject) Tengo que trabajar. TEHN-goh keh trah-bah-HAHR 🔊 TEHN-goh kay trah-bah-HAHR I have to work.
hay que eye keh + infinitive people in general (“one must”) Hay que estudiar. eye keh ehs-too-DYAHR 🔊 eye kay ehs-too-DYAHR You/one has to study. (general rule)
deber deh-BEHR + infinitive a specific person; duty or “should” Debes descansar. DEH-behs dehs-kahn-SAHR 🔊 DEH-behs dehs-kahn-SAHR You should / must rest.
Tener que — present
Person Form Example English
yo yoh tengo que TEHN-goh keh Tengo que irme. TEHN-goh keh EER-meh 🔊 I have to leave.
tú too tienes que TYEH-nehs keh Tienes que llamar. TYEH-nehs keh yah-MAHR 🔊 You have to call.
vos bohs tenés que teh-NEHS keh Tenés que comer. teh-NEHS keh koh-MEHR 🔊 You have to eat. (LatAm voseo)
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD tiene que TYEH-neh keh Ella tiene que estudiar. EH-yah TYEH-neh keh ehs-too-DYAHR 🔊 She has to study.
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs tenemos que teh-NEH-mohs keh Tenemos que pagar. teh-NEH-mohs keh pah-GAHR 🔊 We have to pay.
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs tenéis que teh-NAYS keh Tenéis que esperar. teh-NAYS keh ehs-peh-RAHR 🔊 You all have to wait. (Spain)
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs tienen que TYEH-nehn keh Tienen que llegar temprano. TYEH-nehn keh yeh-GAHR tehm-PRAH-noh 🔊 They / you all have to arrive early.
Deber — present
Person Form Pronunciation English
yo yoh debo DEH-boh DEH-boh I should / I must / I owe
tú too debes DEH-behs DEH-behs you should
él ehl / usted oos-TEHD debe DEH-beh DEH-beh he/she/you should
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs debemos deh-BEH-mohs deh-BEH-mohs we should
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs debéis deh-BEH-ees deh-BEH-ees you all should (Spain)
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs deben DEH-behn DEH-behn they / you all should

Debería deh-beh-REE-ah (conditional) is a softer “should”: Deberías dormir más. deh-beh-REE-ahs dohr-MEER mahs 🔊

Obligation tips
  • Hay que eye keh never changes: not *hayo que, not *hemos que.
  • Tener que teh-NEHR keh is the everyday “I gotta.” Deber deh-BEHR can sound more like advice or moral duty.
  • Need: necesitar neh-seh-see-TAHR is “to need,” not quite as strong as have-to: Necesito agua. neh-seh-SEE-toh AH-gwah
  • See also Tener for more tener que teh-NEHR keh examples.
