Prepositions

Small words that show place, direction, time, and relationships

Example sentences:
  • Voy a casa. boy ah KAH-sah
    I'm going home. (to / at)
  • Al lado del banco. ahl LAH-doh dehl BAHN-koh

  • Antes de las tres. AHN-tehs deh lahs trehs
    Before three o'clock. (before)
  • Bajo la mesa. BAH-hoh lah MEH-sah
    Under the table. (under)
  • Cerca del parque. SEHR-kah dehl PAHR-keh
    Near the park. (near)
  • Café con leche. kah-FEH kohn LEH-cheh
    Coffee with milk. (with)
  • Soy de Texas. soy deh TEHK-sahs
    I'm from Texas. (of / from)
  • Delante de la casa. deh-LAHN-teh deh lah KAH-sah
    In front of the house. (in front of)
  • Desde las ocho. DEHS-deh lahs OH-choh
    Since eight o'clock. (from / since)
  • Después del trabajo. dehs-PWEHS dehl trah-BAH-hoh
    After work. (after)
  • Detrás del coche. deh-TRAHS dehl KOH-cheh
    Behind the car. (behind)
  • Durante la clase. doo-RAHN-teh lah KLAH-seh
    During class. (during)
  • Estoy en casa. ehs-TOY ehn KAH-sah
    I'm at home. (in / on / at)
  • Entre tú y yo. EHN-treh too ee yoh
    Between you and me. (between / among)
  • Hacia la izquierda. AH-syah lah ees-KYEHR-dah
    To the left. (toward)
  • Hasta mañana. AHS-tah mah-NYAH-nah
    See you tomorrow. (until / up to)
  • Lejos de aquí. LEH-hohs deh ah-KEE
    Far from here. (far from)
  • Esto es para ti. EHS-toh ehs PAH-rah tee
    This is for you. (for / in order to / toward)
  • Gracias por todo. GRAH-syahs por TOH-doh
    Thanks for everything. (for / by / through / because of)
  • Sin azúcar. seen ah-SOO-kahr
    Sugar-free / Without sugar. (without)
  • El libro está sobre la mesa. ehl LEE-broh ehs-TAH SOH-breh lah MEH-sah
    The book is on the table. (on / about)

Contractions (must memorize)

Form = Pronunciation Example
al a + el ahl Voy al banco boy ahl BAHN-koh.
del de + el dehl Cerca del parque SEHR-kah dehl PAHR-keh.

No contraction with la: a la, de la.


Additional simple prepositions
Spanish Pronunciation Main English ideas
ante AHN-teh before / in the presence of / faced with
contra KOHN-trah against / opposite
mediante meh-DYAHN-teh by means of / through
según seh-GOON according to / depending on
tras trahs after / behind / besides
vía VEE-ah via / by way of
Example sentences:
  • Compareció ante el juez. kohm-pah-reh-SYOH AHN-teh ehl HWEHS
    He appeared before the judge. (before / in the presence of)
  • Estamos contra la pared. ehs-TAH-mohs KOHN-trah lah pah-REHD
    We are against the wall. (against)
  • Se comunicó mediante señas. seh koh-moo-nee-KOH meh-DYAHN-teh SEH-nyahs
    He communicated through signs. (by means of / through)
  • Según el periódico, lloverá. seh-GOON ehl peh-RYOH-dee-koh, yoh-beh-RAH
    According to the newspaper, it will rain. (according to)
  • Tras la tormenta, salió el sol. trahs lah tor-MEHN-tah, sah-LYOH ehl sohl
    After the storm, the sun came out. (after)
  • Viajamos a Roma vía París. byah-HAH-mohs ah ROH-mah VEE-ah pah-REES
    We traveled to Rome via Paris. (via / by way of)
Useful compound prepositions
Spanish Pronunciation Main English ideas
a pesar de ah peh-SAHR deh despite / in spite of

debajo de deh-BAH-hoh deh underneath / below

Example sentences:
  • Salió a pesar de la lluvia. sah-LYOH ah peh-SAHR deh lah YOO-byah
    He went out despite the rain. (despite / in spite of)
  • Caminamos a través del parque. kah-mee-NAH-mohs ah trah-VEHS dehl PAHR-keh
    We walked through the park. (through / across)
  • Además de cantar, baila. ah-deh-MAHS deh kahn-TAHR, BAH-ee-lah
    Besides singing, she dances. (besides / in addition to)
  • El gato está debajo de la mesa. ehl GAH-toh ehs-TAH deh-BAH-hoh deh lah MEH-sah
    The cat is under the table. (underneath / below)
  • Fui yo en lugar de él. fwee yoh ehn loo-GAHR deh ehl
    I went instead of him. (instead of)
  • Vive junto a la estación. BEE-beh HOON-toh ah lah ehs-tah-SYOHN
    He lives next to the station. (next to / beside)
Preposition tips
  • Voy a España boy ah ehs-PAH-nyah (to) vs Estoy en España ehs-TOY ehn ehs-PAH-nyah (in).
  • Soy de México soy deh MEH-hee-koh = I'm from Mexico.
  • Always: al = a + el, del = de + el.
  • If you mean "in order to + verb," use para + infinitive.
  • If you mean "because of / thanks for / through," lean por.
