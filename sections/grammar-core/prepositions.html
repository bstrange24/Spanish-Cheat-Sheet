Contractions (must memorize)

Form = Pronunciation Example
al ahl a + el ehl ahl Voy al banco boy ahl BAHN-koh. → boy ahl BAHN-koh
del dehl de deh + el ehl dehl Cerca del parque SEHR-kah dehl PAHR-keh. → SEHR-kah dehl PAHR-kay

No contraction with la lah: a la, de la.

Por vs para (the important contrast)

Rough guide

  • Para PAH-rah → destination, purpose, deadline, recipient, opinion
  • Por por → reason, exchange, duration, movement through, "per," passive "by"

Preposition tips
  • Voy a España boy ah ehs-PAH-nyah (to) vs Estoy en España ehs-TOY ehn ehs-PAH-nyah (in).
  • Soy de México soy deh MEH-hee-koh = I'm from Mexico.
  • Always: al ahl = a + el ehl, del dehl = de deh + el ehl.
  • If you mean "in order to + verb," use para PAH-rah + infinitive.
  • If you mean "because of / thanks for / through," lean por por.
