Contractions (must memorize)

Form = Pronunciation Example
al a + el ahl Voy al banco boy ahl BAHN-koh.
del de + el dehl Cerca del parque SEHR-kah dehl PAHR-keh.

No contraction with la: a la, de la.


Por vs para (the important contrast)

Rough guide

  • Para → destination, purpose, deadline, recipient, opinion
  • Por → reason, exchange, duration, movement through, "per," passive "by"
Use Prefer
Recipient (for someone) para
Purpose (in order to) para
Deadline para
Destination para
Opinion para
Reason / because of por
Duration por
Exchange / price por

"Per" por

Example sentences:
  • Esto es para ti. EHS-toh ehs PAH-rah tee
    This is for you. (recipient)
  • Este regalo es para mi mamá. EHS-teh reh-GAH-loh ehs PAH-rah mee mah-MAH
    This gift is for my mom. (recipient)
  • Trabajo para una empresa. trah-BAH-hoh PAH-rah OO-nah ehm-PREH-sah
    I work for a company. (recipient/employment)
  • Estudio para aprender. ehs-TOO-dyoh PAH-rah ah-prehn-DEHR
    I study in order to learn. (purpose)
  • Para mañana. PAH-rah mah-NYAH-nah
    By/for tomorrow. (deadline)
  • Necesito el informe para el viernes. neh-seh-SEE-toh ehl een-FOR-meh PAH-rah ehl BYEHR-nehs
    I need the report by Friday. (deadline)
  • Salgo para Madrid. SAHL-goh PAH-rah mah-DREED
    I'm leaving for Madrid. (destination)
  • Para mí, es fácil. PAH-rah mee ehs FAH-seel
    For me, it's easy. (opinion)
  • Gracias por todo. GRAH-syahs por TOH-doh
    Thanks for everything. (reason)
  • Gracias por tu ayuda. GRAH-syahs por too ah-YOO-dah
    Thanks for your help. (reason)
  • Lo hago por ti. loh AH-goh por tee
    I do it because of you / for your sake. (reason)
  • Estudio por dos horas. ehs-TOO-dyoh por dohs OH-rahs
    I study for two hours. (duration)
  • Lo compro por diez dólares. loh KOHM-proh por dyehs DOH-lah-rehs
    I'll buy it for ten dollars. (exchange/price)
  • Pago por el café. PAH-goh por ehl kah-FEH
    I pay for the coffee. (exchange/price)
  • Caminamos por el parque. kah-mee-NAH-mohs por ehl PAHR-keh
    We walk through the park. (movement through)
  • Viajamos por España. byah-HAH-mohs por ehs-PAH-nyah
    We travel through Spain. (movement through)
  • Dos veces por semana. dohs BEH-sehs por seh-MAH-nah
    Twice per week. ("per")
  • Hablo por teléfono. AH-bloh por teh-LEH-foh-noh
    I talk by phone. (means/by)
  • Está por aquí. ehs-TAH por ah-KEE
    It's around here. (approximate place)
  • Estudio por la mañana. ehs-TOO-dyoh por lah mah-NYAH-nah
    I study in the morning. (time of day)

Quick tip:
Para looks forward — purpose, destination, deadline, recipient.
Por looks backward — reason, cause, duration, exchange, means.

Preposition tips
  • Voy a España boy ah ehs-PAH-nyah (to) vs Estoy en España ehs-TOY ehn ehs-PAH-nyah (in).
  • Soy de México soy deh MEH-hee-koh = I'm from Mexico.
  • Always: al = a + el, del = de + el.
  • If you mean "in order to + verb," use para + infinitive.
  • If you mean "because of / thanks for / through," lean por.
