Tú / Usted / Vos

Choosing the right “you” — informal, formal, and voseo

The three “you”s
Form Pronunciation When Verb style
tú too Informal singular: friends, family, kids, peers tú hablas, tú eres
usted oos-TEHD Formal singular: strangers, elders, customers, respect Uses 3rd-person endings: usted habla, usted es
vos bohs Informal singular in much of Central America + Argentina/Uruguay/Paraguay (and parts of Colombia, Chile) Present often stress on the ending: vos hablás, vos sos
Plural “you”
Form Region Example English
ustedes Latin America: all “you all.” Spain: formal “you all.” Ustedes hablan. 🔊 You all speak.
vosotros / vosotras Spain informal “you all” Vosotros habláis. 🔊 You all speak. (Spain)

Tú / usted / vos tips
  • If unsure with an adult you just met, usted is safer. They may invite you to tutea (switch to tú).
  • This cheat sheet includes vos rows on Tener, Hacer, Ir, Poder, Querer, Decir so you can hear both.
  • Object pronouns stay te for both tú and vos: te quiero.
