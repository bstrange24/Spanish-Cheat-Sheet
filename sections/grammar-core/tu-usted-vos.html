Tú / Usted / Vos

Choosing the right “you” — informal, formal, and voseo

The three “you”s
Form Pronunciation When Verb style
tú too too Informal singular: friends, family, kids, peers tú hablas too AH-blahs, tú eres too EH-rehs
usted oos-TEHD oos-TEHD Formal singular: strangers, elders, customers, respect Uses 3rd-person endings: usted habla oos-TEHD AH-blah, usted es oos-TEHD ehs
vos bohs bohs Informal singular in much of Central America + Argentina/Uruguay/Paraguay (and parts of Colombia, Chile) Present often stress on the ending: vos hablás bohs ah-BLAHS, vos sos bohs sohs
Plural “you”
Form Region Example English
ustedes oos-TEH-dehs Latin America: all “you all.” Spain: formal “you all.” Ustedes hablan. oos-TEH-dehs AH-blahn 🔊 You all speak.
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs Spain informal “you all” Vosotros habláis. boh-SOH-trohs ah-BLEYES 🔊 You all speak. (Spain)

Tú / usted / vos tips
  • If unsure with an adult you just met, usted oos-TEHD is safer. They may invite you to tutea too-TEH-ah (switch to tú).
  • This cheat sheet includes vos bohs rows on Tener, Hacer, Ir, Poder, Querer, Decir so you can hear both.
  • Object pronouns stay te teh for both tú and vos: te quiero teh KYEH-roh.
