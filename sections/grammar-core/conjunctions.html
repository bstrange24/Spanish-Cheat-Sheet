Conjunctions

Connecting words and phrases — and, or, but, because, if, when

Coordinating conjunctions

Coordinating conjunctions join equals: two words, two phrases, or two independent clauses.

Spanish Pronunciation English
y ee and
e eh and (before i- / hi-)
o oh or
u oo or (before o- / ho-)
pero PEH-roh but
sino SEE-noh but rather (after a negative)
ni nee nor / not even
Example sentences:
  • Pan y queso. pahn ee KAY-so
    Bread and cheese.
  • Padre e hijo. PAH-dreh eh EE-hoh
    Father and son.
  • Café o té. kah-FEH oh teh
    Coffee or tea.
  • Uno u otro. OO-noh oo OH-troh
    One or the other.
  • Es caro, pero bueno. ehs KAH-roh PEH-roh BWEH-noh
    It's expensive, but good.
  • No es rojo sino azul. noh ehs ROH-hoh SEE-noh ah-SOOL
    It's not red but rather blue.
  • No come ni carne ni pescado. noh KOH-meh nee KAHR-neh nee pehs-KAH-doh
    He/she doesn't eat meat or fish.

Tip: Y ee → E eh before a word that starts with the sound ee (i- or hi-).

Tip: O oh → U oo before a word that starts with the sound oh (o- or ho-).

Use Spanish Pronunciation English
o (normal) té o café teh oh kah-FEH tea or coffee

pero vs sino

Both can mean but. Sino replaces a negated idea ("not X, but Y instead").

Use Prefer Spanish Pronunciation English
Contrast / add information pero pero PEH-roh but
After a negative: not A, but B sino sino SEE-noh but (rather)
After a negative + a full clause sino que sino que SEE-noh keh but rather (with verb)
Not only… but also… no solo… sino también no solo… sino también noh SOH-loh SEE-noh tahm-BYEHN not only… but also
Example sentences:
  • Quiero ir, pero estoy cansado. KYEH-roh eer PEH-roh ehs-TOY kahn-SAH-doh
    I want to go, but I'm tired.
  • No es médico sino profesor. noh ehs MEH-dee-koh SEE-noh proh-feh-SOHR
    He's not a doctor but a teacher.
  • No estudia, sino que trabaja. noh ehs-TOO-dyah SEE-noh keh trah-BAH-hah
    He doesn't study; rather, he works.
  • No solo canta sino también baila. noh SOH-loh KAHN-tah SEE-noh tahm-BYEHN BEYE-lah
    Not only does she sing, she also dances.

Quick test: if you can say "on the other hand / however," use pero. If you are correcting a negated word, use sino.


Subordinating conjunctions

These introduce a dependent clause (the idea cannot stand alone).

Spanish Pronunciation English
que keh that
porque pohr-KEH because
cuando KWAHN-doh when
mientras MYEHN-trahs while / as long as
aunque OWN-keh although / even if
si see if
para que PAH-rah keh so that (purpose)
Example sentences:
  • Creo que es verdad. KREH-oh keh ehs behr-DAHD
    I believe that it's true.
  • Estudio porque quiero aprender. ehs-TOO-dyoh pohr-KEH KYEH-roh ah-prehn-DEHR
    I study because I want to learn.
  • Te llamo cuando llego. teh YAH-moh KWAHN-doh YEH-goh
    I call you when I arrive.
  • Leo mientras como. LEH-oh MYEHN-trahs KOH-moh
    I read while I eat.
  • Salgo aunque llueve. SAHL-goh OWN-keh YWEH-beh
    I go out although it's raining.
  • Si llueve, me quedo. see YWEH-beh meh KEH-doh
    If it rains, I stay.
  • Hablo despacio para que me entiendas. AH-bloh dehs-PAH-syoh PAH-rah keh meh ehn-TYEHN-dahs
    I speak slowly so that you understand me.

Notes on mood:

  • Porque + indicative (a real reason):
    Lo hago porque quiero. loh AH-goh pohr-KEH KYEH-roh
    I do it because I want to.
  • Para que + subjunctive (purpose, different subject):
    Te lo explico para que lo entiendas. teh loh ehks-PLEE-koh PAH-rah keh loh ehn-TYEHN-dahs
    I'll explain it to you so you understand.
  • Same subject → use para + infinitive, not para que:
    Estudio para aprender. ehs-TOO-dyoh PAH-rah ah-prehn-DEHR
    I study to learn.
  • Cuando + indicative (habit / past / now); + subjunctive (future not yet happened):
    Cuando llegue, te llamo. KWAHN-doh YEH-geh teh YAH-moh
    I'll call you when I arrive.
  • Si (if) has no accent. Sí = yes.

Example sentences:

    It's raining, so I'm staying.
  • Entonces, ¿qué hacemos? ehn-TOHN-sehs keh ah-SEH-mohs
    So, what do we do?
  • Es barato y además bueno. ehs bah-RAH-toh ee ah-deh-MAHS BWEH-noh
    It's cheap and besides that, good.
  • Es difícil; sin embargo, es útil. ehs dee-FEE-seel seen ehm-BAHR-goh ehs OO-teel
    It's difficult; however, it's useful.
  • No puedo, o sea, no tengo tiempo. noh PWEH-doh oh SEH-ah noh TEHN-goh TYEHM-poh
    I can't, I mean, I don't have time.
  • Como no tengo dinero, no voy. KOH-moh noh TEHN-goh dee-NEH-roh noh boy
    Since I don't have money, I'm not going.
Conjunction tips
  • y → e before i- / hi- (not hie-): Francia e Italia FRAHN-syah eh ee-TAH-lyah.
  • o → u before o- / ho-: siete u ocho SYEH-teh oo OH-choh.
  • pero = but (however). sino = but rather (after no).
  • ni… ni… = neither… nor…: ni tú ni yo nee too nee yoh.
  • porque (because, one word, no accent on que) vs por qué (why?).
  • para que needs a new subject + subjunctive; same subject → para + infinitive.
  • si = if; sí = yes.
