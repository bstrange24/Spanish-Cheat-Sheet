Conjunctions

Connecting words and phrases — and, or, but, because, if, when

pero vs sino

Both can mean but. Sino replaces a negated idea ("not X, but Y instead").

Use Prefer Spanish Pronunciation English
Contrast / add information pero pero PEH-roh but
After a negative: not A, but B sino sino SEE-noh but (rather)
After a negative + a full clause sino que sino que SEE-noh keh but rather (with verb)
Not only… but also… no solo… sino también no solo… sino también noh SOH-loh SEE-noh tahm-BYEHN not only… but also
Contrast with emphasis pero sí pero sí PEH-roh see but yes / but it does
Contrast with emphasis pero no pero no PEH-roh noh but no / but it doesn't
Not… but rather (noun) sino no… sino noh… SEE-noh not… but rather
Not… but rather (verb) sino que no… sino que noh… SEE-noh keh not… but rather (with verb)
Example sentences:
  • Quiero ir, pero estoy cansado. KYEH-roh eer PEH-roh ehs-TOY kahn-SAH-doh
    I want to go, but I'm tired.
  • No es médico sino profesor. noh ehs MEH-dee-koh SEE-noh proh-feh-SOHR
    He's not a doctor but a teacher.
  • No estudia, sino que trabaja. noh ehs-TOO-dyah SEE-noh keh trah-BAH-hah
    He doesn't study; rather, he works.
  • No solo canta sino también baila. noh SOH-loh KAHN-tah SEE-noh tahm-BYEHN BEYE-lah
    Not only does she sing, she also dances.
  • No es tonto, pero sí es terco. noh ehs TOHN-toh PEH-roh see ehs TEHR-koh
    He's not dumb, but he is stubborn.
  • Quiero ir, pero no puedo. KYEH-roh eer PEH-roh noh PWEH-doh
    I want to go, but I can't.
  • No quiero café sino té. noh KYEH-roh kah-FEH SEE-noh teh
    I don't want coffee but rather tea.
  • No es que no quiera, sino que no puedo. noh ehs keh noh KYEH-rah SEE-noh keh noh PWEH-doh
    It's not that I don't want to, but rather that I can't.

Quick test: if you can say "on the other hand / however," use pero. If you are correcting a negated word, use sino.
Pero sí PEH-roh see = but yes / but it does; pero no PEH-roh noh = but no / but it doesn't.


que vs. de que

Use que after most verbs. Use de que after verbs and nouns that require de.

Use Spanish Pronunciation English
que (direct) Creo que es verdad. KREH-oh keh ehs behr-DAHD I believe it's true.
de que (after alegrarse de, darse cuenta de, etc.) Me alegro de que estés aquí. meh ah-LEH-groh deh keh ehs-TEHS ah-KEE I'm glad you're here.
de que (after nouns like la idea, el hecho) La idea de que vengas me gusta. lah ee-DEH-ah deh keh BEHN-gahs meh GOOS-tah I like the idea of you coming.
de que (after estar seguro de) Estoy seguro de que vendrá. ehs-TOY seh-GOO-roh deh keh behn-DRAH I'm sure he/she will come.
de que (after dudar de) Dudo de que sea verdad. DOO-doh deh keh SEH-ah behr-DAHD I doubt it's true.
de que (after acordarse de) Me acuerdo de que lo dije. meh ah-KWEHR-doh deh keh loh DEE-heh I remember that I said it.
de que (after olvidarse de) Me olvidé de que era hoy. meh ohl-bee-DEH deh keh EH-rah oy I forgot that it was today.
de que (after tener miedo de) Tengo miedo de que se caiga. TEHN-goh MYEH-doh deh keh seh KIGH-gah I'm afraid it might fall.
que (after decir, pensar, saber) Digo que sí. DEE-goh keh see I say yes.
que (after esperar, querer) Espero que vengas. ehs-PEH-roh keh BEHN-gahs I hope you come.

Tip: If the verb or noun is followed by de in other contexts, keep de before que.
Me alegro de que meh ah-LEH-groh deh keh — because it's alegrarse de algo.
Creo que KREH-oh keh — because it's creer algo (no de).

Notes on mood:

  • Porque + indicative (a real reason):
    Lo hago porque quiero. loh AH-goh pohr-KEH KYEH-roh
    I do it because I want to.
  • Para que + subjunctive (purpose, different subject):
    Te lo explico para que lo entiendas. teh loh ehks-PLEE-koh PAH-rah keh loh ehn-TYEHN-dahs
    I'll explain it to you so you understand.
  • Same subject → use para + infinitive, not para que:
    Estudio para aprender. ehs-TOO-dyoh PAH-rah ah-prehn-DEHR
    I study to learn.
  • Cuando + indicative (habit / past / now); + subjunctive (future not yet happened):
    Cuando llegue, te llamo. KWAHN-doh YEH-geh teh YAH-moh
    I'll call you when I arrive.
  • Si (if) has no accent. Sí = yes.
  • Aunque + indicative (known fact) or subjunctive (hypothetical):
    Aunque llueve, salgo. OWN-keh YWEH-beh SAHL-goh (Although it's raining, I go out.)
    Aunque llueva, saldré. OWN-keh YWEH-bah sahl-DREH (Even if it rains, I'll go out.)
  • A menos que + subjunctive:
    No iré a menos que me invites. noh ee-REH ah MEH-nohs keh meh een-BEE-tehs
    I won't go unless you invite me.
  • Antes de que + subjunctive:
    Antes de que llegues, prepara la cena. AHN-tehs deh keh YEH-gehs preh-PAH-rah lah SEH-nah
    Before you arrive, prepare dinner.
  • Hasta que + indicative (past) or subjunctive (future):
    Espera hasta que llegue. ehs-PEH-rah AHS-tah keh YEH-geh
    Wait until he/she arrives.

Conjunction tips
  • y → e before i- / hi- (not hie-): Francia e Italia FRAHN-syah eh ee-TAH-lyah.
  • o → u before o- / ho-: siete u ocho SYEH-teh oo OH-choh.
  • pero = but (however). sino = but rather (after no).
  • ni… ni… = neither… nor…: ni tú ni yo nee too nee yoh.
  • o… o… = either… or…: o vienes o te quedas oh BYEH-nehs oh teh KEH-dahs.
  • porque (because, one word, no accent on que) vs por qué (why?).
  • para que needs a new subject + subjunctive; same subject → para + infinitive.
  • si = if; sí = yes.
  • Aunque + indicative (fact) or subjunctive (hypothetical): Aunque llueve, salgo. vs Aunque llueva, saldré.
  • A menos que ah MEH-nohs keh + subjunctive = unless.
  • Antes de que AHN-tehs deh keh + subjunctive = before (with new subject).
  • Hasta que AHS-tah keh + indicative (past) or subjunctive (future) = until.
  • En cuanto ehn KWAHN-toh = as soon as; tan pronto como tahn PROHN-toh KOH-moh = as soon as.
  • Sin que seen keh + subjunctive = without.
  • A pesar de que ah peh-SAHR deh keh = despite / in spite of.
  • De modo que deh MOH-doh keh and de manera que deh mah-NEH-rah keh = so that / in such a way that.
  • Dado que DAH-doh keh = given that; puesto que PWEHS-toh keh = since.
  • En caso de que ehn KAH-soh deh keh + subjunctive = in case.
  • A condición de que ah kohn-dee-SYOHN deh keh + subjunctive = on condition that.
  • Siempre y cuando SYEHM-preh ee KWAHN-doh + subjunctive = as long as / provided that.
  • O sea oh SEH-ah = that is / I mean; es decir ehs deh-SEER = that is to say.
  • Por ejemplo pohr eh-HEHM-ploh = for example; en resumen ehn reh-SOO-mehn = in summary.
