Comparisons

More than, less than, as… as, and the most

Inequality — más / menos + adj + que

más + adjective + que = more … than
menos + adjective + que = less … than

Pattern Spanish Pronunciation English
más + adj + que más alto que mahs AHL-toh keh taller than
menos + adj + que menos caro que MEH-nohs KAH-roh keh less expensive than
With an adverb más rápido que mahs RAH-pee-doh keh faster than
With a noun más libros que mahs LEE-brohs keh more books than
With a verb más que mahs keh more than
With a pronoun más que yo mahs keh yoh more than me
With a clause más de lo que mahs deh loh keh more than (what)
Negative comparison menos alto que MEH-nohs AHL-toh keh less tall than
With numbers más de diez mahs deh dyehs more than ten
With numbers (less) menos de cinco MEH-nohs deh SEEN-koh less than five
Emphatic mucho más que MOO-choh mahs keh much more than
Emphatic mucho menos que MOO-choh MEH-nohs keh much less than
Approximation poco más que POH-koh mahs keh slightly more than
Approximation poco menos que POH-koh MEH-nohs keh slightly less than
Example sentences:
  • Ana es más alta que Luis. AH-nah ehs mahs AHL-tah kay lwees
    Ana is taller than Luis.
  • Este coche es menos caro que ese. EHS-teh KOH-cheh ehs MEH-nohs KAH-roh kay EH-seh
    This car is less expensive than that one.
  • Corre más rápido que yo. KOH-rreh mahs RAH-pee-doh kay yoh
    He/she runs faster than I do.
  • Tengo más libros que tú. TEHN-goh mahs LEE-brohs kay too
    I have more books than you.
  • Estudio más que antes. ehs-TOO-dyoh mahs kay AHN-tehs
    I study more than before.
  • Ella sabe más que yo. EH-yah SAH-beh mahs keh yoh
    She knows more than I do.
  • Es más complicado de lo que pensaba. ehs mahs kohm-plee-KAH-doh deh loh keh pehn-SAH-bah
    It's more complicated than I thought.
  • Este examen es menos difícil que el anterior. EHS-teh ehk-SAH-mehn ehs MEH-nohs dee-FEE-seel keh ehl ahN-teh-RYOHR
    This exam is less difficult than the previous one.
  • Hay más de veinte personas. eye mahs deh BAYN-teh pehr-SOH-nahs
    There are more than twenty people.
  • Cuesta menos de cinco euros. KWEHS-tah MEH-nohs deh SEEN-koh EH-oo-rohs
    It costs less than five euros.
  • Es mucho más interesante que eso. ehs MOO-choh mahs een-teh-reh-SAHN-teh keh EH-soh
    It's much more interesting than that.
  • Es mucho menos peligroso. ehs MOO-choh MEH-nohs peh-lee-GROH-soh
    It's much less dangerous.
  • Gana poco más que el mínimo. GAH-nah POH-koh mahs keh ehl MEE-nee-moh
    He/She earns slightly more than the minimum.
  • Es poco menos que imposible. ehs POH-koh MEH-nohs keh eem-poh-SEE-bleh
    It's slightly less than impossible.

Tip: After a number, use de (not que): más de diez mahs deh dyehs = more than ten.
Negative + number: no más de noh mahs deh or no más que noh mahs keh can mean "only."
Mucho más MOO-choh mahs = much more; mucho menos MOO-choh MEH-nohs = much less.
Poco más POH-koh mahs = slightly more; poco menos POH-koh MEH-nohs = slightly less.

lo más / lo menos + adjective + posible = as … as possible.

Pattern Spanish Pronunciation English
lo más + adj + posible lo más rápido posible loh mahs RAH-pee-doh poh-SEE-bleh as fast as possible
lo menos + adj + posible lo menos caro posible loh MEH-nohs KAH-roh poh-SEE-bleh as cheap as possible
lo más + adv + posible lo más pronto posible loh mahs PROHN-toh poh-SEE-bleh as soon as possible
lo menos + adv + posible lo menos frecuentemente posible loh MEH-nohs freh-kwehn-teh-MEHN-teh poh-SEE-bleh as infrequently as possible
With noun la mayor cantidad posible lah mah-YOHR kahn-tee-DAHD poh-SEE-bleh as much as possible
With verb lo más que puedas loh mahs keh PWEH-dahs as much as you can
With verb lo menos que puedas loh MEH-nohs keh PWEH-dahs as little as you can
Alternative tan rápido como sea posible tahn RAH-pee-doh KOH-moh seh-ah poh-SEE-bleh as fast as possible
Example sentences:
  • Hazlo lo más rápido posible. AHS-loh loh mahs RAH-pee-doh poh-SEE-bleh
    Do it as fast as possible.
  • Quiero lo menos caro posible. KYEH-roh loh MEH-nohs KAH-roh poh-SEE-bleh
    I want the cheapest possible (as cheap as possible).
  • Ven lo más pronto posible. behn loh mahs PROHN-toh poh-SEE-bleh
    Come as soon as possible.
  • Habla lo menos frecuentemente posible. AH-blah loh MEH-nohs freh-kwehn-teh-MEHN-teh poh-SEE-bleh
    Speak as infrequently as possible.
  • Quiero la mayor cantidad posible. KYEH-roh lah mah-YOHR kahn-tee-DAHD poh-SEE-bleh
    I want as much as possible.
  • Estudia lo más que puedas. ehs-TOO-dyah loh mahs keh PWEH-dahs
    Study as much as you can.
  • Come lo menos que puedas. KOH-meh loh MEH-nohs keh PWEH-dahs
    Eat as little as you can.
  • Corre tan rápido como sea posible. KOH-rreh tahn RAH-pee-doh KOH-moh seh-ah poh-SEE-bleh
    Run as fast as possible.

Use de lo que when comparing to a clause (a whole idea), not just a noun or pronoun.

Pattern Spanish Pronunciation English
más + adj + de lo que más caro de lo que mahs KAH-roh deh loh keh more expensive than (what)
menos + adj + de lo que menos difícil de lo que MEH-nohs dee-FEE-seel deh loh keh less difficult than (what)
With adverb más rápido de lo que mahs RAH-pee-doh deh loh keh faster than (what)
With verb más de lo que pensaba mahs deh loh keh pehn-SAH-bah more than I thought
With verb menos de lo que esperaba MEH-nohs deh loh keh ehs-peh-RAH-bah less than I expected
With noun más dinero de lo que tengo mahs dee-NEH-roh deh loh keh TEHN-goh more money than I have
With noun menos tiempo de lo que necesito MEH-nohs TYEHM-poh deh loh keh neh-seh-SEE-toh less time than I need
Emphatic mucho más de lo que MOO-choh mahs deh loh keh much more than
Emphatic mucho menos de lo que MOO-choh MEH-nohs deh loh keh much less than
Example sentences:
  • Es más caro de lo que pensaba. ehs mahs KAH-roh deh loh keh pehn-SAH-bah
    It's more expensive than I thought.
  • Es menos difícil de lo que parece. ehs MEH-nohs dee-FEE-seel deh loh keh pah-REH-seh
    It's less difficult than it seems.
  • Corre más rápido de lo que creía. KOH-rreh mahs RAH-pee-doh deh loh keh kreh-EE-ah
    He/she runs faster than I believed.
  • Gasté más de lo que quería. gahs-TEH mahs deh loh keh keh-REE-ah
    I spent more than I wanted.
  • Recibí menos de lo que esperaba. reh-see-BEE MEH-nohs deh loh keh ehs-peh-RAH-bah
    I received less than I expected.
  • Necesito más dinero de lo que tengo. neh-seh-SEE-toh mahs dee-NEH-roh deh loh keh TEHN-goh
    I need more money than I have.
  • Tengo menos tiempo de lo que necesito. TEHN-goh MEH-nohs TYEHM-poh deh loh keh neh-seh-SEE-toh
    I have less time than I need.
  • Es mucho más complicado de lo que pensábamos. ehs MOO-choh mahs kohm-plee-KAH-doh deh loh keh pehn-SAH-bah-mohs
    It's much more complicated than we thought.
  • Es mucho menos grave de lo que parece. ehs MOO-choh MEH-nohs GRAH-beh deh loh keh pah-REH-seh
    It's much less serious than it seems.
Equality — tan + adj + como

tan + adjective/adverb + como = as … as

Pattern Spanish Pronunciation English
tan + adj + como tan alta como tahn AHL-tah KOH-moh as tall as
tan + adverb + como tan rápido como tahn RAH-pee-doh KOH-moh as fast as
Negative equality no tan fácil como noh tahn FAH-seel KOH-moh (LatAm) / FAH-theel (Spain) not as easy as
With pronoun tan alto como yo tahn AHL-toh KOH-moh yoh as tall as me
With clause tan pronto como pueda tahn PROHN-toh KOH-moh PWEH-dah as soon as I can
With noun tan interesante como tahn een-teh-reh-SAHN-teh KOH-moh as interesting as
With adverb tan lentamente como tahn lehn-tah-MEHN-teh KOH-moh as slowly as
Negative no tan caro como noh tahn KAH-roh KOH-moh not as expensive as
Negative no tan rápido como noh tahn RAH-pee-doh KOH-moh not as fast as
Emphatic tan… como siempre tahn… KOH-moh SYEHM-preh as … as always
Emphatic tan… como nunca tahn… KOH-moh NOON-kah as … as never before
Example sentences:
  • Ana es tan alta como Luis. AH-nah ehs tahn AHL-tah KOH-moh lwees
    Ana is as tall as Luis.
  • Corre tan rápido como tú. KOH-rreh tahn RAH-pee-doh KOH-moh too
    He/she runs as fast as you.
  • No es tan fácil como parece. noh ehs tahn FAH-seel KOH-moh pah-REH-seh (LatAm) / FAH-theel… pah-REH-theh (Spain)
    It's not as easy as it seems.
  • Es tan alto como yo. ehs tahn AHL-toh KOH-moh yoh
    He is as tall as me.
  • Ven tan pronto como puedas. behn tahn PROHN-toh KOH-moh PWEH-dahs
    Come as soon as you can.
  • Es tan interesante como el otro. ehs tahn een-teh-reh-SAHN-teh KOH-moh ehl OH-troh
    It's as interesting as the other one.
  • Habla tan lentamente como siempre. AH-blah tahn lehn-tah-MEHN-teh KOH-moh SYEHM-preh
    He/She speaks as slowly as always.
  • No es tan caro como pensaba. noh ehs tahn KAH-roh KOH-moh pehn-SAH-bah
    It's not as expensive as I thought.
  • No corre tan rápido como ella. noh KOH-rreh tahn RAH-pee-doh KOH-moh EH-yah
    He doesn't run as fast as she does.
  • Estoy tan cansado como siempre. ehs-TOY tahn kahn-SAH-doh KOH-moh SYEHM-preh
    I'm as tired as always.
  • Está tan feliz como nunca. ehs-TAH tahn feh-LEES KOH-moh NOON-kah
    He/She is as happy as never before.

Tip: Tan tahn is invariable — it never changes for gender or number. It modifies adjectives and adverbs only.
For nouns, use tanto TAHN-toh / tanta TAHN-tah / tantos TAHN-tohs / tantas TAHN-tahs.
Tan pronto como tahn PROHN-toh KOH-moh = as soon as.


Equality with nouns — tanto/a/os/as + noun + como

Tanto agrees with the noun (not the person).

Form Use with Spanish Pronunciation English
tanto masculine singular tanto dinero como TAHN-toh dee-NEH-roh KOH-moh as much money as
tanta feminine singular tanta agua como TAHN-tah AH-gwah KOH-moh as much water as
tantos masculine plural tantos libros como TAHN-tohs LEE-brohs KOH-moh as many books as
tantas feminine plural tantas amigas como TAHN-tahs ah-MEE-gahs KOH-moh as many (female) friends as
tanto (invariable) with a verb tanto como TAHN-toh KOH-moh as much as
Negative with noun no tanto dinero como noh TAHN-toh dee-NEH-roh KOH-moh not as much money as
Negative with verb no trabajo tanto como noh trah-BAH-hoh TAHN-toh KOH-moh I don't work as much as
With pronoun with noun tantos como tú TAHN-tohs KOH-moh too as many as you
With demonstrative with noun tanto de esto como TAHN-toh deh EHS-toh KOH-moh as much of this as
Emphatic with noun tanto… como sea posible TAHN-toh… KOH-moh seh-ah poh-SEE-bleh as much … as possible
Example sentences:
  • Tengo tanto dinero como tú. TEHN-goh TAHN-toh dee-NEH-roh KOH-moh too
    I have as much money as you.
  • Hay tanta agua como ayer. eye TAHN-tah AH-gwah KOH-moh ah-YEHR
    There's as much water as yesterday.
  • Tengo tantos libros como ella. TEHN-goh TAHN-tohs LEE-brohs KOH-moh EH-yah
    I have as many books as she does.
  • Tiene tantas amigas como yo. TYEH-neh TAHN-tahs ah-MEE-gahs KOH-moh yoh
    She has as many (female) friends as I do.
  • Trabaja tanto como yo. trah-BAH-hah TAHN-toh KOH-moh yoh
    He/she works as much as I do.
  • No tengo tanto dinero como pensaba. noh TEHN-goh TAHN-toh dee-NEH-roh KOH-moh pehn-SAH-bah
    I don't have as much money as I thought.
  • No trabajo tanto como antes. noh trah-BAH-hoh TAHN-toh KOH-moh AHN-tehs
    I don't work as much as before.
  • Quiero tantos como tú. KYEH-roh TAHN-tohs KOH-moh too
    I want as many as you.
  • Quiero tanto de esto como sea posible. KYEH-roh TAHN-toh deh EHS-toh KOH-moh seh-ah poh-SEE-bleh
    I want as much of this as possible.
  • Ahorra tanto dinero como sea posible. ah-OH-rrah TAHN-toh dee-NEH-roh KOH-moh seh-ah poh-SEE-bleh
    Save as much money as possible.

Tip: Tanto TAHN-toh agrees with the noun it modifies: tanto dinero (masc. sing.), tanta agua (fem. sing.), tantos libros (masc. pl.), tantas amigas (fem. pl.).
With a verb, it stays invariable: trabaja tanto como trah-BAH-hah TAHN-toh KOH-moh.

cada vez más + adv cada vez más lentamente KAH-dah behs mahs lehn-tah-MEHN-teh more and more slowly
cada vez menos + adv cada vez menos frecuentemente KAH-dah behs MEH-nohs freh-kwehn-teh-MEHN-teh less and less frequently
cada vez más + verb cada vez más estudia KAH-dah behs mahs ehs-TOO-dyah studies more and more
cada vez menos + verb cada vez menos sale KAH-dah behs MEH-nohs SAH-leh goes out less and less
Alternative más y más mahs ee mahs more and more
Alternative menos y menos MEH-nohs ee MEH-nohs less and less
Example sentences:
  • El tren va cada vez más rápido. ehl trehn bah KAH-dah behs mahs RAH-pee-doh
    The train goes faster and faster.
  • La película es cada vez menos interesante. lah peh-LEE-koo-lah ehs KAH-dah behs MEH-nohs een-teh-reh-SAHN-teh
    The movie is less and less interesting.
  • Hay cada vez más gente. eye KAH-dah behs mahs HEHN-teh
    There are more and more people.
  • Tengo cada vez menos tiempo. TEHN-goh KAH-dah behs MEH-nohs TYEHM-poh
    I have less and less time.
  • Habla cada vez más lentamente. AH-blah KAH-dah behs mahs lehn-tah-MEHN-teh
    He/She speaks more and more slowly.
  • Sale cada vez menos frecuentemente. SAH-leh KAH-dah behs MEH-nohs freh-kwehn-teh-MEHN-teh
    He/She goes out less and less frequently.
  • Cada vez más estudia. KAH-dah behs mahs ehs-TOO-dyah
    He/She studies more and more.
  • Cada vez menos sale. KAH-dah behs MEH-nohs SAH-leh
    He/She goes out less and less.
  • Es más y más difícil. ehs mahs ee mahs dee-FEE-seel
    It's more and more difficult.
  • Es menos y menos común. ehs MEH-nohs ee MEH-nohs koh-MOON
    It's less and less common.

Tip: Cada vez más KAH-dah behs mahs = more and more; cada vez menos KAH-dah behs MEH-nohs = less and less.
Más y más mahs ee mahs and menos y menos MEH-nohs ee MEH-nohs are interchangeable with cada vez KAH-dah behs in many contexts.


Comparison tips
  • More/less: más mahs / menos MEH-nohs + adj + que keh.
  • As… as: tan tahn + adj + como KOH-moh.
  • As much/many: tanto TAHN-toh/tanta TAHN-tah/tantos TAHN-tohs/tantas TAHN-tahs + noun + como KOH-moh.
  • Irregulars: mejor meh-HOR, peor peh-OR, mayor mah-YOHR, menor meh-NOHR.
  • Superlative of a group: el más ehl mahs / la más lah mahs + adj + de deh.
  • -ísimo EE-see-moh = extremely (not "the most of a group").
  • Number after "more than" → más de mahs deh: más de veinte mahs deh BAYN-teh.
  • As … as possible: lo más loh mahs / lo menos loh MEH-nohs + adj + posible poh-SEE-bleh.
  • Comparing to a clause: más/menos… de lo que mahs/MEH-nohs… deh loh keh.
  • Progressive: cada vez más KAH-dah behs mahs = more and more; cada vez menos KAH-dah behs MEH-nohs = less and less.
  • Emphatic: mucho más MOO-choh mahs = much more; mucho menos MOO-choh MEH-nohs = much less.
  • Approximation: poco más POH-koh mahs = slightly more; poco menos POH-koh MEH-nohs = slightly less.
  • Tan pronto como tahn PROHN-toh KOH-moh = as soon as.
