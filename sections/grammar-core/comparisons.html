Comparisons

More than, less than, as… as, and the most

Inequality — más / menos + adj + que

más mahs + adjective + que keh = more … than
menos MEH-nohs + adjective + que keh = less … than

Pattern Example Pronunciation English
más mahs + adj + que keh Ana es más alta que Luis. AH-nah ehs mahs AHL-tah keh LWEES 🔊 AH-nah ehs mahs AHL-tah kay lwees Ana is taller than Luis.
menos MEH-nohs + adj + que keh Este coche es menos caro que ese. EHS-teh KOH-cheh ehs MEH-nohs KAH-roh keh EH-seh 🔊 EHS-teh KOH-cheh ehs MEH-nohs KAH-roh kay EH-seh This car is less expensive than that one.
With an adverb Corre más rápido que yo. KOH-rreh mahs RAH-pee-doh kay yoh 🔊 KOH-rreh mahs RAH-pee-doh kay yoh He/she runs faster than I do.
With a noun Tengo más libros que tú. TEHN-goh mahs LEE-brohs kay too 🔊 TEHN-goh mahs LEE-brohs kay too I have more books than you.
With a verb Estudio más que antes. ehs-TOO-dyoh mahs kay AHN-tehs 🔊 ehs-TOO-dyoh mahs kay AHN-tehs I study more than before.

After a number, use de deh (not que keh): más de diez mahs deh dyehs 🔊 = more than ten.
Negative + number: no más de noh mahs deh or no más que noh mahs keh can mean “only.”

Equality — tan + adj + como

tan tahn + adjective/adverb + como KOH-moh = as … as

Pattern Example Pronunciation English
tan tahn + adj + como KOH-moh Ana es tan alta como Luis. AH-nah ehs tahn AHL-tah KOH-moh LWEES 🔊 AH-nah ehs tahn AHL-tah KOH-moh lwees Ana is as tall as Luis.
tan tahn + adverb + como KOH-moh Corre tan rápido como tú. KOH-rrheh tahn RAH-pee-doh KOH-moh too 🔊 KOH-rreh tahn RAH-pee-doh KOH-moh too He/she runs as fast as you.
Negative equality No es tan fácil como parece. noh ehs tahn FAH-seel KOH-moh pah-REH-seh 🔊 noh ehs tahn FAH-seel KOH-moh pah-REH-seh (LatAm) / FAH-theel… pah-REH-theh (Spain) It's not as easy as it seems.
Equality with nouns — tanto/a/os/as + noun + como

Tanto TAHN-toh agrees with the noun (not the person).

Form Use with Example English
tanto TAHN-toh masculine singular Tengo tanto dinero como tú. TEHN-goh TAHN-toh dee-NEH-roh KOH-moh too 🔊 I have as much money as you.
tanta TAHN-tah feminine singular Hay tanta agua como ayer. eye TAHN-tah AH-gwah KOH-moh ah-YEHR 🔊 There's as much water as yesterday.
tantos TAHN-tohs masculine plural Tengo tantos libros como ella. TEHN-goh TAHN-tohs LEE-brohs KOH-moh EH-yah 🔊 I have as many books as she does.
tantas TAHN-tahs feminine plural Tiene tantas amigas como yo. TYEH-neh TAHN-tahs ah-MEE-gahs KOH-moh yoh 🔊 She has as many (female) friends as I do.
tanto TAHN-toh (invariable) with a verb Trabaja tanto como yo. trah-BAH-hah TAHN-toh KOH-moh yoh 🔊 He/she works as much as I do.

Pronunciation: TAHN-toh / TAHN-tah / TAHN-tohs / TAHN-tahs.

Irregular comparatives

Don't use más bueno mahs BWEH-noh or más malo mahs MAH-loh for the usual “better / worse.”

Normal adjective Comparative Pronunciation English Example
bueno BWEH-noh mejor meh-HOR meh-HOHR better Este libro es mejor que ese. EHS-teh LEE-broh ehs meh-HOR keh EH-seh 🔊
malo MAH-loh peor peh-OR peh-OHR worse Hoy me siento peor. oy meh SYEHN-toh peh-OR 🔊
grande GRAHN-deh (age / rank) mayor mah-YOHR mah-YOHR older / greater Soy mayor que tú. soy mah-YOHR keh too 🔊
pequeño peh-KEH-nyoh (age) menor meh-NOHR meh-NOHR younger / lesser Mi hermana es menor. mee ehr-MAH-nah ehs meh-NOHR 🔊

Size (not age): prefer más grande mahs GRAHN-deh / más pequeño mahs peh-KEH-nyoh: Esta casa es más grande. EHS-tah KAH-sah ehs mahs GRAHN-deh 🔊
Mayor mah-YOHR / menor meh-NOHR are the usual pair for older / younger.

Superlatives — el/la más, -ísimo

Relative superlative: el ehl / la lah / los lohs / las lahs + más mahs / menos MEH-nohs + adjective (+ de deh…).

Spanish Pronunciation English
Ana es la más alta de la clase. AH-nah ehs lah mahs AHL-tah deh lah KLAH-seh 🔊 AH-nah ehs lah mahs AHL-tah deh lah KLAH-seh Ana is the tallest in the class.
Es el menos caro. ehs ehl MEH-nohs KAH-roh 🔊 ehs ehl MEH-nohs KAH-roh It's the least expensive.
Es el mejor restaurante de la ciudad. ehs ehl meh-HOHR rehs-tow-RAHN-teh deh lah syoo-DAHD 🔊 ehs ehl meh-HOHR rehs-tow-RAHN-teh deh lah syoo-DAHD (LatAm) / thyoo-DAHD (Spain) It's the best restaurant in the city.
mi hermano menor mee ehr-MAH-noh meh-NOHR 🔊 mee ehr-MAH-noh meh-NOHR my younger / youngest brother

Absolute superlative (-ísimo EE-see-moh): very / extremely — not a comparison with someone else.

Adjective -ísimo form Pronunciation English
bueno BWEH-noh buenísimo bweh-NEE-see-moh bweh-NEE-see-moh (LatAm) / bweh-NEE-thee-moh (Spain) extremely good
fácil FAH-seel facilísimo fah-thee-LEE-thee-moh fah-see-LEE-see-moh / fah-thee-LEE-thee-moh extremely easy
rápido RAH-pee-doh rapidísimo rah-pee-DEE-see-moh rah-pee-DEE-see-moh extremely fast
rico REE-koh riquísimo ree-KEE-see-moh rree-KEE-see-moh delicious / extremely rich
largo LAHR-goh larguísimo lahr-HEE-see-moh lahr-GEE-see-moh extremely long

Spelling tweaks: c → qu (rico REE-koh → riquísimo ree-KEE-see-moh), g → gu (largo LAHR-goh → larguísimo lahr-HEE-see-moh), drop a written accent (fácil FAH-seel → facilísimo fah-thee-LEE-thee-moh).

Example: La sopa está riquísima. lah SOH-pah ehs-TAH ree-KEE-see-mah 🔊 — The soup is delicious.

Comparison tips
  • More/less: más mahs / menos MEH-nohs + adj + que keh.
  • As… as: tan tahn + adj + como KOH-moh.
  • As much/many: tanto TAHN-toh/tanta TAHN-tah/tantos TAHN-tohs/tantas TAHN-tahs + noun + como KOH-moh.
  • Irregulars: mejor meh-HOR, peor peh-OR, mayor mah-YOHR, menor meh-NOHR.
  • Superlative of a group: el más ehl mahs / la más lah mahs + adj + de deh.
  • -ísimo EE-see-moh = extremely (not “the most of a group”).
  • Number after “more than” → más de mahs deh: más de veinte mahs deh BAYN-teh. 🔊
