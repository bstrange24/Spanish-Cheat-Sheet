    Back then we lived in Madrid.

    I'll call you right now / in a little while.
  • Ven enseguida. behn ehn-seh-GEE-dah
    Come immediately.
  • De repente se fue. deh reh-PEHN-teh seh fweh
    Suddenly he/she left.
  • Últimamente no duermo bien. OOL-tee-mah-MEHN-teh noh DWEHR-moh BYEHN
    Lately I don't sleep well.

    I recently moved.
  • Actualmente trabajo aquí. ahk-twahl-MEHN-teh trah-BAH-hoh ah-KEE
    I currently work here.
  • Antiguamente había un mercado. ahn-tee-gwah-MEHN-teh ah-BEE-ah oon mehr-KAH-doh
    Formerly there was a market.

    They previously announced it.

    We will talk later / subsequently.
  • Finalmente llegamos. fee-nahl-MEHN-teh yeh-GAH-mohs
    Finally we arrived.
  • De vez en cuando salimos. deh behs ehn KWAHN-doh sah-LEE-mohs
    From time to time we go out.
  • En aquel entonces no había internet. ehn ah-KEHL ehn-TOHN-sehs noh ah-BEE-ah een-tehr-NEHT
    Back then there was no internet.

Tips:
Ya + negative = no longer: Ya no vivo ahí. yah noh BEE-boh ah-EE (I no longer live there.)
Todavía + negative = not yet: Todavía no llego. toh-dah-BEE-ah noh YEH-goh (I haven't arrived yet.)
Anoche ah-NOH-cheh = last night; anteayer ahn-teh-ah-YEHR = the day before yesterday.
Ahorita ah-oh-REE-tah is common in Latin America and can mean "right now" or "in a little while" depending on context.

    It's nowhere.

    I left it somewhere.

    Look for it elsewhere.
  • Está aquí mismo. ehs-TAH ah-KEE MEE-smoh
    It's right here.
  • Déjalo allí mismo. DEH-hah-loh ah-YEE MEE-smoh
    Leave it right there.

Tip: The difference between aquí, acá, ahí, allí, and allá:
Aquí = right here (near me).
Acá = over here (LatAm, often with motion).
Ahí = there (near you).
Allí = there (farther away).
Allá = over there (even farther, vaguer).
Adelante ah-deh-LAHN-teh = forward / ahead; atrás ah-TRAHS = back / behind.
Detrás deh-TRAHS = behind; delante deh-LAHN-teh = in front.
Encima ehn-SEE-mah = on top; debajo deh-BAH-hoh = underneath.

Adverb tips
  • Adverbs do not change for gender or number.
  • muy mwee = very (with adjectives/adverbs). mucho MOO-choh = a lot (with verbs/nouns).
  • bien BYEHN / mal mahl (how) vs bueno BWEH-noh / malo MAH-loh (what kind).
  • ya yah = already; ya no yah noh = no longer; todavía toh-dah-BEE-ah = still; todavía no toh-dah-BEE-ah noh = not yet.
  • Place: aquí ah-KEE (here) → ahí ah-EE (there by you) → allí ah-YEE / allá ah-YAH (over there).
  • -mente MEHN-teh = feminine adjective + mente MEHN-teh. Keep any written accent.
  • Manner adverbs usually follow the verb: habla despacio AH-blah dehs-PAH-syoh.
  • ⚠️ sí see = yes (with accent). si see = if (no accent): Si quieres, vamos. see KYEH-rehs BAH-mohs (If you want, let's go.)
  • ⚠️ Interrogative adverbs take a written accent: cómo KOH-moh, cuándo KWAHN-doh, cuánto KWAHN-toh, dónde DOHN-deh, adónde ah-DOHN-deh, por qué pohr KEH. The same words without the accent are relative adverbs: como (like/as), cuando (when), cuanto (as much as), donde (where), porque (because).
  • ⚠️ Do not confuse solo (only) with sólo — the accent is no longer required by the RAE (Real Academia Española) unless there's ambiguity.
  • También tahm-BYEHN = also; tampoco tahm-POH-koh = neither / not either.
  • Sin duda seen DOO-dah = without a doubt; por supuesto pohr soo-PWEHS-toh = of course.
  • De acuerdo deh ah-KWEHR-doh = agreed; vale BAH-leh = okay (Spain); dale DAH-leh = okay / go ahead (LatAm).
