Adverbs

Words that tell when, how often, where, how much, and how

Time

Time adverbs answer when?

Spanish Pronunciation English
ahora ah-OH-rah now
antes AHN-tehs before / earlier
ayer ah-YEHR yesterday
después dehs-PWEHS after / afterward
hoy oy today
luego LWEH-goh later / then
mañana mah-NYAH-nah tomorrow
tarde TAHR-deh late
temprano tehm-PRAH-noh early
todavía toh-dah-BEE-ah still / yet
ya yah already / now
Example sentences:
  • Lo hago ahora. loh AH-goh ah-OH-rah
    I do it now.
  • Antes vivía aquí. AHN-tehs bee-BEE-ah ah-KEE
    Before, I lived here.
  • Ayer fui al mercado. ah-YEHR fwee ahl mehr-KAH-doh
    Yesterday I went to the market.
  • Comemos después. koh-MEH-mohs dehs-PWEHS
    We eat afterward.
  • Hoy trabajo. oy trah-BAH-hoh
    Today I work.
  • Te llamo luego. teh YAH-moh LWEH-goh
    I'll call you later.
  • Mañana viajamos. mah-NYAH-nah byah-HAH-mohs
    Tomorrow we travel.
  • Llegas tarde. YEH-gahs TAHR-deh
    You arrive late.
  • Llego temprano. YEH-goh tehm-PRAH-noh
    I arrive early.
  • Todavía estoy aquí. toh-dah-BEE-ah ehs-TOY ah-KEE
    I'm still here.
  • Ya comí. yah koh-MEE
    I already ate.

Tips:
Ya + negative = no longer: Ya no vivo ahí. yah noh BEE-boh ah-EE (I no longer live there.)
Todavía + negative = not yet: Todavía no llego. toh-dah-BEE-ah noh YEH-goh (I haven't arrived yet.)


Frequency

Frequency adverbs answer how often?

Spanish Pronunciation English
a menudo ah meh-NOO-doh often
a veces ah BEH-sehs (LatAm) / ah BEH-thehs (Spain) sometimes
casi nunca KAH-see NOON-kah almost never
casi siempre KAH-see SYEHM-preh almost always
generalmente heh-neh-rahl-MEHN-teh generally
nunca NOON-kah never
siempre SYEHM-preh always
todos los días TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs every day
Example sentences:
  • Viajo a menudo. BYAH-hoh ah meh-NOO-doh
    I travel often.
  • A veces camino. ah BEH-sehs kah-MEE-noh
    Sometimes I walk.
  • Casi nunca veo televisión. KAH-see NOON-kah BEH-oh teh-leh-bee-SYOHN
    I almost never watch TV.
  • Casi siempre como en casa. KAH-see SYEHM-preh KOH-moh ehn KAH-sah
    I almost always eat at home.
  • Generalmente llego a las ocho. heh-neh-rahl-MEHN-teh YEH-goh ah lahs OH-choh
    I generally arrive at eight.
  • Nunca fumo. NOON-kah FOO-moh
    I never smoke.
  • Siempre desayuno. SYEHM-preh deh-sah-YOO-noh
    I always have breakfast.
  • Corro todos los días. KOH-rroh TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs
    I run every day.

Tip: Nunca is already negative. If it comes after the verb, add no:
No fumo nunca. noh FOO-moh NOON-kah (I never smoke.)


Place

Place adverbs answer where?

Spanish Pronunciation English
abajo ah-BAH-hoh down / downstairs
acá ah-KAH over here (LatAm, with motion)
ahí ah-EE there (near you)
allá ah-YAH over there
allí ah-YEE there (farther)
aquí ah-KEE here (near me)
arriba ah-RREE-bah up / upstairs
cerca SEHR-kah (LatAm) / THEHR-kah (Spain) nearby
dentro DEHN-troh inside
fuera FWEH-rah outside
lejos LEH-hohs far
Example sentences:
  • Baja abajo. BAH-hah ah-BAH-hoh
    Go down / downstairs.
  • Ven acá. behn ah-KAH
    Come over here.
  • El libro está ahí. ehl LEE-broh ehs-TAH ah-EE
    The book is there (near you).
  • Mira allá. MEE-rah ah-YAH
    Look over there.
  • Viven allí. BEE-behn ah-YEE
    They live there (farther away).
  • Ven aquí. behn ah-KEE
    Come here (near me).
  • El gato está arriba. ehl GAH-toh ehs-TAH ah-RREE-bah
    The cat is upstairs.
  • El banco está cerca. ehl BAHN-koh ehs-TAH SEHR-kah
    The bank is nearby.
  • Está dentro. ehs-TAH DEHN-troh
    It's inside.
  • Espera fuera. ehs-PEH-rah FWEH-rah
    Wait outside.
  • Vivo lejos. BEE-boh LEH-hohs
    I live far away.

Tip: The difference between aquí, acá, ahí, allí, and allá:
Aquí = right here (near me).
Acá = over here (LatAm, often with motion).
Ahí = there (near you).
Allí = there (farther away).
Allá = over there (even farther, vaguer).


Quantity

Quantity adverbs answer how much? They do not change for gender/number.

Spanish Pronunciation English
bastante bahs-TAHN-teh quite / enough
casi KAH-see almost
demasiado deh-mah-SYAH-doh (LatAm) / deh-mah-THYAH-doh (Spain) too much
más mahs more
menos MEH-nohs less / fewer
mucho MOO-choh a lot
muy mwee very
poco POH-koh little / not much
tan tahn so (with adjectives/adverbs)
Example sentences:
  • Es bastante fácil. ehs bahs-TAHN-teh FAH-seel
    It's quite easy.
  • Casi termino. KAH-see tehr-MEE-noh
    I'm almost finished.
  • Hablas demasiado. AH-blahs deh-mah-SYAH-doh
    You talk too much.
  • Quiero más. KYEH-roh mahs
    I want more.
  • Habla menos. AH-blah MEH-nohs
    Talk less.
  • Trabaja mucho. trah-BAH-hah MOO-choh
    He/she works a lot.
  • Muy bien. mwee BYEHN
    Very good.
  • Como poco. KOH-moh POH-koh
    I eat little / not much.
  • Es tan rápido. ehs tahn RAH-pee-doh
    It's so fast.

Tips:
Muy mwee + adjective/adverb: muy rápido mwee RAH-pee-doh (very fast).
Mucho MOO-choh + verb / noun: como mucho KOH-moh MOO-choh (I eat a lot), mucho trabajo MOO-choh trah-BAH-hoh (a lot of work).
⚠️ Do not say muy mucho.


Manner

Manner adverbs answer how?

Spanish Pronunciation English
así ah-SEE like this / that way
bien byehn well
despacio dehs-PAH-syoh (LatAm) / dehs-PAH-thyoh (Spain) slowly
juntos HOON-tohs together
mal mahl badly
mejor meh-HOHR better
peor peh-OHR worse
rápido RAH-pee-doh fast / quickly
Example sentences:
  • Se hace así. seh AH-seh ah-SEE
    You do it like this / that way.
  • Hablo bien. AH-bloh BYEHN
    I speak well.
  • Habla despacio. AH-blah dehs-PAH-syoh
    Speak slowly.
  • Vamos juntos. BAH-mohs HOON-tohs
    We go together.
  • Duermo mal. DWEHR-moh mahl
    I sleep badly.
  • Estoy mejor. ehs-TOY meh-HOHR
    I'm better.
  • Canta peor. KAHN-tah peh-OHR
    He/she sings worse.
  • Corre rápido. KOH-rreh RAH-pee-doh
    He/she runs fast.

Tip: Bueno BWEH-noh / Malo MAH-loh are adjectives (describe nouns).
For "well / badly," use Bien BYEHN / Mal mahl (adverbs):
Canto bien. KAHN-toh BYEHN (I sing well.)
Canto bueno. (incorrect)


-mente formation

Many manner adverbs are formed from the feminine adjective + -mente MEHN-teh (like English -ly).

Adjective Feminine Adverb Pronunciation English
rápido rápida rápidamente RAH-pee-dah-MEHN-teh quickly
lento lenta lentamente lehn-tah-MEHN-teh slowly
claro clara claramente klah-rah-MEHN-teh clearly
fácil (no change) fácilmente FAH-seel-MEHN-teh (LatAm) / FAH-theel-MEHN-teh (Spain) easily
feliz (no change) felizmente feh-lees-MEHN-teh (LatAm) / feh-leeth-MEHN-teh (Spain) happily
normal (no change) normalmente nohr-mahl-MEHN-teh normally
  • If the adjective has a written accent, keep it on the adverb: fácil FAH-seel → fácilmente FAH-seel-mehn-teh, rápido RAH-pee-doh → rápidamente RAH-pee-dah-mehn-teh.
  • Adjectives ending in -e or a consonant usually do not change: inteligente een-teh-lee-HEHN-teh → inteligentemente een-teh-lee-hehn-teh-MEHN-teh.
  • Two -mente MEHN-teh adverbs in a row: drop -mente MEHN-teh on the first: lenta y cuidadosamente LEHN-tah ee kwee-dah-doh-sah-MEHN-teh.

Example: Habla claramente. AH-blah klah-rah-MEHN-teh — She/he speaks clearly.


Conjunctive & relative adverbs

These adverbs connect clauses or refer to previously mentioned information.

Spanish Pronunciation English
además ah-deh-MAHS furthermore / besides
aunque OW-n-keh although / even though
cuando KWAHN-doh when
donde DOHN-deh where
mientras MYEHN-trahs while
por eso pohr EH-soh that's why / therefore
porque POHR-keh because
pues pwehs well / then / since
sin embargo seen ehm-BAHR-goh however / nevertheless
también tahm-BYEHN also / too
tampoco tahm-POH-koh neither / not either
Example sentences:
  • Además, es muy caro. ah-deh-MAHS ehs mwee KAH-roh
    Furthermore, it's very expensive.
  • Aunque llueve, salgo. OW-n-keh YWEH-beh SAHL-goh
    Even though it's raining, I go out.
  • Ven cuando puedas. behn KWAHN-doh PWEH-dahs
    Come when you can.
  • Vivo donde trabajo. BEE-boh DOHN-deh trah-BAH-hoh
    I live where I work.
  • Mientras espero, leo. MYEHN-trahs ehs-PEH-roh LEH-oh
    While I wait, I read.
  • Por eso llegué tarde. pohr EH-soh yeh-GEH TAHR-deh
    That's why I arrived late.
  • No fui porque estaba enfermo. noh fwee POHR-keh ehs-TAH-bah ehn-FEHR-moh
    I didn't go because I was sick.
  • Pues, vamos. pwehs BAH-mohs
    Well, let's go.
  • Sin embargo, no es fácil. seen ehm-BAHR-goh noh ehs FAH-seel
    However, it's not easy.
  • Yo también quiero. yoh tahm-BYEHN KYEH-roh
    I want it too.
  • Ella tampoco vino. EH-yah tahm-POH-koh BEE-noh
    She didn't come either.

Tips:
También tahm-BYEHN = also (affirmative).
Tampoco tahm-POH-koh = neither / not either (negative).
Porque POHR-keh = because (one word). Por qué pohr KEH = why? (question, two words).
Sin embargo seen ehm-BAHR-goh = however (formal); pero PEH-roh = but (informal).


Affirmation & negation

These adverbs express agreement, disagreement, or doubt.

Spanish Pronunciation English
ciertamente syehr-tah-MEHN-teh certainly
claro KLAH-roh of course / clearly
correcto kohr-REHK-toh correctly / right
jamás hah-MAHS never (emphatic)
ni nee neither / nor / not even
no noh no / not
quizás kee-SAHS maybe / perhaps
quizá kee-SAH maybe / perhaps
seguramente seh-goo-rah-MEHN-teh surely
sí see yes
tal vez tahl behs perhaps / maybe
Example sentences:
  • Ciertamente es posible. syehr-tah-MEHN-teh ehs poh-SEE-bleh
    It's certainly possible.
  • Claro que sí. KLAH-roh keh see
    Of course.
  • Respondió correcto. rehs-pohn-DYOH kohr-REHK-toh
    He/She answered correctly.
  • Jamás lo haré. hah-MAHS loh ah-REH
    I will never do it.
  • Ni siquiera sé. nee see-KYEH-rah seh
    I don't even know.
  • No quiero nada. noh KYEH-roh NAH-dah
    I don't want anything.
  • Quizás venga mañana. kee-SAHS BEHN-gah mah-NYAH-nah
    Maybe he/she will come tomorrow.
  • Quizá llueva. kee-SAH YWEH-bah
    Maybe it will rain.
  • Seguramente está aquí. seh-goo-rah-MEHN-teh ehs-TAH ah-KEE
    Surely it's here.
  • Sí, quiero. see KYEH-roh
    Yes, I want to.
  • Tal vez salga. tahl behs SAHL-gah
    Maybe I'll go out.

Tips:
Sí see = yes (with accent). Si see = if (no accent).
No noh = no / not. Double negatives are common: No sé nada. noh seh NAH-dah (I don't know anything).
Quizás kee-SAHS and Quizá kee-SAH are interchangeable. Tal vez tahl behs is more common in Latin America.
Jamás hah-MAHS is stronger than nunca NOON-kah.


Interrogative & exclamatory adverbs

Used to ask questions or express exclamations. Always have a written accent.

Spanish Pronunciation English
cómo KOH-moh how? / what? (exclamatory)
cuándo KWAHN-doh when?
cuánto KWAHN-toh how much? / how many?
dónde DOHN-deh where?
por qué pohr KEH why?
Example sentences:
  • ¿Cómo estás? KOH-moh ehs-TAHS
    How are you?
  • ¡Cómo llueve! KOH-moh YWEH-beh
    How it's raining! / It's raining so much!
  • ¿Cuándo llegas? KWAHN-doh YEH-gahs
    When do you arrive?
  • ¿Cuánto cuesta? KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah
    How much does it cost?
  • ¿Dónde vives? DOHN-deh BEE-behs
    Where do you live?
  • ¿Por qué no vienes? pohr KEH noh BYEH-nehs
    Why don't you come?

Tip: The same words without the accent are relative adverbs: como KOH-moh (like / as), cuando KWAHN-doh (when), cuanto KWAHN-toh (as much as), donde DOHN-deh (where), porque POHR-keh (because).


Focusing adverbs

These adverbs limit, specify, or highlight information in a sentence.

Spanish Pronunciation English
al menos ahl MEH-nohs at least
apenas ah-PEH-nahs barely / hardly
exactamente ehk-sahk-tah-MEHN-teh exactly
incluso een-KLOO-soh even / including
prácticamente PRAHK-tee-kah-MEHN-teh practically
principalmente preen-see-pahl-MEHN-teh mainly / primarily
solo SOH-loh only / just
solamente soh-lah-MEHN-teh only / just
sobre todo SOH-breh TOH-doh especially / above all
Example sentences:
  • Al menos intenta. ahl MEH-nohs een-TEHN-tah
    At least try.
  • Apenas llegué. ah-PEH-nahs yeh-GEH
    I barely arrived.
  • Exactamente eso. ehk-sahk-tah-MEHN-teh EH-soh
    Exactly that.
  • Incluso él sabe. een-KLOO-soh ehl SAH-beh
    Even he knows.
  • Prácticamente terminé. PRAHK-tee-kah-MEHN-teh tehr-mee-NEH
    I practically finished.
  • Principalmente estudio. preen-see-pahl-MEHN-teh ehs-TOO-dyoh
    I mainly study.
  • Solo quiero agua. SOH-loh KYEH-roh AH-gwah
    I only want water.
  • Solamente uno. soh-lah-MEHN-teh OO-noh
    Only one.
  • Sobre todo, no te rindas. SOH-breh TOH-doh noh teh REEN-dahs
    Above all, don't give up.

Tip: Solo SOH-loh (only) and Solamente soh-lah-MEHN-teh (only) are interchangeable.
⚠️ Do not confuse solo (only) with sólo — the accent is no longer required by the RAE (Real Academia Española) unless there's ambiguity.
Incluso een-KLOO-soh often appears before the emphasized element.


Adverb tips
  • Adverbs do not change for gender or number.
  • muy mwee = very (with adjectives/adverbs). mucho MOO-choh = a lot (with verbs/nouns).
  • bien BYEHN / mal mahl (how) vs bueno BWEH-noh / malo MAH-loh (what kind).
  • ya yah = already; ya no yah noh = no longer; todavía toh-dah-BEE-ah = still; todavía no toh-dah-BEE-ah noh = not yet.
  • Place: aquí ah-KEE (here) → ahí ah-EE (there by you) → allí ah-YEE / allá ah-YAH (over there).
  • -mente MEHN-teh = feminine adjective + mente MEHN-teh. Keep any written accent.
  • Manner adverbs usually follow the verb: habla despacio AH-blah dehs-PAH-syoh.
