Adverbs

Words that tell when, how often, where, how much, and how

muy mwee + adjective/adverb: muy rápido mwee RAH-pee-doh. 🔊
mucho MOO-choh + verb / noun: como mucho KOH-moh MOO-choh, mucho trabajo MOO-choh trah-BAH-hoh. 🔊
Do not say muy mucho.

Manner

Manner adverbs answer how?

Spanish Pronunciation English Example
así ah-SEE ah-SEE like this / that way Se hace así. seh AH-seh ah-SEE 🔊
bien BYEHN byehn well Hablo bien. AH-bloh BYEHN 🔊
despacio dehs-PAH-syoh dehs-PAH-syoh (LatAm) / dehs-PAH-thyoh (Spain) slowly Habla despacio. AH-blah dehs-PAH-syoh 🔊
juntos HOON-tohs HOON-tohs together Vamos juntos. BAH-mohs HOON-tohs 🔊
mal mahl mahl badly Duermo mal. DWEHR-moh mahl 🔊
mejor meh-HOR meh-HOHR better Estoy mejor. ehs-TOY meh-HOR 🔊
peor peh-OR peh-OHR worse Canta peor. KAHN-tah peh-OR 🔊
rápido RAH-pee-doh RAH-pee-doh fast / quickly Corre rápido. KOH-rrheh RAH-pee-doh 🔊

bueno BWEH-noh / malo MAH-loh are adjectives. For “well / badly,” use bien BYEHN / mal mahl: canto bien KAHN-toh BYEHN, not canto bueno.

-mente formation

Many manner adverbs are formed from the feminine adjective + -mente MEHN-teh (like English -ly).

Adjective Feminine Adverb Pronunciation English
rápido RAH-pee-doh rápida RAH-pee-dah rápidamente RAH-pee-dah-mehn-teh RAH-pee-dah-MEHN-teh quickly
lento LEHN-toh lenta LEHN-tah lentamente lehn-tah-MEHN-teh lehn-tah-MEHN-teh slowly
claro KLAH-roh clara KLAH-rah claramente klah-rah-MEHN-teh klah-rah-MEHN-teh clearly
fácil FAH-seel (no change) fácilmente FAH-seel-mehn-teh FAH-seel-MEHN-teh (LatAm) / FAH-theel-MEHN-teh (Spain) easily
feliz feh-LEES (no change) felizmente feh-lees-MEHN-teh feh-lees-MEHN-teh (LatAm) / feh-leeth-MEHN-teh (Spain) happily
normal nohr-MAHL (no change) normalmente nohr-mahl-MEHN-teh nohr-mahl-MEHN-teh normally
  • If the adjective has a written accent, keep it on the adverb: fácil FAH-seel → fácilmente FAH-seel-mehn-teh, rápido RAH-pee-doh → rápidamente RAH-pee-dah-mehn-teh.
  • Adjectives ending in -e or a consonant usually do not change: inteligente een-teh-lee-HEHN-teh → inteligentemente een-teh-lee-hehn-teh-MEHN-teh.
  • Two -mente MEHN-teh adverbs in a row: drop -mente MEHN-teh on the first: lenta y cuidadosamente LEHN-tah ee kwee-dah-doh-sah-MEHN-teh. 🔊

Example: Habla claramente. AH-blah klah-rah-MEHN-teh 🔊 — She/he speaks clearly.

Adverb tips
  • Adverbs do not change for gender or number.
  • muy mwee = very (with adjectives/adverbs). mucho MOO-choh = a lot (with verbs/nouns).
  • bien BYEHN / mal mahl (how) vs bueno BWEH-noh / malo MAH-loh (what kind).
  • ya yah = already; ya no yah noh = no longer; todavía toh-dah-BEE-ah = still; todavía no toh-dah-BEE-ah noh = not yet.
  • Place: aquí ah-KEE (here) → ahí ah-EE (there by you) → allí ah-YEE / allá ah-YAH (over there).
  • -mente MEHN-teh = feminine adjective + mente MEHN-teh. Keep any written accent.
  • Manner adverbs usually follow the verb: habla despacio AH-blah dehs-PAH-syoh. 🔊
