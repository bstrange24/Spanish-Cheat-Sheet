Pronunciation

LatAm vs Spain — the main sound differences

Most of Spanish sounds the same in both. Only a few letters change. Learn these and you're covered.

Spelling Latin America Spain (Castilian) Example
c before e / i s as in see th as in thin cine → SEE-neh / THEE-neh
z (anywhere) s as in see th as in thin zapato → sah-PAH-toh / thah-PAH-toh
s s s (same) sol → sohl (both)
ll usually y as in yes also y today llamar → yah-MAHR
j / g before e/i strong h strong h (raspier) gente → HEN-teh
