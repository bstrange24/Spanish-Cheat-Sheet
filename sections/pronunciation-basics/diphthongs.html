Diphthongs (two vowels in one syllable)

A diphthong is two vowels gliding together in one syllable.

Triphthongs (three vowels in one syllable)

A triphthong is three vowels gliding together in one syllable. It is formed by a weak vowel (i or u) + a strong vowel (a, e, o) + a weak vowel.

Spelling Sound guide Example Pronunciation
iai eye-ee (quick glide) estudiáis es-too-DYEYES
iei yay-ee cambiéis kahm-BYEYS
uai / uay why (as in why) Paraguay pah-rah-GWHY
uei / uey way (as in way) buey BWAY
When it is not a diphthong (hiatus)

If you see a written accent on í or ú, the vowels usually split into two syllables:

Word Pronunciation
día DEE-ah
país pah-EES
baúl bah-OOL
tío TEE-oh
