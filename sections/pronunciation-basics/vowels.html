Spanish vowels (the foundation)

Spanish has 5 vowels. They are short, clear, and almost always the same sound.

Letter Spanish sound Say it like… Example Pronunciation
a open "ah" father (short) casa KAH-sah KAH-sah
e "eh" bet / met mesa MEH-sah MEH-sah
i "ee" see / machine sí see SEE
o pure "oh" go (no glide) hola OH-lah OH-lah
u "oo" food / moon luna LOO-nah LOO-nah
