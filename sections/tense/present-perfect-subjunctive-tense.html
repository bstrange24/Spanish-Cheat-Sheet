Present Perfect Subjunctive

The present perfect subjunctive is used for actions that may have happened or that are relevant to the present, after expressions of doubt, emotion, desire, etc.
Formed with haber ah-BEHR (present subjunctive) + past participle.

Regular verbs: full present perfect subjunctive

• hablar ah-BLAHR → hablado ah-BLAH-doh

Person Spanish English
yo yoh haya hablado AH-yah ah-BLAH-doh that I have spoken
tú too hayas hablado AH-yahs ah-BLAH-doh that you have spoken
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD haya hablado AH-yah ah-BLAH-doh that he/she has spoken
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs hayamos hablado ah-YAH-mohs ah-BLAH-doh that we have spoken
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs hayan hablado AH-yahn ah-BLAH-doh that they have spoken

Example sentences:
  • Espero que hayas hablado con ella. ehs-PEH-roh keh AH-yahs ah-BLAH-doh kohn EH-yah
    I hope you have spoken with her.
  • Dudo que hayan comido. DOO-doh keh AH-yahn koh-MEE-doh
    I doubt that they have eaten.
  • Me alegra que hayamos vivido esta experiencia. meh ah-LEH-grah keh ah-YAH-mohs bee-BEE-doh EHS-tah ehk-speh-RYEHN-syah
    I'm glad that we have lived this experience.
