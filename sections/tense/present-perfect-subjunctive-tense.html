Present Perfect Subjunctive

The present perfect subjunctive is used for actions that may have happened or that are relevant to the present, after expressions of doubt, emotion, desire, etc.
Formed with haber (present subjunctive) + past participle.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm.

Quick start – Present Subjunctive of haber
Person Form Pronunciation
yo haya AH-yah
tú hayas AH-yahs
él / ella / usted haya AH-yah
nosotros hayamos ah-YAH-mohs
vosotros (Spain) hayáis ah-YAHS
ellos / ustedes hayan AH-yahn
Regular verbs: full present perfect subjunctive

• hablar → hablado

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo haya hablado AH-yah ah-BLAH-doh that I have spoken
tú hayas hablado AH-yahs ah-BLAH-doh that you have spoken
él / ella / usted haya hablado AH-yah ah-BLAH-doh that he/she has spoken
nosotros hayamos hablado ah-YAH-mohs ah-BLAH-doh that we have spoken
ellos / ustedes hayan hablado AH-yahn ah-BLAH-doh that they have spoken

• comer → comido

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo haya comido AH-yah koh-MEE-doh that I have eaten
tú hayas comido AH-yahs koh-MEE-doh that you have eaten
él / ella / usted haya comido AH-yah koh-MEE-doh that he/she has eaten
nosotros hayamos comido ah-YAH-mohs koh-MEE-doh that we have eaten
ellos / ustedes hayan comido AH-yahn koh-MEE-doh that they have eaten
Examples
Spanish LatAm English
Espero que hayas hablado con ella 🔊 ehs-PEH-roh kay AH-yahs ah-BLAH-doh kohn EH-yah I hope you have spoken with her.
Dudo que hayan comido 🔊 DOO-doh kay AH-yahn koh-MEE-doh I doubt that they have eaten.
Me alegra que hayamos vivido esta experiencia 🔊 meh ah-LEH-grah kay ah-YAH-mohs bee-BEE-doh EHS-tah ehk-speh-RYEHN-syah I’m glad that we have lived this experience.
