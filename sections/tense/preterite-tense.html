Preterite (Simple Past)

The preterite is used for completed actions in the past (what happened, when, or how many times).

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar ah-BLAHR = to speak to speak
Who did it subject yo yoh, tú too, él ehl…
Ending that shows person + past conjugation hablé ah-BLEH = I spoke I spoke

Three verb families (by infinitive ending)

Type Ending Example English
-ar (most common) -ar hablar ah-BLAHR to speak
-er -er comer koh-MEHR to eat
-ir -ir vivir bee-BEER to live

Key differences from Present

  • Stress usually falls on the ending (hablé, comí, vivió).
  • -ar and -er/-ir share almost no endings (unlike the present).
  • Many common verbs are irregular in the preterite (ser/ir, estar, tener, hacer, etc.).

Regular verbs: full preterite + pronunciation

• hablar ah-BLAHR — to speak / to talk → ah-BLAHR

Person Spanish English
yo yoh hablé ah-BLEH I spoke
tú too hablaste ah-BLAHS-teh you spoke (informal)
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD habló ah-BLOH he/she spoke; you spoke (formal)
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs hablamos ah-BLAH-mohs we spoke
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (Spain) hablasteis ah-BLAHS-teys you all spoke
ellos EH-yohs / ellas EH-yahs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs hablaron ah-BLAH-rohn they / you all spoke

More useful regular verbs (preterite stem + meaning)

Drop -ar / -er / -ir, then add the preterite endings above. Pronunciations below are for the infinitive and the yo form.

Infinitive English yo form

estudiar ehs-too-DYAHR to study estudié ehs-too-DYEH

Note: Verbs like pagar pah-GAHR change g → gu before -é (pagué pah-HEH) to keep the hard /g/ sound. leer leh-EHR keeps the two vowels: leí leh-EE, leíste leh-EES-teh, leyó leh-YOH, etc.


Example sentences:
  • Hablé español ayer. ah-BLEH ehs-pah-NYOHL ah-YEHR
    I spoke Spanish yesterday.
  • ¿Hablaste con ella? ah-BLAHS-teh kohn EH-yah
    Did you speak with her?
  • Ella habló con su mamá. EH-yah ah-BLOH kohn soo mah-MAH
    She spoke with her mom.
  • Nosotros comimos pizza. noh-SOH-trohs koh-MEE-mohs PEET-sah
    We ate pizza.
  • ¿Qué comiste? keh koh-MEES-teh
    What did you eat?
  • Viví en Texas dos años. bee-BEE ehn TEHK-sahs dohs AH-nyohs
    I lived in Texas for two years.
  • ¿Dónde viviste? DOHN-deh bee-BEES-teh
    Where did you live?
  • Ellos vivieron aquí. EH-yohs bee-BYEH-rohn ah-KEE
    They lived here.
  • Vosotros hablasteis muy bien. boh-SOH-trohs ah-BLAHS-teys mwee BYEHN
    You all spoke very well. (Spain)
  • Trabajé mucho ayer. trah-bah-HEH MOO-choh ah-YEHR
    I worked a lot yesterday.
  • Estudié español toda la noche. ehs-too-DYEH ehs-pah-NYOHL TOH-dah lah NOH-cheh
    I studied Spanish all night.
  • Escuché música. ehs-koo-CHEH MOO-see-kah
    I listened to music.
  • Necesité ayuda. neh-seh-see-TEH ah-YOO-dah
    I needed help.
  • Compré pan. kohm-PREH pahn
    I bought bread.
  • ¿Me ayudaste? meh ah-yoo-DAHS-teh
    Did you help me?
  • Bebí agua. beh-BEE AH-gwah
    I drank water.
  • Aprendí rápido. ah-prehn-DEE RAH-pee-doh
    I learned quickly.
  • Leí un libro. leh-EE oon LEE-broh
    I read a book.
  • Escribí un mensaje. ehs-kree-BEE oon mehn-SAH-heh
    I wrote a message.
  • Abrí la puerta. ah-BREE lah PWEHR-tah
    I opened the door.
