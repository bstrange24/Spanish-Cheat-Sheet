Future Progressive

The future progressive describes an action that will be happening at a certain time in the future.
Formed with estar (future) + gerund.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm.

Quick start – Future of estar
Person Form Pronunciation
yo estaré ehs-tah-REH
tú estarás ehs-tah-RAHS
él / ella / usted estará ehs-tah-RAH
nosotros estaremos ehs-tah-REH-mohs
vosotros (Spain) estaréis ehs-tah-REHS
ellos / ustedes estarán ehs-tah-RAHN
Regular verbs: full future progressive
