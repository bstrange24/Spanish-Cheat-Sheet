Future Progressive

The future progressive describes an action that will be happening at a certain time in the future.
Formed with estar ehs-TAHR (future) + gerund.

Regular verbs: full future progressive

• hablar ah-BLAHR → hablando ah-BLAHN-doh

Person Spanish English
yo yoh estaré hablando ehs-tah-REH ah-BLAHN-doh I will be speaking
tú too estarás hablando ehs-tah-RAHS ah-BLAHN-doh you will be speaking
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD estará hablando ehs-tah-RAH ah-BLAHN-doh he/she will be speaking
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs estaremos hablando ehs-tah-REH-mohs ah-BLAHN-doh we will be speaking
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs estarán hablando ehs-tah-RAHN ah-BLAHN-doh they will be speaking

Example sentences:
  • Estaré hablando contigo a las cinco. ehs-tah-REH ah-BLAHN-doh kohn-TEE-goh ah lahs SEEN-koh
    I will be talking with you at five.
  • Estaremos comiendo cuando llegues. ehs-tah-REH-mohs koh-MYEHN-doh KWAHN-doh YEH-gehs
    We will be eating when you arrive.
