Future Progressive

The future progressive describes an action that will be happening at a certain time in the future.
Formed with estar ehs-TAHR (future) + gerund.

Quick start – Future of estar ehs-TAHR
Person Form Pronunciation
yo yoh estaré ehs-tah-REH ehs-tah-REH
tú too estarás ehs-tah-RAHS ehs-tah-RAHS
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD estará ehs-tah-RAH ehs-tah-RAH

vosotros boh-SOH-trohs (Spain) estaréis ehs-tah-REHS ehs-tah-REHS
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs estarán ehs-tah-RAHN ehs-tah-RAHN
