Conditional Progressive

The conditional progressive describes what would be happening (hypothetical ongoing action).
Formed with estar (conditional) + gerund.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm.

Quick start – Conditional of estar
Person Form Pronunciation
yo estaría ehs-tah-REE-ah
tú estarías ehs-tah-REE-ahs
él / ella / usted estaría ehs-tah-REE-ah
nosotros estaríamos ehs-tah-REE-ah-mohs
vosotros (Spain) estaríais ehs-tah-REE-ah-ees
ellos / ustedes estarían ehs-tah-REE-ahn
Regular verbs: full conditional progressive

• hablar → hablando

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo estaría hablando ehs-tah-REE-ah ah-BLAHN-doh I would be speaking
tú estarías hablando ehs-tah-REE-ahs ah-BLAHN-doh you would be speaking
él / ella / usted estaría hablando ehs-tah-REE-ah ah-BLAHN-doh he/she would be speaking
nosotros estaríamos hablando ehs-tah-REE-ah-mohs ah-BLAHN-doh we would be speaking
ellos / ustedes estarían hablando ehs-tah-REE-ahn ah-BLAHN-doh they would be speaking
