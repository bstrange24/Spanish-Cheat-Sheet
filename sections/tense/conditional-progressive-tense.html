Conditional Progressive

The conditional progressive describes what would be happening (hypothetical ongoing action).
Formed with estar ehs-TAHR (conditional) + gerund.

Quick start – Conditional of estar ehs-TAHR

Regular verbs: full conditional progressive

• hablar ah-BLAHR → hablando ah-BLAHN-doh

Person Spanish English
yo yoh estaría hablando ehs-tah-REE-ah ah-BLAHN-doh I would be speaking
tú too estarías hablando ehs-tah-REE-ahs ah-BLAHN-doh you would be speaking
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD estaría hablando ehs-tah-REE-ah ah-BLAHN-doh he/she would be speaking
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs estaríamos hablando ehs-tah-REE-ah-mohs ah-BLAHN-doh we would be speaking
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs estarían hablando ehs-tah-REE-ahn ah-BLAHN-doh they would be speaking

Example sentences:
  • Estaría hablando contigo si tuviera tiempo. ehs-tah-REE-ah ah-BLAHN-doh kohn-TEE-goh see too-BYEHR-ah TYEHM-poh

  • Estaríamos comiendo ahora. ehs-tah-REE-ah-mohs koh-MYEHN-doh ah-OH-rah
