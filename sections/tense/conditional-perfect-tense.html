Conditional Perfect

The conditional perfect describes what would have happened under certain conditions.
Formed with haber ah-BEHR (conditional) + past participle.

Quick start – Conditional of haber ah-BEHR

Regular verbs: full conditional perfect

• hablar ah-BLAHR → hablado ah-BLAH-doh

Person Spanish English
yo yoh habría hablado ah-BREE-ah ah-BLAH-doh I would have spoken
tú too habrías hablado ah-BREE-ahs ah-BLAH-doh you would have spoken
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD habría hablado ah-BREE-ah ah-BLAH-doh he/she would have spoken
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs habríamos hablado ah-BREE-ah-mohs ah-BLAH-doh we would have spoken
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs habrían hablado ah-BREE-ahn ah-BLAH-doh they would have spoken

Example sentences:
  • Habría hablado contigo si hubiera tenido tiempo. ah-BREE-ah ah-BLAH-doh kohn-TEE-goh see oo-BYEHR-ah teh-NEE-doh TYEHM-poh
    I would have spoken with you if I had had time.
  • Habríamos comido juntos. ah-BREE-ah-mohs koh-MEE-doh HOON-tohs
    We would have eaten together.
