Conditional Perfect

The conditional perfect describes what would have happened under certain conditions.
Formed with haber ah-BEHR (conditional) + past participle.

Quick start – Conditional of haber ah-BEHR
Person Form Pronunciation
yo yoh habría ah-BREE-ah ah-BREE-ah
tú too habrías ah-BREE-ahs ah-BREE-ahs
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD habría ah-BREE-ah ah-BREE-ah
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs habríamos ah-BREE-ah-mohs ah-BREE-ah-mohs
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs (Spain) habríais ah-BREE-ah-ees ah-BREE-ah-ees
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs habrían ah-BREE-ahn ah-BREE-ahn
