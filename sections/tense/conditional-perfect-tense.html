Conditional Perfect

The conditional perfect describes what would have happened under certain conditions.
Formed with haber (conditional) + past participle.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm.

Quick start – Conditional of haber
Person Form Pronunciation
yo habría ah-BREE-ah
tú habrías ah-BREE-ahs
él / ella / usted habría ah-BREE-ah
nosotros habríamos ah-BREE-ah-mohs
vosotros (Spain) habríais ah-BREE-ah-ees
ellos / ustedes habrían ah-BREE-ahn
Regular verbs: full conditional perfect

• hablar → hablado

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo habría hablado ah-BREE-ah ah-BLAH-doh I would have spoken
tú habrías hablado ah-BREE-ahs ah-BLAH-doh you would have spoken
él / ella / usted habría hablado ah-BREE-ah ah-BLAH-doh he/she would have spoken
nosotros habríamos hablado ah-BREE-ah-mohs ah-BLAH-doh we would have spoken
ellos / ustedes habrían hablado ah-BREE-ahn ah-BLAH-doh they would have spoken
