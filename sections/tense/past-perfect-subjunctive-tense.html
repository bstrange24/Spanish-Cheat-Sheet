Past Perfect Subjunctive

The past perfect subjunctive is used for hypothetical or contrary-to-fact situations in the past, or after past triggers of the subjunctive (“if I had spoken…”).
Formed with haber (imperfect subjunctive) + past participle.
There are two sets of forms (-ra and -se). The -ra forms are more common.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm.

Quick start – Imperfect Subjunctive of haber (-ra forms)
Person Form Pronunciation
yo hubiera oo-BYEHR-ah
tú hubieras oo-BYEHR-ahs
él / ella / usted hubiera oo-BYEHR-ah
nosotros hubiéramos oo-BYEHR-ah-mohs
vosotros (Spain) hubierais oo-BYEHR-ah-ees
ellos / ustedes hubieran oo-BYEHR-ahn

Alternative -se forms: hubiese, hubieses, hubiese, hubiésemos, hubieseis, hubiesen

Regular verbs: full past perfect subjunctive (-ra)

• hablar → hablado

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo hubiera hablado oo-BYEHR-ah ah-BLAH-doh if I had spoken / that I had spoken

él / ella / usted hubiera hablado oo-BYEHR-ah ah-BLAH-doh if he/she had spoken
nosotros hubiéramos hablado oo-BYEHR-ah-mohs ah-BLAH-doh if we had spoken
ellos / ustedes hubieran hablado oo-BYEHR-ahn ah-BLAH-doh if they had spoken

Examples
Spanish LatAm English
Si hubiera hablado antes, todo sería diferente 🔊 see oo-BYEHR-ah ah-BLAH-doh AHN-tehs TOH-doh seh-REE-ah dee-feh-REHN-teh If I had spoken earlier, everything would be different.
Ojalá hubiéramos comido juntos 🔊 oh-hah-LAH oo-BYEHR-ah-mohs koh-MEE-doh HOON-tohs I wish we had eaten together.
No creía que hubieran vivido allí 🔊 noh kreh-EE-ah kay oo-BYEHR-ahn bee-BEE-doh ah-YEE I didn’t believe that they had lived there.
