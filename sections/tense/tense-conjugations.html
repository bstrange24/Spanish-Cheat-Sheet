Tenses & Conjugations

All Spanish verb tenses — formations, endings, and examples

Present & Preterite endings (regular)

Present: actions happening now, habitual actions, general truths
Preterite: completed actions in the past, actions with a definite start or end, events that happened once or during a specific timeframe

Person (Subject Pronoun) Present Preterite Pronunciation pattern
-ar -er -ir -ar -er / -ir
yo yoh (I) -o -o -o -é -í Pres: oh; Pret: -é = EH; -í = EE
tú too (you, informal singular) -as -es -es -aste -iste Pres: -as = ahs; -es = ehs; Pret: -aste = AHS-teh; -iste = EES-teh
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD (he / she / you, formal singular) -a -e -e -ó -ió Pres: -a = ah; -e = eh; Pret: -ó = OH; -ió = YOH
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs (we) -amos -emos -imos -amos -imos Pres: AH-mohs / EH-mohs / EE-mohs; Pret: AH-mohs / EE-mohs
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (you all, Spain) -áis -éis -ís -asteis -isteis Pres: AHEES / EHEES / EES; Pret: AHS-teys / EES-teys
ellos EH-yohs / ellas EH-yahs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs (they / you all formal) -an -en -en -aron -ieron Pres: ahn / ehn; Pret: AH-rohn / YEH-rohn
Examples

hablar ah-BLAHR (to speak), comer koh-MEHR (to eat), vivir bee-BEER (to live)

Present

  • Hablo español. AH-bloh ehs-pah-NYOHL 🔊 — I speak Spanish.
  • Come manzanas. KOH-meh mahn-SAH-nahs 🔊 — He/she eats apples.
  • Vivimos en Madrid. bee-BEE-mohs ehn mah-DREED 🔊 — We live in Madrid.

Preterite

  • Hablé español. ah-BLEH ehs-pah-NYOHL 🔊 — I spoke Spanish.
  • Comió manzanas. koh-MYOH mahn-SAH-nahs 🔊 — He/she ate apples.
  • Vivimos en Madrid. bee-BEE-mohs ehn mah-DREED 🔊 — We lived in Madrid.

📌 Note: For nosotros noh-SOH-trohs, the present and preterite forms are identical for -ar and -ir verbs:

  • Hablamos = We speak OR We spoke (context tells you which!)
  • Vivimos = We live OR We lived (context tells you which!)
  • For -er verbs, they are different: Comemos (present) vs. Comimos (preterite)

Imperfect endings (regular)

Used for: ongoing or habitual past actions, descriptions in the past, telling time, weather, and background information ("used to," "was/were -ing")

Person (Subject Pronoun) -ar -er / -ir Pronunciation pattern
yo yoh (I) -aba -ía -aba = AH-bah; -ía = EE-ah
tú too (you, informal singular) -abas -ías -abas = AH-bahs; -ías = EE-ahs
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD -aba -ía same as yo
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs -ábamos -íamos AH-bah-mohs / EE-ah-mohs
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (Spain) -abais -íais AH-bah-ees / EE-ah-ees
ellos EH-yohs / ellas EH-yahs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs -aban -ían AH-bahn / EE-ahn
Examples

hablar ah-BLAHR (to speak), comer koh-MEHR (to eat), vivir bee-BEER (to live)

  • Hablaba español. ah-BLAH-bah ehs-pah-NYOHL 🔊 — I used to speak Spanish / I was speaking Spanish.
  • Comían manzanas. koh-MEE-ahn mahn-SAH-nahs 🔊 — They used to eat apples / They were eating apples.
  • Vivíamos en Madrid. bee-BEE-ah-mohs ehn mah-DREED 🔊 — We used to live in Madrid / We were living in Madrid.
Present Subjunctive endings (regular)

Used for: wishes, doubts, emotions, and hypothetical situations

Present Subjunctive

Formation: start with yo yoh form in present, drop the -o, add the opposite endings

Person (Subject Pronoun) -ar -er / -ir Pronunciation pattern
yo yoh (I) -e -a -e = eh; -a = ah
tú too (you, informal singular) -es -as -es = ehs; -as = ahs
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD -e -a same as yo
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs -emos -amos EH-mohs / AH-mohs
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (Spain) -éis -áis EHS / AHS
ellos EH-yohs / ellas EH-yahs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs -en -an ehn / ahn
Examples

hablar ah-BLAHR (to speak), comer koh-MEHR (to eat), vivir bee-BEER (to live)

  • Espero que hable español. ehs-PEH-roh keh AH-bleh ehs-pah-NYOHL 🔊 — I hope (that) he/she speaks Spanish.
  • Dudo que comas eso. DOO-doh keh KOH-mahs EH-soh 🔊 — I doubt (that) you eat that.
  • Quiere que vivamos aquí. KYEH-reh keh bee-BAH-mohs ah-KEE 🔊 — He/she wants us to live here.
Conditional endings (same for -ar, -er, -ir)

Used for: "would" actions, polite requests, hypothetical situations, and future-in-the-past

Formation: add these endings directly to the infinitive (no stem change for regular verbs)

Person Ending Pronunciation pattern
yo yoh -ía EE-ah
tú too -ías EE-ahs
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD -ía EE-ah
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs -íamos EE-ah-mohs
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (Spain) -íais EE-ah-ees
ellos EH-yohs / ellas EH-yahs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs -ían EE-ahn
Examples

hablar ah-BLAHR (to speak), comer koh-MEHR (to eat), vivir bee-BEER (to live)

  • Hablaría español. ah-blah-REE-ah ehs-pah-NYOHL 🔊 — I would speak Spanish.
  • Comería manzanas. koh-meh-REE-ah mahn-SAH-nahs 🔊 — He/she would eat apples.
  • Viviríamos en Madrid. bee-bee-REE-ah-mohs ehn mah-DREED 🔊 — We would live in Madrid.
Future endings (same for -ar, -er, -ir)

Used for: actions that will happen, predictions, promises, and spontaneous decisions

Formation: add these endings directly to the infinitive (no stem change for regular verbs)

Person Ending Pronunciation pattern
yo yoh -é EH
tú too -ás AHS
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD -á AH
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs -emos EH-mohs
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (Spain) -éis EHS
ellos EH-yohs / ellas EH-yahs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs -án AHN
Examples

hablar ah-BLAHR (to speak), comer koh-MEHR (to eat), vivir bee-BEER (to live)

  • Hablaré español. ah-blah-REH ehs-pah-NYOHL 🔊 — I will speak Spanish.
  • Comerás manzanas. koh-meh-RAHS mahn-SAH-nahs 🔊 — You will eat apples.
  • Viviremos en Madrid. bee-bee-REH-mohs ehn mah-DREED 🔊 — We will live in Madrid.
Affirmative Imperative (commands)

Used for: giving direct orders, instructions, advice, or requests to do something

Note: Affirmative tú commands use the él/ella/usted present tense form (3rd person singular).

Person -ar -er -ir
tú too (informal singular) -a -e -e
usted oos-TEHD (formal singular) -e -a -a
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs -emos -amos -amos
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (Spain) -ad -ed -id
ustedes oos-TEH-dehs (formal / LatAm plural) -en -an -an
Examples

hablar ah-BLAHR (to speak), comer koh-MEHR (to eat), vivir bee-BEER (to live)

  • Habla español. AH-blah ehs-pah-NYOHL 🔊 — Speak Spanish! (tú, informal)
  • Coma manzanas. KOH-mah mahn-SAH-nahs 🔊 — Eat apples! (usted, formal)
  • Vivamos en Madrid. bee-BAH-mohs ehn mah-DREED 🔊 — Let's live in Madrid! (nosotros)
Negative Imperative (commands)

Used for: telling someone not to do something — giving prohibitions, warnings, or negative advice

Formation: start with yo yoh form in present, drop the -o, add the opposite endings (same as present subjunctive)

Person -ar -er / -ir
tú too -es -as
usted oos-TEHD -e -a
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs -emos -amos
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs (Spain) -éis -áis
ustedes oos-TEH-dehs -en -an
Examples

hablar ah-BLAHR (to speak), comer koh-MEHR (to eat), vivir bee-BEER (to live)

  • No hables español. noh AH-blehs ehs-pah-NYOHL 🔊 — Don't speak Spanish! (tú, informal)
  • No coma manzanas. noh KOH-mah mahn-SAH-nahs 🔊 — Don't eat apples! (usted, formal)
  • No vivamos en Madrid. noh bee-BAH-mohs ehn mah-DREED 🔊 — Let's not live in Madrid! (nosotros)

Perfect Tenses

Formation: haber ah-BEHR + past participle (-ado AH-doh for -ar, -ido EE-doh for -er/-ir)

Past participle formation: hablar ah-BLAHR → hablado ah-BLAH-doh, comer koh-MEHR → comido koh-MEE-doh, vivir bee-BEER → vivido bee-BEE-doh
