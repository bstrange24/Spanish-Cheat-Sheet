Negative Imperative

Negative commands tell someone not to do something.
All forms use the present subjunctive.

Key points

  • Always start with no.
  • All persons use the present subjunctive forms.
  • Object and reflexive pronouns go before the verb (unlike affirmative).
Regular verbs: full negative imperative

• hablar ah-BLAHR — to speak → ah-BLAHR

Person Spanish English
tú too no hables noh AH-blehs Don’t speak! (informal)
usted oos-TEHD no hable noh AH-bleh Don’t speak! (formal)
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs no hablemos noh ah-BLEH-mohs Let’s not speak!
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs (Spain) no habléis noh ah-BLEHS Don’t speak! (you all)
ustedes oos-TEH-dehs no hablen noh AH-blehn Don’t speak! (you all)

Example sentences:
  • ¡No hables tan alto! noh AH-blehs tahn AHL-toh
    Don't speak so loud! (informal)
  • ¡No coma eso! noh KOH-mah EH-soh
    Don't eat that! (formal)
  • ¡No comamos todavía! noh koh-MAH-mohs toh-dah-BEE-ah
    Let's not eat yet!
  • ¡No vivan aquí! noh BEE-bahn ah-KEE
    Don't live here! (you all)
