Present Progressive

Present Progressive (Present Continuous)

The present progressive describes an action that is happening right now.
Formed with estar (present) + gerund (-ando / -iendo).
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm; Spain only if different.

Quick start
Idea Spanish Example Pronunciation English
Helping verb estar (present) estoy, estás, está… ehs-TOY, ehs-TAHS, ehs-TAH I am, you are, he/she is…
Gerund (-ar) -ando hablando ah-BLAHN-doh speaking
Gerund (-er / -ir) -iendo comiendo / viviendo koh-MYEHN-doh / bee-BYEHN-doh eating / living

Regular verbs: full present progressive

• hablar → hablando

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo estoy hablando ehs-TOY ah-BLAHN-doh I am speaking
tú estás hablando ehs-TAHS ah-BLAHN-doh you are speaking
él / ella / usted está hablando ehs-TAH ah-BLAHN-doh he/she is speaking; you are speaking
nosotros estamos hablando ehs-TAH-mohs ah-BLAHN-doh we are speaking
vosotros (Spain) estáis hablando ehs-TAHS ah-BLAHN-doh you all are speaking
ellos / ustedes están hablando ehs-TAHN ah-BLAHN-doh they / you all are speaking
