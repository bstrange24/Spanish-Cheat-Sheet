Present Progressive

The present progressive describes an action that is happening right now.
Formed with estar ehs-TAHR (present) + gerund (-ando / -iendo).

Quick start
Idea Spanish Example English
Helping verb estar ehs-TAHR (present) estoy ehs-TOY, estás ehs-TAHS, está ehs-TAH… I am, you are, he/she is…
Gerund (-ar) -ando hablando ah-BLAHN-doh speaking
Gerund (-er / -ir) -iendo comiendo koh-MYEHN-doh / viviendo bee-BYEHN-doh eating / living

Regular verbs: full present progressive

• hablar ah-BLAHR → hablando ah-BLAHN-doh

Person Spanish English
yo yoh estoy hablando ehs-TOY ah-BLAHN-doh I am speaking
tú too estás hablando ehs-TAHS ah-BLAHN-doh you are speaking
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD está hablando ehs-TAH ah-BLAHN-doh he/she is speaking; you are speaking
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs estamos hablando ehs-TAH-mohs ah-BLAHN-doh we are speaking
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs (Spain) estáis hablando ehs-TAHS ah-BLAHN-doh you all are speaking
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs están hablando ehs-TAHN ah-BLAHN-doh they / you all are speaking

Example sentences:
  • Estoy hablando por teléfono. ehs-TOY ah-BLAHN-doh por teh-LEH-foh-noh
    I am talking on the phone.
  • ¿Qué estás comiendo? keh ehs-TAHS koh-MYEHN-doh
    What are you eating?
  • Estamos viviendo en Texas. ehs-TAH-mohs bee-BYEHN-doh ehn TEHK-sahs
    We are living in Texas.
