Present Progressive

The present progressive describes an action that is happening right now.
Formed with estar ehs-TAHR (present) + gerund (-ando / -iendo).

Quick start
Idea Spanish Example Pronunciation English
Helping verb estar ehs-TAHR (present) estoy ehs-TOY, estás ehs-TAHS, está ehs-TAH… ehs-TOY, ehs-TAHS, ehs-TAH I am, you are, he/she is…
Gerund (-ar) -ando hablando ah-BLAHN-doh ah-BLAHN-doh speaking
Gerund (-er / -ir) -iendo comiendo koh-MYEHN-doh / viviendo bee-BYEHN-doh koh-MYEHN-doh / bee-BYEHN-doh eating / living

• hablar ah-BLAHR → hablando ah-BLAHN-doh

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
