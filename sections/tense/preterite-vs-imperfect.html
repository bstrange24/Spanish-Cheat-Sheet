Preterite vs Imperfect

Two past tenses — what happened vs what was going on

Quick contrast

Preterite = completed action, a snapshot, a sequence, a specific time.
Imperfect = ongoing, habitual, background, or how things used to be.

Preterite Imperfect
Idea The action finished The action was in progress / used to happen
English feel I spoke / I went / I ate I was speaking / I used to go / I would eat (habit)
Time picture Dot on a timeline A stretch of time, no end given
Example Ayer fui al mercado. ah-YEHR fwee ahl mehr-KAH-doh Cuando era niño, iba al mercado. KWAHN-doh EH-rah NEE-nyoh EE-bah ahl mehr-KAH-doh
Quick decision guide
Ask yourself… Use Example
Is it a completed action with a beginning and end? preterite Comí pizza. koh-MEE PEET-sah
Is there a specific time (yesterday, last year, at 3:00)? preterite Ayer llamé a Ana. ah-YEHR yah-MEH ah AH-nah
Is it a sequence of events (then… then…)? preterite Llegué, comí y me fui. yeh-HEH koh-MEE ee meh fwee
Did it happen a counted number of times? preterite Fui tres veces. fwee trehs BEH-sehs
Was it habitual (“used to / would always”)? imperfect Iba todos los días. EE-bah TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs
Was it ongoing background (“was -ing”) when something else happened? imperfect Leía cuando sonó el teléfono. leh-EE-ah KWAHN-doh soh-NOH ehl teh-LEH-foh-noh
Time, age, or weather in the past? imperfect Eran las tres. EH-rahn lahs trehs / Tenía diez años. teh-NEE-ah dyehs AH-nyohs / Hacía frío. ah-SEE-ah FREE-oh
Describing a person, scene, or how things were? imperfect La casa era grande y había un jardín. lah KAH-sah EH-rah GRAHN-deh ee ah-BEE-ah oon hahr-DEEN

Classic combo: imperfect sets the scene; preterite interrupts it.
Caminaba por el parque cuando vi a María. kah-mee-NAH-bah por ehl PAHR-keh KWAHN-doh bee ah mah-REE-ah — I was walking through the park when I saw María.

Tuvo una casa en Madrid. TOO-boh OO-nah KAH-sah ehn mah-DREED He/she had (got / owned for a bounded time) a house in Madrid. Tenía una casa en Madrid. teh-NEE-ah OO-nah KAH-sah ehn mah-DREED He/she had a house in Madrid. (description)
Fue a las tres. fweh ah lahs trehs He/she went at three. / It took place at three. (event) Eran las tres. EH-rahn lahs trehs It was three o'clock. (clock time)

Some verbs shift meaning: preterite often = the moment of change; imperfect = the state.

Imperfect is almost regular. Only three irregulars: ir eer (iba EE-bah), ser sehr (era EH-rah), ver behr (veía beh-EE-ah). Preterite has many irregular stems.


Mini practice

Choose preterite or imperfect (infinitive given).

  1. Ayer yo (ir) al cine. (yesterday, one completed trip)
  2. Cuando era niño, (jugar) en el parque. (habitual childhood)
  3. (Ser) las tres cuando (llegar) María. (clock time + completed arrival)
  4. Siempre (comer) con mi familia. (always / habit)
  5. De repente, (empezar) a llover. (sudden start)
  6. El verano pasado (viajar) a México. (specific time)
  7. La casa (ser) blanca y (tener) un jardín. (description)
  8. Yo (leer) cuando (sonar) el teléfono. (ongoing + interruption)
  9. (Tener) diez años en 2010. (age in the past)
  10. Anoche (ver) una película y luego (dormir). (sequence)
Answers
  1. Ayer yo fui al cine. ah-YEHR yoh fwee ahl SEE-neh Preterite (completed, ayer)
  2. Cuando era niño, jugaba en el parque. KWAHN-doh EH-rah NEE-nyoh hoo-GAH-bah ehn ehl PAHR-kay Imperfect (habitual)
  3. Eran las tres cuando llegó María. EH-rahn lahs trehs KWAHN-doh yeh-GOH mah-REE-ah Imperfect (time) + Preterite (event)
  4. Siempre comía con mi familia. SYEHM-preh koh-MEE-ah kohn mee fah-MEE-lyah Imperfect (siempre)
  5. De repente, empezó a llover. deh reh-PEHN-teh ehm-peh-SOH ah yoh-BEHR Preterite (sudden start)
  6. El verano pasado viajé a México. ehl beh-RAH-noh pah-SAH-doh byah-HEH ah MEH-hee-koh Preterite (specific time)
  7. La casa era blanca y tenía un jardín. lah KAH-sah EH-rah BLAHN-kah ee teh-NEE-ah oon hahr-DEEN Imperfect (description)
  8. Yo leía cuando sonó el teléfono. yoh leh-EE-ah KWAHN-doh soh-NOH ehl teh-LEH-foh-noh Imperfect (background) + Preterite (interruption)
  9. Tenía diez años en 2010. teh-NEE-ah dyehs AH-nyohs ehn dos mil diez Imperfect (age)
  10. Anoche vi una película y luego dormí. ah-NOH-cheh bee OO-nah peh-LEE-koo-lah ee LWEH-goh dohr-MEE Preterite (sequence)
Preterite vs imperfect tips
  • Finished / dated / sequenced → preterite: Ayer fui. ah-YEHR fwee
  • Used to / was -ing / scene-setting → imperfect: Iba todos los días. EE-bah TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs
  • Past clock time, age, weather as background → imperfect: Eran las dos. EH-rahn lahs dohs / Tenía ocho años. teh-NEE-ah OH-choh AH-nyohs / Hacía frío. ah-SEE-ah FREE-oh
  • Story recipe: imperfect paints the background; preterite moves the plot.
  • conocer koh-noh-SEHR / saber sah-BEHR / poder poh-DEHR / querer keh-REHR often change flavor: preterite = the moment of meeting / finding out / succeeding.
  • Cue words help, but meaning wins: siempre SYEHM-preh can still meet a one-time preterite if you mean “that time I always…” — rare. Default: habit = imperfect.
