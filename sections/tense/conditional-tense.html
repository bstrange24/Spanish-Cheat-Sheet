Conditional

The conditional expresses what would happen, polite requests, or hypothetical situations.

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar ah-BLAHR to speak
Ending added to infinitive conjugation hablaría ah-blah-REE-ah = I would speak I would speak

Key points

  • Formed by adding the endings directly to the infinitive.
  • Same endings for all three verb families.
  • Used for “would”, polite requests (“Could you…?”), and probability in the past.
Regular verbs: full conditional + pronunciation

Example sentences:
  • Hablaría español contigo. ah-blah-REE-ah ehs-pah-NYOHL kohn-TEE-goh
    I would speak Spanish with you.
  • ¿Comerías pizza? koh-meh-REE-ahs PEET-sah
    Would you eat pizza?
  • Viviría en España. bee-bee-REE-ah ehn ehs-PAH-nyah
    I would live in Spain.
  • Nosotros hablaríamos más. noh-SOH-trohs ah-blah-REE-ah-mohs mahs
    We would talk more.
