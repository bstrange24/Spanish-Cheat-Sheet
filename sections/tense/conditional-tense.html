Conditional

The conditional expresses what would happen, polite requests, or hypothetical situations.

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example Pronunciation English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar ah-BLAHR ah-BLAHR to speak
Ending added to infinitive conjugation hablaría ah-blah-REE-ah = I would speak ah-blah-REE-ah I would speak

Conditional endings (same for -ar, -er, -ir)

Person Ending Pronunciation pattern
yo yoh -ía EE-ah
tú too -ías EE-ahs
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD -ía EE-ah
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs -íamos EE-ah-mohs
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (Spain) -íais EE-ah-ees
ellos EH-yohs / ellas EH-yahs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs -ían EE-ahn

Key points

  • Formed by adding the endings directly to the infinitive.
  • Same endings for all three verb families.
  • Used for “would”, polite requests (“Could you…?”), and probability in the past.
