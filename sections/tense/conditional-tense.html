Conditional

Conditional Tense

The conditional expresses what would happen, polite requests, or hypothetical situations.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm; Spain only if different.

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example Pronunciation English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar ah-BLAHR to speak
Ending added to infinitive conjugation hablaría = I would speak ah-blah-REE-ah I would speak

Conditional endings (same for -ar, -er, -ir)

Person Ending Pronunciation pattern
yo -ía EE-ah
tú -ías EE-ahs
él / ella / usted -ía EE-ah
nosotros / nosotras -íamos EE-ah-mohs
vosotros / vosotras (Spain) -íais EE-ah-ees
ellos / ellas / ustedes -ían EE-ahn

Key points

  • Formed by adding the endings directly to the infinitive.
  • Same endings for all three verb families.
  • Used for “would”, polite requests (“Could you…?”), and probability in the past.
Regular verbs: full conditional + pronunciation

• hablar — to speak → ah-BLAHR

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo hablaría ah-blah-REE-ah I would speak
tú hablarías ah-blah-REE-ahs you would speak

vosotros / vosotras (Spain) hablaríais ah-blah-REE-ah-ees you all would speak
ellos / ellas / ustedes hablarían ah-blah-REE-ahn they / you all would speak
