Past Perfect (Pluperfect)

The past perfect (pluperfect) describes an action that happened before another past action (“I had spoken”).
Formed with haber ah-BEHR (imperfect) + past participle.

Regular verbs: full past perfect

• hablar ah-BLAHR → hablado ah-BLAH-doh

Person Spanish English
yo yoh había hablado ah-BEE-ah ah-BLAH-doh I had spoken
tú too habías hablado ah-BEE-ahs ah-BLAH-doh you had spoken
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD había hablado ah-BEE-ah ah-BLAH-doh he/she had spoken
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs habíamos hablado ah-BEE-ah-mohs ah-BLAH-doh we had spoken
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs habían hablado ah-BEE-ahn ah-BLAH-doh they had spoken

Example sentences:
  • Ya había hablado con ella. yah ah-BEE-ah ah-BLAH-doh kohn EH-yah
    I had already spoken with her.
  • Cuando llegué, ya habían comido. KWAHN-doh yeh-GEH yah ah-BEE-ahn koh-MEE-doh
    When I arrived, they had already eaten.
