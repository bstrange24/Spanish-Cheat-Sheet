Past Perfect (Pluperfect)

Past Perfect

The past perfect (pluperfect) describes an action that happened before another past action (“I had spoken”).
Formed with haber ah-BEHR (imperfect) + past participle.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm.
