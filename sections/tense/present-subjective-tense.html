Present Subjunctive

The present subjunctive is used for wishes, doubts, emotions, recommendations, and after certain conjunctions (when the action is not a statement of fact).

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar ah-BLAHR = to speak to speak
Who / what is involved subject yo yoh, tú too, él ehl…
Ending that shows subjunctive conjugation hable AH-bleh = that I speak / I speak (subj.) that I speak

Three verb families (by infinitive ending)

Type Ending Example English
-ar -ar hablar ah-BLAHR to speak
-er -er comer koh-MEHR to eat
-ir -ir vivir bee-BEER to live

Key points

  • The vowel is the opposite of the present indicative (-ar verbs take -e, -er/-ir take -a).
  • Used after expressions of desire, emotion, doubt, impersonal phrases, and many conjunctions (que, para que, antes de que, etc.).
  • Many common verbs have stem changes or are irregular (tener → tenga, hacer → haga, ir → vaya, ser → sea, etc.).

More useful regular verbs (present subjunctive)

Drop -ar / -er / -ir, then add the present subjunctive endings. Pronunciations are for the yo form.

Infinitive English yo form
trabajar trah-bah-HAHR to work trabaje trah-BAH-heh
estudiar ehs-too-DYAHR to study estudie ehs-TOO-dyeh
escuchar ehs-koo-CHAHR to listen escuche ehs-KOO-cheh

comprar kohm-PRAHR to buy compre KOHM-preh

Example sentences:
  • Quiero que hables español. KYEH-roh keh AH-blehs ehs-pah-NYOHL
    I want you to speak Spanish.
  • Espero que comas bien. ehs-PEH-roh keh KOH-mahs byehn
    I hope you eat well.
  • Es importante que vivamos juntos. ehs eem-por-TAHN-teh keh bee-BAH-mohs HOON-tohs
    It's important that we live together.
  • Dudo que hablen la verdad. DOO-doh keh AH-blehn lah behr-DAHD
    I doubt that they are telling the truth.
  • Ojalá que coman pronto. oh-hah-LAH keh KOH-mahn PROHN-toh
    I hope they eat soon.
