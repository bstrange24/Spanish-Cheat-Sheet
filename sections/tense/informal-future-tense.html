Informal Future

The informal future (also called the “going to” future) is the most common way to talk about the future in everyday spoken Spanish.
It is formed with ir (present) + a + infinitive.
Used for plans, intentions, and near-future actions.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm; Spain only if different.

Quick start
Idea Spanish Example Pronunciation English
Helping verb ir (present) voy, vas, va… boy, bahs, bah I go, you go, he/she goes…
Preposition a — ah to
Main verb infinitive hablar / comer / vivir ah-BLAHR / koh-MEHR / bee-BEER to speak / to eat / to live

Regular verbs: full informal future

• hablar — to speak / to talk

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo voy a hablar boy ah ah-BLAHR I am going to speak
tú vas a hablar bahs ah ah-BLAHR you are going to speak
él / ella / usted va a hablar bah ah ah-BLAHR he/she is going to speak; you are going to speak
nosotros / nosotras vamos a hablar BAH-mohs ah ah-BLAHR we are going to speak
vosotros / vosotras (Spain) vais a hablar bah-ees ah ah-BLAHR you all are going to speak
ellos / ellas / ustedes van a hablar bahn ah ah-BLAHR they / you all are going to speak

Voy a hablar con ella mañana 🔊 boy ah ah-BLAHR kohn EH-yah mah-NYAH-nah same I’m going to talk to her tomorrow.
