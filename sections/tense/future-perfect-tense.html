Future Perfect

The future perfect describes an action that will have happened by a certain point in the future.
Formed with haber ah-BEHR (future) + past participle.

Quick start – Future of haber ah-BEHR

Regular verbs: full future perfect

• hablar ah-BLAHR → hablado ah-BLAH-doh

Person Spanish English
yo yoh habré hablado ah-BREH ah-BLAH-doh I will have spoken
tú too habrás hablado ah-BRAHS ah-BLAH-doh you will have spoken
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD habrá hablado ah-BRAH ah-BLAH-doh he/she will have spoken
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs habremos hablado ah-BREH-mohs ah-BLAH-doh we will have spoken
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs habrán hablado ah-BRAHN ah-BLAH-doh they will have spoken

• comer koh-MEHR → comido koh-MEE-doh

Person Spanish English

tú too habrás comido ah-BRAHS koh-MEE-doh you will have eaten
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD habrá comido ah-BRAH koh-MEE-doh he/she will have eaten
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs habremos comido ah-BREH-mohs koh-MEE-doh we will have eaten

Example sentences:
  • Para mañana ya habré hablado con ella. PAH-rah mah-NYAH-nah yah ah-BREH ah-BLAH-doh kohn EH-yah
    By tomorrow I will have already spoken with her.
  • Habremos comido antes de las tres. ah-BREH-mohs koh-MEE-doh AHN-tehs deh lahs trehs
    We will have eaten before three.
