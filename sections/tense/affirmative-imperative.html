Affirmative Imperative

Affirmative commands tell someone to do something.
There is no “yo” form. Forms exist for tú, usted, nosotros, vosotros (Spain), and ustedes.

Key points

  • tú affirmative commands use the third-person singular present indicative form: habla, come, vive.
  • usted, nosotros/as, and ustedes use the present subjunctive forms.
  • vosotros/as affirmative commands are formed by dropping the final -r from the infinitive and adding -d: hablad, comed, vivid.
  • There is no regular yo affirmative command because you normally do not give a command to yourself.
Regular verbs: full affirmative imperative

• hablar ah-BLAHR — to speak → ah-BLAHR

Person Spanish English
tú too habla AH-blah Speak! (informal)
usted oos-TEHD hable AH-bleh Speak! (formal)
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs hablemos ah-BLEH-mohs Let’s speak!
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs (Spain) hablad ah-BLAHD Speak! (you all)
ustedes oos-TEH-dehs hablen AH-blehn Speak! (you all)

Example sentences:
  • ¡Habla más alto! AH-blah mahs AHL-toh
    Speak louder! (informal)
  • ¡Hable despacio, por favor! AH-bleh dehs-PAH-syoh por fah-BOHR
    Speak slowly, please! (formal)
  • ¡Comamos juntos! koh-MAH-mohs HOON-tohs
    Let's eat together!
  • ¡Vivan felices! BEE-bahn feh-LEE-sehs
    Live happily! (you all)
