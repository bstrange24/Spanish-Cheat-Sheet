Affirmative Imperative

Affirmative Commands

Affirmative commands tell someone to do something.
There is no “yo” form. Forms exist for tú, usted, nosotros, vosotros (Spain), and ustedes.

Quick start – Affirmative Imperative endings
Person -ar -er -ir
tú too (informal singular) -a -e -e
usted oos-TEHD (formal singular) -e -a -a
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs -emos -amos -amos
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (Spain) -ad -ed -id
ustedes oos-TEH-dehs (formal / LatAm plural) -en -an -an

Key points

  • tú form looks like the él/ella present (but without the final -s for -ar/-er/-ir).
  • usted / ustedes / nosotros use the present subjunctive forms.
  • vosotros affirmative is unique: drop -r and add -d.
Regular verbs: full affirmative imperative

• hablar ah-BLAHR — to speak → ah-BLAHR

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
tú too habla AH-blah AH-blah Speak! (informal)
usted oos-TEHD hable AH-bleh AH-bleh Speak! (formal)
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs hablemos ah-BLEH-mohs ah-BLEH-mohs Let’s speak!
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs (Spain) hablad ah-BLAHD ah-BLAHD Speak! (you all)
ustedes oos-TEH-dehs hablen AH-blehn AH-blehn Speak! (you all)
