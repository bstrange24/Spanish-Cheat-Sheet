Preterite Progressive

The preterite progressive describes an action that was in progress at a specific moment in the past and is now finished.
Formed with estar ehs-TAHR (preterite) + gerund.

Quick start – Preterite of estar ehs-TAHR

Regular verbs: full preterite progressive

• hablar ah-BLAHR → hablando ah-BLAHN-doh

Person Spanish English
yo yoh estuve hablando ehs-TOO-beh ah-BLAHN-doh I was speaking (at that moment)
tú too estuviste hablando ehs-too-BEES-teh ah-BLAHN-doh you were speaking
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD estuvo hablando ehs-TOO-boh ah-BLAHN-doh he/she was speaking
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs estuvimos hablando ehs-too-BEE-mohs ah-BLAHN-doh we were speaking
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs estuvieron hablando ehs-too-BYEHR-ohn ah-BLAHN-doh they were speaking

Example sentences:
  • Estuve hablando con ella toda la tarde. ehs-TOO-beh ah-BLAHN-doh kohn EH-yah TOH-dah lah TAHR-deh
    I was talking with her all afternoon.
  • ¿Qué estuviste comiendo? keh ehs-too-BEES-teh koh-MYEHN-doh
    What were you eating?
