Preterite Progressive

The preterite progressive describes an action that was in progress at a specific moment in the past and is now finished.
Formed with estar (preterite) + gerund.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm.

Quick start – Preterite of estar
Person Form Pronunciation
yo estuve ehs-TOO-beh
tú estuviste ehs-too-BEES-teh
él / ella / usted estuvo ehs-TOO-boh
nosotros estuvimos ehs-too-BEE-mohs
vosotros (Spain) estuvisteis ehs-too-BEES-teys
ellos / ustedes estuvieron ehs-too-BYEHR-ohn
Regular verbs: full preterite progressive

• hablar → hablando

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo estuve hablando ehs-TOO-beh ah-BLAHN-doh I was speaking (at that moment)
tú estuviste hablando ehs-too-BEES-teh ah-BLAHN-doh you were speaking
él / ella / usted estuvo hablando ehs-TOO-boh ah-BLAHN-doh he/she was speaking
nosotros estuvimos hablando ehs-too-BEE-mohs ah-BLAHN-doh we were speaking
ellos / ustedes estuvieron hablando ehs-too-BYEHR-ohn ah-BLAHN-doh they were speaking
