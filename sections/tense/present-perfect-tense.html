Present Perfect

The present perfect describes actions that happened in the recent past or that still affect the present (“I have spoken”).
Formed with haber ah-BEHR (present) + past participle.

Quick start
Idea Spanish Example English
Helping verb haber ah-BEHR (present) he eh, has ahs, ha ah… I have, you have, he/she has…
Past participle (-ar) -ado hablado ah-BLAH-doh spoken
Past participle (-er / -ir) -ido comido koh-MEE-doh / vivido bee-BEE-doh eaten / lived

Regular verbs: full present perfect

• hablar ah-BLAHR → hablado ah-BLAH-doh

Person Spanish English
yo yoh he hablado eh ah-BLAH-doh I have spoken
tú too has hablado ahs ah-BLAH-doh you have spoken
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD ha hablado ah ah-BLAH-doh he/she has spoken; you have spoken
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs hemos hablado EH-mohs ah-BLAH-doh we have spoken
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs (Spain) habéis hablado ah-BEHS ah-BLAH-doh you all have spoken
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs han hablado ahn ah-BLAH-doh they / you all have spoken

Example sentences:
  • He hablado con ella. eh ah-BLAH-doh kohn EH-yah
    I have spoken with her.
  • ¿Has comido ya? ahs koh-MEE-doh yah
    Have you eaten already?
  • Hemos vivido aquí cinco años. EH-mohs bee-BEE-doh ah-KEE SEEN-koh AH-nyohs
    We have lived here for five years.
