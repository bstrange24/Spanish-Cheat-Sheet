Present Perfect

Present Perfect

The present perfect describes actions that happened in the recent past or that still affect the present (“I have spoken”).
Formed with haber (present) + past participle.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm; Spain only if different.

Quick start
Idea Spanish Example Pronunciation English
Helping verb haber (present) he, has, ha… eh, ahs, ah I have, you have, he/she has…
Past participle (-ar) -ado hablado ah-BLAH-doh spoken

Regular verbs: full present perfect

• hablar → hablado

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo he hablado eh ah-BLAH-doh I have spoken
tú has hablado ahs ah-BLAH-doh you have spoken
él / ella / usted ha hablado ah ah-BLAH-doh he/she has spoken; you have spoken
nosotros hemos hablado EH-mohs ah-BLAH-doh we have spoken
vosotros (Spain) habéis hablado ah-BEHS ah-BLAH-doh you all have spoken
ellos / ustedes han hablado ahn ah-BLAH-doh they / you all have spoken
