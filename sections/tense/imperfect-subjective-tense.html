Imperfect Subjunctive

The imperfect subjunctive is used for past wishes, doubts, hypothetical situations, and polite requests, or after past-tense triggers of the subjunctive.
There are two interchangeable sets of endings (-ra and -se). The -ra forms are more common in modern Spanish.

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar ah-BLAHR to speak
Base for conjugation 3rd-person plural preterite hablaron ah-BLAH-rohn → drop -ron
Ending that shows imperfect subjunctive conjugation hablara ah-BLAH-rah / hablase ah-BLAH-seh that I spoke / I would speak
Imperfect Subjunctive endings

How to form it

Take the ellos/ellas form of the preterite, drop the -ron, and add the endings below.

Example sentences:
  • Si hablara español, viajaría más. see ah-BLAH-rah ehs-pah-NYOHL byah-hah-REE-ah mahs
    If I spoke Spanish, I would travel more.
  • Quería que comieras conmigo. keh-REE-ah keh koh-MYEH-rahs kohn-MEE-goh
    I wanted you to eat with me.
  • Ojalá vivieran cerca. oh-hah-LAH bee-BYEH-rahn SEHR-kah
    I wish they lived nearby.
  • Si tuviéramos tiempo, hablaríamos más. see too-BYEHR-ah-mohs TYEHM-poh ah-blah-REE-ah-mohs mahs
    If we had time, we would talk more.
  • No creía que comieran tanto. noh kreh-EE-ah keh koh-MYEH-rahn TAHN-toh
    I didn't believe they ate so much.
