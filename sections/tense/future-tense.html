Future

The future expresses what will happen or what someone will do.

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example Pronunciation English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar ah-BLAHR ah-BLAHR to speak
Ending added to infinitive conjugation hablaré ah-blah-REH = I will speak ah-blah-REH I will speak

Future endings (same for -ar, -er, -ir)

Person Ending Pronunciation pattern
yo yoh -é EH
tú too -ás AHS
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD -á AH
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs -emos EH-mohs
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (Spain) -éis EHS
ellos EH-yohs / ellas EH-yahs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs -án AHN

Key points

  • Formed by adding the endings directly to the infinitive.
  • Same endings for all three verb families.
  • Also used for probability in the present (“I wonder…”, “must be…”).
