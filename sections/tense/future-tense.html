Future

The future expresses what will happen or what someone will do.

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar ah-BLAHR to speak
Ending added to infinitive conjugation hablaré ah-blah-REH = I will speak I will speak

Key points

  • Formed by adding the endings directly to the infinitive.
  • Same endings for all three verb families.
  • Also used for probability in the present (“I wonder…”, “must be…”).
Regular verbs: full future + pronunciation

• hablar ah-BLAHR — to speak → ah-BLAHR

Person Spanish English
yo yoh hablaré ah-blah-REH I will speak
tú too hablarás ah-blah-RAHS you will speak

nosotros noh-SOH-trohs / nosotras noh-SOH-trahs hablaremos ah-blah-REH-mohs we will speak
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs / vosotras boh-SOH-trahs (Spain) hablaréis ah-blah-REHS you all will speak
ellos EH-yohs / ellas EH-yahs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs hablarán ah-blah-RAHN they / you all will speak

Example sentences:
  • Hablaré español mañana. ah-blah-REH ehs-pah-NYOHL mah-NYAH-nah
    I will speak Spanish tomorrow.
  • ¿Comerás pizza? koh-meh-RAHS PEET-sah
    Will you eat pizza?
  • Viviré en España. bee-bee-REH ehn ehs-PAH-nyah
    I will live in Spain.
  • Nosotros hablaremos más. noh-SOH-trohs ah-blah-REH-mohs mahs
    We will talk more.
