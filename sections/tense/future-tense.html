Future

Future Tense

The future expresses what will happen or what someone will do.
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm; Spain only if different.

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example Pronunciation English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar ah-BLAHR to speak
Ending added to infinitive conjugation hablaré = I will speak ah-blah-REH I will speak

Future endings (same for -ar, -er, -ir)

Person Ending Pronunciation pattern
yo -é EH
tú -ás AHS
él / ella / usted -á AH
nosotros / nosotras -emos EH-mohs
vosotros / vosotras (Spain) -éis EHS
ellos / ellas / ustedes -án AHN

Key points

  • Formed by adding the endings directly to the infinitive.
  • Same endings for all three verb families.
  • Also used for probability in the present (“I wonder…”, “must be…”).
Regular verbs: full future + pronunciation

• hablar — to speak → ah-BLAHR

Person Spanish Pronunciation English
yo hablaré ah-blah-REH I will speak
tú hablarás ah-blah-RAHS you will speak
él / ella / usted hablará ah-blah-RAH he/she will speak; you will speak
nosotros / nosotras hablaremos ah-blah-REH-mohs we will speak
vosotros / vosotras (Spain) hablaréis ah-blah-REHS you all will speak
ellos / ellas / ustedes hablarán ah-blah-RAHN they / you all will speak
