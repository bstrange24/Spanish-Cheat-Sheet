Imperfect

The imperfect describes ongoing, habitual, or background actions in the past (what used to happen, was happening, or how things were).

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar ah-BLAHR to speak
Who was doing it subject yo yoh, tú too, él ehl… yoh, too, ehl
Ending that shows person + past ongoing conjugation hablaba ah-BLAH-bah I was speaking / used to speak

Three verb families (by infinitive ending)

Type Ending Example English
-ar -ar hablar ah-BLAHR to speak
-er -er comer koh-MEHR to eat
-ir -ir vivir bee-BEER to live

Key points

  • Very regular — only three verbs are irregular: ir eer, ser sehr, ver behr.
  • Used for descriptions, habits, age, time, and ongoing past actions.

Regular verbs: full imperfect + pronunciation

More useful regular verbs (imperfect)

Drop -ar / -er / -ir, then add the imperfect endings. Pronunciations are for the yo form.

Infinitive English yo form
trabajar trah-bah-HAHR to work trabajaba trah-bah-HAH-bah
estudiar ehs-too-DYAHR to study estudiaba ehs-too-DYAH-bah
escuchar ehs-koo-CHAHR to listen escuchaba ehs-koo-CHAH-bah
necesitar neh-seh-see-TAHR to need necesitaba neh-seh-see-TAH-bah
comprar kohm-PRAHR to buy compraba kohm-PRAH-bah
beber beh-BEHR to drink bebía beh-BEE-ah
aprender ah-prehn-DEHR to learn aprendía ah-prehn-DEE-ah
escribir ehs-kree-BEER to write escribía ehs-kree-BEE-ah
abrir ah-BREER to open abría ah-BREE-ah
Example sentences:
  • Hablaba español todos los días. ah-BLAH-bah ehs-pah-NYOHL TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs
    I used to speak Spanish every day.
  • ¿Hablabas con ella? ah-BLAH-bahs kohn EH-yah
    Were you talking with her?
  • Ella hablaba con su mamá. EH-yah ah-BLAH-bah kohn soo mah-MAH
    She was talking with her mom.
  • Nosotros comíamos pizza. noh-SOH-trohs koh-MEE-ah-mohs PEET-sah
    We used to eat / were eating pizza.
  • ¿Qué comías? keh koh-MEE-ahs
    What were you eating?
  • Vivía en Texas. bee-BEE-ah ehn TEHK-sahs
    I used to live / was living in Texas.
  • ¿Dónde vivías? DOHN-deh bee-BEE-ahs
    Where did you used to live?
  • Ellos vivían aquí. EH-yohs bee-BEE-ahn ah-KEE
    They used to live here.
