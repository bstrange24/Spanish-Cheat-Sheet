Imperfect

Imperfect Tense

The imperfect describes ongoing, habitual, or background actions in the past (what used to happen, was happening, or how things were).
Every form includes pronunciation. Default = LatAm; Spain only if different.

Quick start
Idea Spanish term Example Pronunciation English
Dictionary form infinitive hablar = to speak ah-BLAHR to speak
Who was doing it subject yo, tú, él… yoh, too, ehl
Ending that shows person + past ongoing conjugation hablaba = I was speaking / I used to speak ah-BLAH-bah I was speaking / used to speak

Imperfect endings (regular)

Person (Subject Pronoun) -ar -er / -ir Pronunciation pattern
yo (I) -aba -ía -aba = AH-bah; -ía = EE-ah
tú (you, informal singular) -abas -ías -abas = AH-bahs; -ías = EE-ahs
él / ella / usted -aba -ía same as yo
nosotros / nosotras -ábamos -íamos AH-bah-mohs / EE-ah-mohs
vosotros / vosotras (Spain) -abais -íais AH-bah-ees / EE-ah-ees
ellos / ellas / ustedes -aban -ían AH-bahn / EE-ahn

Key points

  • Very regular — only three verbs are irregular: ir, ser, ver.
  • Used for descriptions, habits, age, time, and ongoing past actions.
