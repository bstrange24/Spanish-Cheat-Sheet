Imperfect Progressive

The imperfect progressive describes an ongoing or background action in the past (“was/were …ing”).
Formed with estar ehs-TAHR (imperfect) + gerund.

Quick start – Imperfect of estar ehs-TAHR
Person Form Pronunciation
yo yoh estaba ehs-TAH-bah ehs-TAH-bah
tú too estabas ehs-TAH-bahs ehs-TAH-bahs
él ehl / ella EH-yah / usted oos-TEHD estaba ehs-TAH-bah ehs-TAH-bah
nosotros noh-SOH-trohs estábamos ehs-TAH-bah-mohs ehs-TAH-bah-mohs
vosotros boh-SOH-trohs (Spain) estabais ehs-TAH-bah-ees ehs-TAH-bah-ees
ellos EH-yohs / ustedes oos-TEH-dehs estaban ehs-TAH-bahn ehs-TAH-bahn
